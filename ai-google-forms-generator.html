Two Types of AI Google Forms Tools

When people search for an "AI Google Forms generator," they typically want one of two things. Understanding the difference helps you choose the right tool.

Type 1: Prompt-based AI form builders - You describe a form in plain language ("create a customer satisfaction survey") and the AI generates a form from scratch. Tools like Weavely, Jotform AI, and Google's own Duet AI in Forms take this approach. Good for generating new forms when you have no existing document.

Type 2: Document-to-Google-Forms AI converters - You have an existing survey document and want it converted to a Google Form without manual re-entry. Pirai AI is designed specifically for this. The AI reads the document structure, identifies question types and scales, and generates the Google Form automatically via Apps Script.

If you have an existing Word, PDF, or Excel survey document, Type 2 is what you need. The AI preserves your exact question wording, answer options, and structure - rather than generating new content from a prompt.
