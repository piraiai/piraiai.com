The Shift

From builder menus to plain English

Configuring a Qualtrics survey is a craft: questions one by one, logic condition by condition, flow element by flow element. Nexus changes what that work looks like.

BEFORE

Open the question panel. Pick a type. Add choices one by one. Switch to display logic. Set the condition source, the operator, the target. Repeat for every rule. Configure the flow separately. One missed setting and you're back in the menus.

WITH NEXUS

Describe what the survey needs to measure and who it's for. Nexus designs the question sequence, picks the right types, writes the choices, applies the logic, and configures the flow — all from one conversation. Need to change something? Just say so.

Create Questions

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is Pirai AI Nexus?

Pirai AI Nexus is a conversational AI Chrome extension for Qualtrics survey building. Describe your survey objective in plain English and Nexus creates the questions, logic, flow, and properties — inside Qualtrics, instantly.

Can Nexus build a complete survey, not just individual questions?

Yes. Describe your objective — who the survey is for, what it needs to measure — and Nexus designs the entire questionnaire. It picks question types, writes real choice text, applies display logic between questions, sets up the survey flow with screeners and embedded data, and creates everything in one step.

What question types does Nexus support?

Nexus supports all 25 Qualtrics question types including Multiple Choice (single, multiple, horizontal, dropdown), NPS, Matrix/Likert, Text Entry, Rank Order, Constant Sum, Slider, Side by Side, MaxDiff, Bipolar, Pick Group Rank, File Upload, Timing, and Description blocks.

Can Nexus edit questions and logic that already exist in my survey?

Yes. Nexus works on existing surveys too. Add display logic to a question, set a question as required, randomize choices, anchor specific options, add an 'Other' text field, or set a choice as exclusive — all through natural language, without opening Qualtrics' own menus.

Is my survey data safe when using Pirai AI Nexus?

Nexus reads your survey's question text and choice labels to build logic correctly, but never your response data. Nothing you work with is used to train AI models. All configuration happens inside your existing Qualtrics session. Learn more →

How does Nexus know which choices to reference in display logic?

When you describe a condition — for example, "show Q5 if Q3 is Dissatisfied" — Nexus reads the actual choices on Q3 and matches "Dissatisfied" to the correct choice key. It validates against your real survey data, so logic is always applied to the right option, not a guess.
