The Problem Every Teacher Knows

You have a unit review quiz in Word. Forty multiple choice questions. You want to run it as a Kahoot game tomorrow. The options are: spend an hour copying questions one by one into Kahoot's editor, staying within the 95-character question limit and 60-character answer limit for every item. Or use Pirai AI and have a Kahoot-ready spreadsheet in 2 minutes.

Kahoot's native editor requires each question to be typed in manually - there is no paste-from-Word option, no PDF reader, and no batch import from a document. The only bulk import route is Kahoot's spreadsheet import, which requires the questions to be in a specific .xlsx template. Pirai AI produces that template automatically from your document.

Kahoot Character Limits - Handled Automatically

Kahoot's most common import error is character limit violations. Questions are limited to 95 characters and answer options to 60 characters. A question like "Which of the following best describes the primary cause of the First World War according to the July Crisis theory?" is 106 characters and will fail on import.

Pirai AI checks every question and answer against Kahoot's limits during conversion. Items that exceed the limits are flagged in the AI Survey Editor before you download the spreadsheet - so you can shorten them in the editor rather than discovering the error after a failed Kahoot import.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I convert a scanned quiz paper to Kahoot?+

Yes. Upload the scanned PDF to Pirai AI with Advanced PDF Processing enabled for OCR. The text is extracted from the scan and formatted into the Kahoot import spreadsheet. Review the AI Survey Editor output for any OCR errors before downloading.

What happens to open-ended questions in my quiz when converting to Kahoot?+

Kahoot only supports multiple choice questions. Open-ended, short answer, and essay questions from your document are excluded from the Kahoot output and flagged in the AI Survey Editor. If your quiz has a mix of question types, the multiple choice questions convert and the rest are noted for your review.

How many questions can I import into Kahoot at once?+

Kahoot's spreadsheet import supports up to 100 questions per quiz. For longer question banks, Pirai AI can split the output into multiple spreadsheets. The 1MB file size limit per import is also respected in the output.
