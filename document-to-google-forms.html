Why the Apps Script Approach

Most document-to-Google-Forms tools require granting read/write access to your entire Google Drive. Pirai AI uses a different approach: instead of creating the form inside your Drive directly, it generates a Google Apps Script - a text file you paste and run yourself inside your own Google Form. The form is created by your Google account, not by any third party. No OAuth permissions. No Drive access grant. No extension to install.

This matters for healthcare providers, researchers, and corporate users whose IT policies prohibit granting Drive access to external applications. Learn more in the secured converter guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert a Word document to Google Forms?+

Upload your Word file at platform.piraiai.com and select Google Forms as the target. Download the Google Apps Script file. Open a blank Google Form, go to the Script Editor (three-dot menu), paste the script, and click Run. Your form is built automatically from the Word document.

Can I convert a PDF to Google Forms?+

Yes. Upload your PDF at platform.piraiai.com and select Google Forms. For text-based PDFs, conversion is immediate. For scanned PDFs, enable Advanced PDF Processing to apply OCR first. Download the Apps Script and run it in a blank Google Form.

Does converting to Google Forms require Drive access?+

No. Pirai AI generates a Google Apps Script file you download and run yourself. The form is created by your own Google account inside your own Drive. No third party ever accesses your Drive or holds your OAuth credentials.

Can I also convert the same document to Qualtrics or REDCap?+

Yes. From the same document upload, select Google Forms for one export and Qualtrics or REDCap for another. Both outputs come from the same structured extraction. This is particularly useful for deploying the same survey across multiple platforms simultaneously.
