Academic Survey Context

Universities use Google Forms across three main contexts: informal student feedback and event surveys (Google Forms is ideal), standardised course evaluations and student experience surveys (Google Forms works with coordination), and academic research instruments (usually better served by Qualtrics or REDCap via institutional licences). Pirai AI converts academic survey documents to the right platform for each context.

Three Academic Survey Contexts

Informal student feedback and departmental surveys. Exit tickets, session feedback, event RSVPs, peer evaluation forms, and quick informal surveys. Google Forms is the right tool - free, fast, no institutional setup required, and responses go directly to Google Sheets for course coordinators already working in Google Workspace.

Course evaluations and student experience surveys. When universities standardise course evaluation forms across faculties or run the National Student Survey (UK) or NSSE-aligned instruments (US), consistency of question wording across sections and years matters. Converting the approved course evaluation template from a Word document to Google Forms ensures every section delivers the identical instrument rather than each lecturer building their own version manually.

Academic research instruments. PhD students, faculty researchers, and research centres running studies under IRB or ethics committee approval need platforms with stronger data governance than Google Forms typically provides. Most institutional Qualtrics licences cover researchers at no additional cost. REDCap is standard in health sciences. Pirai AI converts the research instrument to the appropriate platform in minutes.

Converting Academic Survey Documents

The typical academic survey workflow begins with a Word document: the course evaluation template, the student experience questionnaire, the validated research instrument from a published study. That document goes through departmental review and ethics approval before anyone builds the actual survey in a platform.

Pirai AI compresses the time between "approved document" and "live survey" from hours to minutes. Frequently Asked Questions

Is Google Forms good enough for a PhD research survey?+

It depends on the research design. For simple surveys with basic question types and no need for row randomisation or composite score calculation, Google Forms is workable. For studies using validated instruments, requiring random item presentation, or needing institutional data sovereignty, Qualtrics or REDCap is more appropriate. Most universities provide free Qualtrics access to PhD students via institutional licences.

How do I convert a course evaluation template to Google Forms?+

Upload the Word or PDF template to Pirai AI, select Google Forms, download the Apps Script, and run it in a blank Google Form. The same template can be imported by multiple faculty members without each one manually recreating every question.

Can I use Google Forms for FERPA-compliant student surveys?+

Google Forms data is processed by Google under your institution's Google Workspace for Education agreement. Whether this satisfies FERPA for specific survey types depends on your institution's DPA with Google and the nature of the survey data. For surveys linking responses to student records, consult your institution's privacy officer.

Can Pirai AI convert a survey to both Qualtrics and REDCap for a multi-site study?+

Yes. Upload the survey document once and export to Qualtrics QSF for social science sites and REDCap data dictionary for clinical sites in the same session. This ensures all sites use identical question wording - critical for pooling data across multi-site studies.
