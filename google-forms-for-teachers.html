Three Ways Teachers Use Google Forms

Self-grading quizzes. Google Forms' Quiz mode turns any form into a self-grading assessment. Set correct answers for Multiple Choice, Checkboxes, and Dropdown questions. Assign point values. Students see their score immediately after submission. Results feed to Google Sheets for grade tracking. For teachers moving from paper quizzes to digital, this is the most time-saving feature.

Course evaluations and feedback surveys. End-of-unit feedback, lesson exit tickets, and course satisfaction surveys are a natural fit for Google Forms. Students can respond anonymously (by not collecting email addresses) and teachers get immediate summary charts. The form can be pre-filled with the same question set across multiple classes.

Formative assessment and check-ins. Quick mid-lesson polls, comprehension checks, and homework submission forms are fast to build in Google Forms and distribute via Google Classroom link or QR code.

Converting Existing Quiz Documents

Most teachers have quiz and test banks built in Word over years - sometimes decades - of teaching. Moving these to Google Forms manually means re-typing every question and entering each answer option one by one. For a 30-question quiz, that is an hour of work. For a 50-question final exam, it is most of a planning period.

Question Types That Work for Teaching

Multiple Choice - best for knowledge-check questions with one correct answer. Can be set as self-grading in Quiz mode.
Checkboxes - for "select all that apply" questions. Self-grading requires all correct options to be selected for full marks.
Short Answer and Paragraph - for written responses. Cannot be auto-graded; teacher must mark manually in the Responses sheet.
Multiple Choice Grid - for matching exercises or rating multiple items on the same scale. Useful for comprehension surveys.
Linear Scale - for student self-assessment, confidence ratings, or feedback surveys.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I convert a Word quiz to Google Forms automatically?+

Yes. Upload your Word quiz document to platform.piraiai.com, select Google Forms as the target, and download the Google Apps Script. Open a blank Google Form, paste the script into the Script Editor (three-dot menu), and click Run. All questions and answer options from your Word document are added to the form automatically.

How do I make a self-grading quiz in Google Forms?+

First build your quiz using Pirai AI to import the questions. Then in Google Forms, go to Settings > Quizzes and turn on 'Make this a quiz.' Return to each Multiple Choice question and click 'Answer key' to mark the correct answer and assign points. Students see their score immediately after submission.

Can Google Forms quiz questions be randomised?+

Yes. In Google Forms Settings > Presentation, enable 'Shuffle question order' to randomise the order questions appear for each respondent. For shuffling answer options within each question, enable 'Shuffle row order' on individual questions. Note that shuffling applies at the question level - you cannot shuffle within a grid.

Can I convert a scanned paper quiz to Google Forms?+

Yes. Upload the scanned PDF to Pirai AI with Advanced PDF Processing enabled for OCR. The text is extracted from the scan and converted to Google Forms. Review the AI Survey Editor output to catch any OCR errors before downloading the Apps Script.
