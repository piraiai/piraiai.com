Microsoft Forms for Teachers

Microsoft Forms is included in every M365 Education licence - free for schools using Microsoft 365. For teachers already using Word and Teams, it's the natural quiz and survey tool: quizzes auto-grade, responses appear in real time, and results export to Excel for gradebook use. Pirai AI converts existing Word and PDF quiz documents to Microsoft Forms automatically - no re-typing.

Why Teachers Choose Microsoft Forms

Free with M365 Education. Every school or university on Microsoft 365 Education has access to Microsoft Forms at no additional cost. For schools that have standardised on M365, Forms is available without new procurement or parent consent for third-party apps.

Self-grading quizzes. Set correct answers and point values for Choice questions. Students receive their score immediately after submission. Teachers see a class-level results summary in real time. Export to Excel for gradebook entry.

Teams integration. Distribute quizzes and surveys as a tab in a Teams class or meeting. Students complete them without leaving Teams. Assignment submission, polls during lessons, and post-lesson feedback all work natively in Teams with Microsoft Forms.

Assignments integration. In Teams Education, Forms quizzes can be assigned via the Assignments tab. Student responses are automatically linked to the assignment for tracking completion and distributing scores to the Teams gradebook.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Microsoft Forms free for teachers?+

Yes. Microsoft Forms is included in every M365 Education licence, which most schools and universities have. Teachers and students access it at no additional cost through office.com or the Microsoft 365 Education portal. Contact your IT department if you are unsure whether your school is on M365 Education.

How do I convert a Word quiz to Microsoft Forms?+

Upload your Word quiz document to Pirai AI at platform.piraiai.com, select Microsoft Forms as the target, and download the output. In Microsoft Forms, create a new form, click the three-dot menu, select 'Import questions from Word,' and upload the file. Your questions appear in the form editor automatically.

Can Microsoft Forms quizzes auto-grade?+

Yes. Turn on Quiz mode in Form Settings. For each Choice question, click 'Answer key' to mark the correct answer and assign points. Students see their score immediately after submitting. Teachers see class-level results and can export to Excel for gradebook entry.

Does Microsoft Forms work with Teams for Education?+

Yes. Microsoft Forms integrates natively with Teams for Education. Add a Form as a tab in a Teams channel, use it in the Assignments tab for gradebook-tracked submissions, or use Polls during a Teams meeting. Students complete forms without leaving Teams.
