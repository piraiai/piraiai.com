How It Works

REDCap imports surveys through a data dictionary CSV - a structured spreadsheet that defines every field in a project. Pirai AI reads your Word, PDF, or Excel survey document and produces a correctly structured data dictionary CSV for direct import into a new or existing REDCap project. Field types, labels, answer choices, branching logic, and validation rules are all mapped from your source document.

What REDCap's Import Requires

REDCap does not import Word documents or PDFs directly. Survey instruments must be entered through REDCap's Online Designer (one field at a time) or uploaded as a data dictionary CSV through Project Setup > Data Dictionary > Upload File. The data dictionary format has 18+ columns defining every aspect of each field. Building this manually for a 40-item validated instrument takes hours and introduces transcription errors.

Pirai AI generates the data dictionary CSV automatically from your source document - preserving exact item wording required for validated instrument fidelity.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I import a survey document into REDCap?+

REDCap imports surveys via a data dictionary CSV through Project Setup > Data Dictionary > Upload File. Upload your Word, PDF, or Excel document to Pirai AI, select REDCap as the target, and download the pre-formatted data dictionary CSV. Upload it into your REDCap project and click Commit Changes.

What is the REDCap data dictionary format?+

The REDCap data dictionary is a CSV file with specific columns defining every survey field: Variable/Field Name, Form Name, Field Type, Field Label, Choices (for radio/checkbox/dropdown), Branching Logic, Required Field, and Text Validation Type among others. Pirai AI populates all critical columns from your source survey document automatically.

Can Pirai AI convert a validated clinical instrument to REDCap?+

Yes. Upload the instrument document (Word or PDF) to Pirai AI, select REDCap, and download the data dictionary CSV. The exact item wording and scale labels from the validated instrument are preserved verbatim - critical for maintaining the published psychometric properties when deploying in REDCap.

Can I convert from REDCap to Qualtrics with Pirai AI?+

Yes. Export your REDCap instruments as a data dictionary CSV. Upload to Pirai AI and select Qualtrics as the target. The REDCap field types are mapped to the closest Qualtrics question types and a QSF file is produced for import.
