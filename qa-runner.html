The Problem

Clicking through a survey link by hand takes hours, and still misses things.

Every route needs its own pass. Required settings and Other boxes are easy to miss. And when a client asks what was tested, the only answer is "we clicked through it".

WITHOUT QA RUNNER

Someone answers every page by hand, once for each route through the survey, and starts again after every change. Required questions that aren't enforced slip through. Questions that never appear go unnoticed. There's no record of which answers were tried.

WITH QA RUNNER

QA Runner answers every page for you and runs each route as its own scenario, in a fresh session. Required settings are checked as it goes. Every answer it gives is logged, and the same seed reproduces any run exactly after a fix.

How It Works

From survey link to signed off routes in four steps.

A quick run checks the whole survey. A test plan proves the routes that matter.

1

Open your survey link

Install QA Runner from the Chrome Web Store, open a Qualtrics preview or live link, and click the extension icon. The side panel recognises the survey and the link type.

2

Run it to the end

Click Run to end. QA Runner answers every question on each page, checks each answer went in, clicks Next, and keeps going until the end of the survey.

3

Build a test plan

Load every question from the survey builder or a QSF, pick the questions that drive your routing, and choose the answers to try. QA Runner turns them into scenarios.

4

Review and download

Each scenario shows whether its pinned questions were shown and answered. Download the results, every answer given and the required checks as CSV files.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is Pirai QA Runner?

Pirai QA Runner is a free Chrome extension that tests Qualtrics survey links. It answers every question on each page, clicks Next until the end of the survey, follows test plans you create, and reports what worked, all from a side panel in Chrome.

Does QA Runner use AI to answer surveys?

No. Answers come from a seed, so the same seed on the same survey always gives the same answers, and any run can be repeated exactly. Questions you pin in a test plan always get the answers you chose.

Will test runs show up in my survey data?

On preview links, no. On live links, each run is submitted as a real response and counts towards quotas, the same as if you answered the survey yourself. The panel turns amber on live links as a reminder, so use the preview link where you can.

What is a test plan?

A test plan lists the questions that drive your survey routing and the answers to try for each. QA Runner turns it into scenarios, runs each one in a fresh session, and reports whether every pinned question was shown and answered. Plans can be downloaded as files to reuse or share.

Can QA Runner check required questions?

Yes. With required checks turned on, QA Runner clicks Next before answering and records which questions block it. It reports questions that are required but not enforced, enforced but not marked, and Other, please specify boxes that need text. It reads the required setting even when the asterisk is hidden.

Do I need to share my Qualtrics login?

No. QA Runner never asks for or reads your password or cookies. When you load questions from the survey builder, the request runs inside your own signed in builder tab.

Which question types are supported?

Multiple choice, dropdowns, text entry, forms, matrix tables (Likert, bipolar, dropdown, constant sum, text entry and MaxDiff), sliders, side by side, and pick, group and rank, in Qualtrics' current survey layout.

Where does my survey data go?

Nowhere. Everything runs in your browser, and nothing is sent to Pirai AI or any other server. Results are saved to your computer only when you download them.
