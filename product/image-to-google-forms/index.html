How it works

Two Ways to Convert — Choose What Works for You

Both methods start with the same upload. Enable Advanced Processing for OCR. Your preference determines how the Google Form is delivered.

Sign in with Google. Pirai AI creates the form directly in your Drive and returns an edit link. No pasting, no running code.

Download Apps Script

Download a .txt script file. Paste it into the built-in Google Forms editor and run it. No Google sign-in with Pirai AI required.

1

Upload your photo or scan

Go to platform.piraiai.com. Upload a clear photo or scan of your exam paper (JPG, PNG, or HEIC). Enable Advanced Processing for OCR text extraction. Select Google Forms as the output.

2

Click Export to Google Forms

Click Export to Google Forms. Sign in with Google if prompted.

3

Grant scoped Drive access

Google shows a permission screen. Pirai AI requests drive.file scope (creates new forms only — no access to existing Drive files) and forms.body scope (adds questions to the form). You can revoke access at any time from your Google Account settings.

4

Your Google Form is ready

Pirai AI creates the form in your Google Drive and returns a direct edit link. All questions extracted from your image are in place.

About the permissions: The drive.file scope gives Pirai AI access only to files it creates — not to any existing files, folders, or documents in your Drive. The forms.body scope is required by the Google Forms API to add questions. Pirai AI does not read, store, or process any other content in your Google account.

FAQ

Image to Google Forms Converter Questions

How do I convert an image of an exam paper to Google Forms?

Upload a clear photo or scan (JPG, PNG, HEIC) to platform.piraiai.com and enable Advanced Processing for OCR. One-click: click Export to Google Forms, sign in with Google, grant scoped permission — your form is created directly in your Drive. Apps Script: download the .txt script, open a blank Google Form, paste into the Apps Script editor, Save then Run.

What is the difference between one-click export and Apps Script?

One-click export uses Google OAuth — Pirai AI creates the form directly in your Drive using scoped drive.file and forms.body permissions. Apps Script generates a .txt file you paste into the Google Forms editor and run yourself — no Drive permissions are granted to Pirai AI. Both require Advanced Processing enabled for OCR extraction from images.

Can I just snap a photo with my phone and convert it?

Yes. Take a clear, well-lit photo of your printed exam paper or quiz with your smartphone camera. Make sure the text is readable and the image is not blurry. Upload the photo to Pirai AI with Advanced Processing enabled. The OCR engine extracts questions and answer options from the image and generates a Google Forms Apps Script.

What tips help get the best OCR results from image conversion?

For the best image to Google Forms results: use good lighting and avoid shadows on the paper, keep the camera parallel to the page to minimize skew, ensure all text is sharp and legible, and use Advanced Processing. Typed or printed text converts more accurately than handwritten text. If your exam has multiple pages, you can combine them into a PDF before uploading.

Does Pirai AI require a Google Workspace add-on?

No. Neither method requires a Marketplace installation. The one-click method uses Google OAuth to create the form in your Drive. The Apps Script method uses Google's built-in Apps Script editor — no Drive permissions are granted to Pirai AI.

What image formats does Pirai AI accept?

Pirai AI accepts JPG, JPEG, PNG, and HEIC image formats for direct upload. You can also combine multiple images into a single PDF before uploading. For multi-page exams, creating a PDF from your images is recommended so all pages are processed together in a single conversion.

Is Pirai AI Google Forms converter free?

Yes. Pirai AI gives you 1-2 free credits every day that reset daily. Image to Google Forms conversions using Advanced Processing may use additional credits for OCR extraction. Credit packs: 5 credits for $2.99, 15 credits for $6.99, 30 credits for $9.99. Credits never expire.
