3

Grant scoped Drive access

Google shows a permission screen. Pirai AI requests drive.file scope (creates new forms only — no access to existing Drive files) and forms.body scope (adds questions to the form). You can revoke access at any time from your Google Account settings.

Your Google Form is ready

About the permissions: The drive.file scope gives Pirai AI access only to files it creates — not to any existing files, folders, or documents in your Drive. The forms.body scope is required by the Google Forms API to add questions. Pirai AI does not read, store, or process any other content in your Google account.

Open Apps Script in Google Forms

Open a blank Google Form at forms.google.com. Click the three-dot menu in the top right. Select Apps Script. Delete the default placeholder code. Paste your Pirai AI script.

Google Forms Converter Questions

How do I convert a document to Google Forms?

Pirai AI offers two methods. One-click: upload your document at platform.piraiai.com, select Google Forms, click Export to Google Forms, sign in with Google, grant scoped permission, and your form is created directly in your Drive with an edit link. Apps Script: upload your document, download the .txt script, open a blank Google Form, paste the script into the Apps Script editor, Save then Run.

What is the difference between one-click export and Apps Script?

One-click export uses Google OAuth — Pirai AI creates the form directly in your Drive using scoped drive.file and forms.body permissions, returning an edit link with no copy-paste step. Apps Script generates a .txt file you paste into the Google Forms editor and run yourself — no Drive permissions are granted to Pirai AI. Both produce an identical Google Form.

Does Pirai AI require a Google Workspace add-on?

No. Neither method requires a Marketplace installation. The one-click method uses Google OAuth to create the form in your Drive directly. The Apps Script method uses Google's built-in Apps Script editor — accessible from the three-dot menu in any Google Form — and grants no permissions to Pirai AI.

Are the permissions safe?

One-click: Pirai AI requests drive.file scope (access only to files it creates — not existing Drive content) and forms.body scope (to add questions via the Google Forms API). You can revoke access at any time from your Google Account settings. Apps Script: the one-time prompt is a standard Google safety check. The script only accesses the specific form you opened. It does not touch your Drive, Gmail, or any other Google service. After clicking Allow, the script runs once and ends.

What question types does Pirai AI convert to Google Forms?

All 8 Google Forms question types: Multiple Choice, Checkboxes, Short Answer, Paragraph, Dropdown, Multiple Choice Grid, Checkbox Grid, and form title and description from Descriptive Blocks. Matrix grids are supported : most Workspace add-ons fail on grid questions.

How is Pirai AI different from Automagical Forms, Doc2Form, and FormsWrite?

Automagical Forms and Doc2Form are Workspace Marketplace add-ons requiring installation and ongoing Drive access. FormsWrite is a dedicated Google Forms converter that requires full Google account access. Pirai AI offers two methods: Apps Script (no Drive permissions granted to Pirai AI at all) and one-click OAuth (scoped drive.file only — new forms, no existing files). Both methods have no question limits, handle matrix grids, and convert to 26 other platforms from the same upload.
