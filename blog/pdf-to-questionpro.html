PDF to QuestionPro: Convert Research PDFs and Questionnaires Directly

Arun Raj•September 1, 2026•6 min read•Guide
PDF to QuestionPro

Research questionnaires, validated scales, and client survey scripts are commonly delivered as PDFs. QuestionPro does not accept PDF uploads. Pirai AI bridges this gap as an official QuestionPro partner: upload the PDF, review extracted questions, and the survey is created directly in your QuestionPro account. Both native digital PDFs and scanned paper questionnaires are supported.

PDF Types That Convert to QuestionPro

Native digital PDFs: Created electronically with selectable text. Common sources include published research instruments, client questionnaire briefs sent as PDFs, research proposal survey appendices, and MR discussion guides. Upload directly to Pirai AI - no additional settings needed.

Scanned paper PDFs: Physical questionnaires or survey scripts photographed or scanned to PDF. The text is an image, not selectable. Enable Advanced PDF Processing before uploading - Pirai AI runs OCR to extract question text from the scanned image.

Image files (JPG, PNG): Photographs of printed questionnaires taken on a mobile phone. Pirai AI's OCR processes these alongside PDF uploads - take a clear photo of each page and upload.

PDF to QuestionPro: The Workflow

1. Go to platform.piraiai.com. For scanned PDFs, enable Advanced PDF Processing. Upload your PDF.

2. Review extracted questions in the workspace. Verify question types (Single Choice, Multiple Choice, Rating Scale, Matrix, Open Text, NPS) and answer option wording. For validated scales, compare against the published instrument to confirm verbatim accuracy.

3. Select QuestionPro. If first time, connect your account. Pirai AI creates the survey in your QuestionPro account.

4. Open the created survey in QuestionPro. Configure skip logic, quotas, theme, and distribution settings. Activate and distribute.

MR Agency PDF Workflows

Market research agencies commonly receive questionnaire briefs and scripts from clients in PDF format. The traditional workflow: receive PDF, programmer manually enters every question in QuestionPro, QC review, back-and-forth on any errors. With Pirai AI, the programmer uploads the client PDF, reviews the automatically extracted questions in the workspace (which doubles as the QC step), and the survey is created in QuestionPro in minutes.

For agencies with multiple simultaneous fieldwork projects, this workflow change compounds into significant capacity. A programmer who previously spent 60-90 minutes per questionnaire on manual entry spends 15-20 minutes on Pirai AI conversion and QC, focusing the remaining time on logic configuration, panel setup, and fieldwork management.

Validated Scales in PDF Format

Academic and clinical researchers frequently need validated scales (standardised psychological or health instruments) deployed in QuestionPro. These scales are distributed as PDFs from publishers, journal supplementary materials, or institutional repositories. Pirai AI converts them to QuestionPro format with verbatim item wording preserved - critical for maintaining the psychometric validity of the instrument.

After conversion, verify item wording in the QuestionPro editor against the published scale and ensure response option codes match the instrument's scoring conventions before launching data collection.
