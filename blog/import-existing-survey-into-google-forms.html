Import an Existing Survey Into Google Forms

Arun Raj•August 13, 2026•5 min read•Guide
Importing an Existing Survey

Moving an existing survey into Google Forms - whether it is sitting in Qualtrics, SurveyMonkey, a Word document, or a PDF - requires more than a simple file upload. Google Forms has no survey import feature from external platforms or documents. Pirai AI bridges this gap: upload the source survey in any format, generate the Apps Script, and run it in Google Forms. The same survey, rebuilt in Google Forms, without retyping a single question.

Import Sources Supported

From Qualtrics: Export the survey as a QSF file from Qualtrics (Tools > Import/Export > Export Survey). Upload the QSF to Pirai AI. Select Google Forms and download the Apps Script.

From SurveyMonkey: Export the survey as a Word document from SurveyMonkey's export options. Upload the Word file to Pirai AI. Select Google Forms.

From Word: Upload the .docx survey script directly to Pirai AI.

From PDF: Upload the PDF survey. Enable Advanced PDF Processing for scanned documents.

From another Google Form: Use Google Forms' built-in import (import questions icon in the right toolbar) to copy questions between Google Forms - no Pirai AI needed for this specific case.

From Excel: Upload the .xlsx question bank. Pirai AI reads the first sheet.

What Transfers and What Does Not

ElementTransfers to Google Forms?
Question text and answer optionsYes - verbatim from source
Question types (MC, open text, matrix)Yes - mapped to Google Forms equivalents
Section breaksYes - become Google Forms sections
Skip logic / branchingNo - must be configured manually in Google Forms
Survey theme and brandingNo - apply in Google Forms after creation
Response data from old surveysNo - only the survey structure transfers

Running the Apps Script in Google Forms

1. Download the Apps Script from Pirai AI.

2. Open a new blank Google Form at forms.google.com.

3. Click the three-dot menu (top right of the form editor) > Script editor.

4. Delete any existing code. Paste the Pirai AI script. Click Run (triangle button).

5. A permissions dialog appears asking to authorise the script. Click Review Permissions > choose your Google account > Allow. This is a standard Google Apps Script permission request for editing the form.

6. Return to the Google Form tab and refresh. Your survey is now in Google Forms.

After Importing Your Survey

After the script runs, set up skip logic by clicking the three-dot menu on each question that routes to different sections and selecting Go to section based on answer. Apply branding in the palette icon at the top. Enable Quiz mode if needed under Settings > Quizzes. If your original survey had quotas or advanced logic, note that Google Forms does not support these - they are features not available in the platform.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I import a Qualtrics survey into Google Forms?+

Yes. Export the Qualtrics survey as a QSF file (Tools > Import/Export > Export Survey). Upload the QSF to Pirai AI at platform.piraiai.com, select Google Forms, download the Apps Script, and run it in a blank Google Form's Script Editor. Question structure and answer options transfer. Display logic must be reconfigured manually.

Can I transfer a SurveyMonkey survey to Google Forms?+

Yes. Export the SurveyMonkey survey as a Word document. Upload to Pirai AI, select Google Forms, download the Apps Script, and run it in a blank Google Form. Alternatively, paste the SurveyMonkey question text directly into the Pirai AI editor.

Does skip logic transfer when importing a survey to Google Forms?+

No. Skip logic (branching) does not transfer in the import - it must be configured manually in Google Forms after the survey structure is created. Set branching via the three-dot menu on each question > Go to section based on answer.

How do I authorise the Apps Script to run in Google Forms?+

When you run the Pirai AI Apps Script for the first time in the Script Editor, Google shows a permissions dialog. Click Review Permissions, select your Google account, and click Allow. This grants the script permission to edit your Google Form - it is a standard Google Apps Script authorisation step.
