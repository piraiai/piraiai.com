QuestionPro vs SurveyMonkey: Which Is Better for Research and Agencies?

Arun Raj•September 1, 2026•6 min read•Guide
QuestionPro vs SurveyMonkey

QuestionPro and SurveyMonkey are both widely used survey platforms with significant market presence. QuestionPro is stronger for professional research applications - offering advanced question types, a research edition, offline capability, and more robust analytics. SurveyMonkey has broader name recognition, a simpler interface, and a free tier that makes it accessible for occasional users. This comparison covers the key differences for research agencies and academic researchers.

Choose QuestionPro When

  • You need advanced research methodologies: MaxDiff, conjoint, Van Westendorp pricing research
  • You conduct field research requiring offline survey capability (in-store, clinical sites, remote areas)
  • You want built-in statistical analysis including cross-tabulation and significance testing
  • You need a research or academic edition with features designed for structured academic study design
  • Your CX programme requires closed-loop follow-up workflows and NPS dashboards
  • You want direct Pirai AI integration as an official partner

Choose SurveyMonkey When

  • You need a simple, familiar interface for non-research teams creating basic feedback forms
  • Your primary use case is employee pulse surveys or simple HR feedback
  • You want the SurveyMonkey brand recognition for respondent trust in consumer studies
  • Your survey is simple enough that advanced question types are not needed
  • You are an individual user who wants a well-known platform with a free starting point

Frequently Asked Questions

Is QuestionPro better than SurveyMonkey for market research?+

For professional market research requiring MaxDiff, conjoint analysis, semantic differential, offline field surveys, and built-in statistical cross-tabulation, QuestionPro is the stronger choice. SurveyMonkey is better for simple surveys where ease of use and brand familiarity matter more than advanced research features.

Does QuestionPro have a free plan?+

Yes. QuestionPro's free plan allows unlimited surveys with limited responses per survey. The Research, Advanced, and Team plans unlock higher response limits, advanced question types (MaxDiff, conjoint), offline capability, and statistical analysis features. The Academic plan is available for university researchers.

Does QuestionPro support offline surveys?+

Yes. QuestionPro has a dedicated offline survey app for iOS and Android. Surveys are downloaded to the device and completed without an internet connection. Responses sync automatically when connectivity is restored. SurveyMonkey does not have native offline survey capability.
