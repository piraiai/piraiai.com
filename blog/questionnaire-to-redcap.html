Questionnaire to REDCap: Convert an Existing Questionnaire in Seconds

Arun Raj•August 13, 2026•5 min read•Guide
Existing Questionnaire to REDCap

You have an existing questionnaire - validated research instrument, a long-running paper form, a questionnaire reviewed and approved by your team or ethics board. Getting it into REDCap without manually re-entering every question and answer option is the challenge. REDCap has no questionnaire import from external documents or platforms. Pirai AI converts your existing questionnaire to a Data Dictionary CSV for direct REDCap import - seconds to generate, minutes to verify.

Existing Questionnaire Sources That Convert

Paper-based questionnaires: Scan to PDF, enable Advanced PDF Processing in Pirai AI, and convert. The same questionnaire your team has used offline moves into REDCap without retyping.

Word document questionnaires: Survey scripts, research instruments, and questionnaire drafts approved for use. Upload the .docx directly.

PDF questionnaires: Published instruments, regulatory forms, validated clinical scales. Native digital PDFs extract cleanly. Scanned PDFs require Advanced PDF Processing.

Questionnaires from other platforms: Qualtrics QSF, SurveyMonkey Word exports, or other platform outputs. Upload the source file to Pirai AI and export as Data Dictionary CSV.

Excel-based question banks: Question lists maintained in spreadsheets with answer options in columns.

Why Verbatim Preservation Matters

For questionnaires used in longitudinal research, clinical studies, or approved ethics protocols - the wording of each question must match the approved instrument exactly. Retyping introduces transcription errors. Pirai AI extracts verbatim text from the source document and applies it directly to the REDCap output - no paraphrasing, no rewriting. The review workspace shows every extracted question alongside its source text so you can verify before importing.

After Converting Your Questionnaire to REDCap

Once imported, complete the setup in REDCap: Review all imported fields in the Online Designer - check field types, labels, and answer codes. Set branching logic for conditional field display using REDCap's logic syntax [field_name] = '1'. Configure validation rules on text fields (date, email, number, integer, phone). The questionnaire structure is in REDCap - the configuration layer is set up in the REDCap editor.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I convert an existing paper questionnaire to REDCap?+

Yes. Scan the paper questionnaire to PDF and upload to Pirai AI at platform.piraiai.com with Advanced PDF Processing enabled. Pirai AI runs OCR to extract question text from the scanned image and generates the Data Dictionary CSV. Import into REDCap via REDCap project.

Does Pirai AI preserve exact question wording when converting to REDCap?+

Yes. Pirai AI extracts verbatim text without paraphrasing. For questionnaires used in ethics-approved research or longitudinal studies, verbatim preservation is critical - any wording change may require protocol amendment and re-approval.

Can I migrate a questionnaire from Qualtrics to REDCap?+

Yes. Export the Qualtrics survey as a QSF file (Tools > Import/Export > Export Survey). Upload the QSF to Pirai AI, select REDCap, download the Data Dictionary CSV, and import into REDCap.

How long does it take to convert an existing questionnaire to REDCap?+

10-20 minutes for most questionnaires: 1-3 minutes for Pirai AI processing, 5-10 minutes for review in the workspace, and under 1 minute for REDCap import. Versus hours of manual question entry.
