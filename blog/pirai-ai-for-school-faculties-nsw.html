Pirai AI for NSW School Faculties: Head Teachers and KLA Coordinators

Arun Raj•August 26, 2026•5 min read•Guide
For NSW Head Teachers and Faculty Coordinators

In NSW public schools, Head Teachers manage Key Learning Areas (KLAs) - coordinating teaching programmes, moderating assessment, managing faculty meetings, and producing compliance documentation for the Principal and NESA. In private and Catholic schools, equivalent roles are Department Heads, Heads of Faculty, or Subject Coordinators. Across all these roles, there is a recurring administrative task: converting faculty documents into digital platforms for distribution to students, parents, or staff. Pirai AI converts any faculty document to Google Forms or Microsoft Forms in minutes.

Faculty-Level Assessment Operations

Common assessment tasks (CATs) and faculty-wide assessment: Faculties that design common assessments for a year group produce these in Word for moderation and NESA compliance. After moderation, the same assessment needs to be in Google Forms for digital delivery. Pirai AI converts the moderated Word document to Google Forms - all teachers in the faculty get the same form from one conversion, with no individual re-entry.

Marking rubrics and feedback forms: Structured feedback instruments for extended response, practical, and research tasks. Convert the faculty-approved rubric from Word to Google Forms for peer assessment, self-assessment, or teacher feedback delivery.

Student performance tracking: End-of-term diagnostic assessments, pre-test and post-test instruments for tracking student progress within a topic or stage. Convert once, deploy across the whole faculty's classes.

Faculty Operations Documents

  • Faculty meeting agenda and action items survey: Convert the faculty meeting agenda from Word to a Google Form for async input from staff who cannot attend - collect priorities, availability, and professional development needs before the meeting
  • Staff professional learning needs analysis: Annual PL survey for faculty staff converted from Word to Google Forms - aggregate responses in Google Sheets to identify KLA-specific professional development priorities
  • Faculty resource audit: Equipment, textbook, and resource needs survey converted to Google Forms for staff to complete and Head Teacher to compile
  • Subject selection information and preference forms: Year 10 subject selection information evenings generate student preference and consultation forms - convert from the faculty's Word template to Google Forms for digital completion
  • Faculty self-evaluation for school plan: Internal faculty review instruments aligned to the School Excellence Framework converted from Word to Google Forms for structured staff reflection

KLA-Specific Uses

English Faculty: Reading and writing sample analysis instruments. HSC marker calibration response forms. Literacy benchmark tracking tools converted to Google Forms.

Mathematics Faculty: Numeracy diagnostic assessments for placement and intervention identification. NAPLAN numeracy preparation question banks converted to Google Forms.

Science Faculty: Practical investigation data recording templates. Equipment safety and competency checklists. Science talent search entry question instruments.

HSIE Faculty: Geography fieldwork data collection instruments. History source evaluation frameworks. HSIE elective selection preference surveys.

PDHPE Faculty: Fitness assessment recording instruments. Student health and wellbeing survey instruments for Stage 4 mandatory programme.

NESA and Compliance Documentation

NSW Head Teachers generate significant NESA-compliance documentation - assessment schedules, marking guidelines, feedback records, and moderation notes. While Pirai AI does not replace NESA documentation systems, it converts the assessment instruments themselves to digital platforms, reducing the time spent on distribution and collection of student responses that then feed back into compliance records.
