Google Forms vs Qualtrics: When to Use Which (2026)

Arun Raj•July 18, 2026•3 min read•Glossary
Definition

Google Forms and Qualtrics are not competing tools - they serve different research contexts. Google Forms is free and simple, best for low-stakes data collection. Qualtrics is enterprise-grade, best for research, CX programmes, and surveys where data quality, logic, and reporting depth matter.

TL;DR Verdict

Use Google Forms when cost is the primary constraint, the survey is simple (no matrix, minimal logic), respondents may not have institutional credentials, and the results are for internal use where summary charts are sufficient.

Use Qualtrics when the survey is for research publication, contains matrix or complex question types, requires advanced branching logic, needs to be benchmarked against industry standards (NPS, HCAHPS), or feeds into a dashboard, CRM, or reporting system.

Where Google Forms Wins

Cost. Google Forms is completely free for any number of forms, responses, and collaborators. For small organisations, individual researchers, educators, and teams that need basic data collection without a budget, Google Forms is unmatched on cost. Qualtrics pricing can be significant for individual researchers not covered by an institutional licence.

Simplicity and speed. A Google Form can be created and shared in five minutes. The interface requires no training. For internal team surveys, event RSVPs, quick feedback forms, and simple data collection tasks, Google Forms is faster to deploy than any alternative.

Google Workspace integration. Responses go directly to Google Sheets for teams already working in Google's ecosystem. Automatic notifications, Sheets formulas, and Looker Studio visualisation work seamlessly with Google Forms data without any additional setup.

Accessibility. Google Forms works on any device and does not require the respondent to have any account or app. For surveys of general public populations, external stakeholders, or community members who may not have institutional credentials, Google Forms has the lowest barrier to response.

Where Qualtrics Wins

Research-grade question types. Matrix questions with row randomisation, rank order, constant sum, semantic differential scales, heat map, slider, and drill-down questions are all available natively in Qualtrics. For academic and market research where question type precision affects data quality, Qualtrics' question library has no equivalent in Google Forms.

Advanced logic. Question-level display logic with multiple conditions, skip logic, embedded data routing, and loop-and-merge are Qualtrics capabilities that have no equivalent in Google Forms' section-level branching. For surveys where the question path depends on multiple prior answers simultaneously, Qualtrics is categorically more capable.

Data quality controls. Qualtrics includes response quality scoring, bot detection, attention check question support, straight-line detection, and completion time flagging built in. For research where data quality affects conclusions, these controls matter. Google Forms has none of these.

Reporting depth. Qualtrics' Results dashboards, Text iQ for qualitative analysis, cross-tabulation, statistical significance testing, and export to SPSS, R, and SAS go far beyond Google Forms' summary charts. For programmes that need actionable insight from survey data rather than just the raw counts, Qualtrics provides the analysis layer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Google Forms good enough for academic research?+

Google Forms is appropriate for low-stakes academic surveys where question types are simple (multiple choice, short answer, linear scale), logic is minimal, and the data does not need to be published in a peer-reviewed journal. For research requiring validated instruments with matrix questions, randomisation, and IRB audit trails, Qualtrics or REDCap are more appropriate.

Does Qualtrics have a free version?+

Qualtrics offers a free account with very limited features (limited surveys, responses, and no advanced logic). Most Qualtrics users access it through an institutional site licence at their university, hospital, or organisation, which provides full features at no per-user cost. Individual researchers not covered by an institution can access Qualtrics through paid plans or the XM for Academic program.

Can I migrate from Google Forms to Qualtrics?+

Yes. Export your Google Form as a Word document or PDF, upload to Pirai AI, and select Qualtrics as the target platform. The question structure, scales, and answer options are converted to a Qualtrics QSF file for direct import. Logic that cannot be replicated from a Google Form export will need to be configured manually in Qualtrics.

Which is better for NPS surveys: Google Forms or Qualtrics?+

Qualtrics has a native NPS question type that renders the 0-10 scale correctly and automatically calculates Promoter, Passive, and Detractor scores in dashboards. Google Forms requires a Linear Scale workaround and manual NPS calculation from the export. For NPS programmes, Qualtrics is significantly better. For a one-off NPS survey without reporting requirements, Google Forms' Linear Scale is an acceptable workaround.
