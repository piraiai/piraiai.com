Pirai AI in Canadian Research

Pirai AI is used by Canadian academic researchers, graduate students, clinical trial teams, and market research agencies to convert Word, PDF, and Excel survey documents to Qualtrics QSF, REDCap Data Dictionary CSV, LimeSurvey LSS, and 24 more platform formats. All document processing is client-side with no server storage - consistent with Canadian privacy principles under PIPEDA and provincial privacy laws.

What Canadian Researchers Use Pirai AI For

  • Converting CIHR-funded clinical instrument PDFs (PROMIS, PHQ-9, EQ-5D-5L) to REDCap Data Dictionary CSV for Health Canada-regulated trials
  • Converting SSHRC dissertation survey instruments from Word to Qualtrics QSF for graduate research
  • Converting the same instrument to Qualtrics (social science survey arm) and REDCap (health data clinical arm) from one upload for mixed-methods Canadian studies
  • Converting NSERC human factors study instruments from Word to Qualtrics for participant research
  • Converting community-based research instruments to LimeSurvey LSS for self-hosted deployment supporting OCAP data sovereignty requirements
  • Converting market research questionnaires from Word to SurveyMonkey or QuestionPro for Canadian MR agencies

PIPEDA-Compatible Processing

Under PIPEDA, personal information should only be collected, used, and disclosed for purposes the individual would consider appropriate. For instrument document processing:

  • Client-side processing: Documents are processed in memory without persistent server storage. Your instrument document is not retained after conversion.
  • No AI training: OpenAI API is configured with data sharing disabled - your documents do not contribute to model training.
  • No third-party account access: Google Forms output runs in your own Google account - no third-party access is granted.

Instrument documents uploaded to Pirai AI are not personal information under PIPEDA - they are research method documents (question batteries, scales, survey frameworks). However, the client-side, no-retention architecture is consistent with PIPEDA's principle of limiting collection to what is necessary for the stated purpose.

OCAP and Indigenous Research Instruments

For Canadian research involving First Nations, Inuit, or Metis communities under OCAP principles, instrument documents may be community-owned or community-sensitive. Pirai AI's client-side processing and no-retention architecture means the instrument document is not stored on third-party servers after conversion - consistent with community ownership of research data. For conversion outputs intended for self-hosted deployment supporting community data control, Pirai AI's LimeSurvey LSS output is the appropriate choice.
