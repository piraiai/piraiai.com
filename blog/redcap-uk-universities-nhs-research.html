REDCap at UK Universities and NHS Research: Access and Setup Guide

Arun Raj•August 05, 2026•5 min read•Guide
REDCap in UK Research

REDCap is the standard electronic data capture platform for NHS-linked and NIHR-funded clinical research in the UK. UK university medical schools, teaching hospitals, and NHS Trusts operate REDCap instances under institutional data governance. For clinical trials requiring ICH GCP compliance, REDCap's field-level audit trail is the UK academic sector's most widely used solution.

UK Institutions with REDCap

REDCap is confirmed at major UK medical research institutions including:

  • University of Oxford - Oxford REDCap operated by the Centre for Clinical Vaccinology and Tropical Medicine and other departments
  • University of Cambridge - REDCap access via Clinical School Computing Service
  • King's College London - REDCap for clinical and health research
  • UCL (University College London) - REDCap via Research Computing
  • University of Edinburgh - REDCap for MRC-funded and NHS-linked studies
  • University of Manchester - REDCap via Manchester Academic Health Science Centre
  • University of Bristol, Imperial College London, Newcastle, Sheffield - REDCap confirmed via consortium membership

NHS Foundation Trusts and Clinical Research Networks in England, Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland also operate REDCap instances for NIHR-sponsored studies. Contact your R&D (Research and Development) department if you need REDCap access for an NHS-sponsored study.

HRA Approval and REDCap

Research involving NHS patients, NHS staff, or NHS data in England requires Health Research Authority (HRA) approval. Your HRA application should specify REDCap as the data capture platform and describe the data governance arrangements - specifically that data is stored on institutional UK servers and covered by the institution's data security frameworks. Scotland (NHS R&D), Wales (Health and Care Research Wales), and Northern Ireland have equivalent approval processes.

NIHR-funded research frequently specifies REDCap in grant conditions. Wellcome Trust clinical research programmes often use REDCap for Phase I-III studies requiring audit trails. The MRC Statistical Analysis Plan (SAP) for clinical trials typically references the EDC system - if REDCap is specified in the SAP, it is contractually required.

REDCap and UK GDPR Data Sovereignty

REDCap on UK institutional servers provides the strongest UK GDPR data sovereignty position for research: health data never leaves UK infrastructure, there is no international data transfer to the US or elsewhere, and the research data is covered by the institution's own information security and data governance framework. For clinical trials involving GDPR-special category data (health data), this institutional control is a significant compliance advantage over US-hosted cloud platforms.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which UK universities have REDCap?+

REDCap is available at most UK university medical schools and teaching hospitals including Oxford, Cambridge, UCL, King's College London, Edinburgh, Manchester, Imperial, Bristol, Newcastle, and Sheffield. NHS Trusts and Clinical Research Networks also operate REDCap for NIHR-sponsored studies. Contact your institution's Research and Development department for access.

Do I need HRA approval to use REDCap for NHS research?+

Yes. Research involving NHS patients, staff, or data in England requires HRA (Health Research Authority) approval regardless of which data capture platform you use. REDCap does not bypass HRA approval requirements - it is the platform used to collect data after HRA approval is obtained. Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland have equivalent NHS research approval processes.

Is REDCap UK GDPR compliant for health data?+

REDCap on UK institutional servers is generally the strongest UK GDPR compliance position for research involving health data: data stays on UK infrastructure with no international data transfer, covered by the institution's information security framework. Health data is GDPR special category data (Article 9) - institutional REDCap with UK data residency provides the clearest compliance basis.
