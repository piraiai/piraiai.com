Survey Tools for Canadian Graduate Students: REB, Qualtrics, and REDCap

Arun Raj•August 05, 2026•5 min read•Guide
For Canadian Graduate Students

Canadian MA, MSc, and PhD students conducting survey research must obtain REB (Research Ethics Board) approval under TCPS 2 before collecting any data. Most Canadian universities provide institutional Qualtrics access and, for health research, REDCap. Building your survey instrument efficiently after REB approval is where Pirai AI saves the most time - converting your approved Word instrument to the target platform in minutes rather than hours.

REB Approval Under TCPS 2

All research involving human participants at Canadian universities requires REB approval under TCPS 2 (Tri-Council Policy Statement: Ethical Conduct for Research Involving Humans, 2nd edition). Your REB application must describe:

  • The survey platform you intend to use and where data will be stored
  • Whether data storage is in Canada or the US (PIPEDA cross-border disclosure)
  • How long data will be retained and how it will be destroyed
  • Whether surveys are anonymous or linked to participant identity

Institutional Qualtrics (US cloud with institutional DPA) and institutional REDCap (Canadian servers) are both well-understood by Canadian REBs. Using a personal Google account for research collecting identifiable information requires more detailed justification.

Accessing Survey Platforms as a Graduate Student

Qualtrics: Access via institutional SSO. At most Canadian universities, graduate students log in directly at [youruni].qualtrics.com. Some institutions require your supervisor to request survey creation rights. Contact your library, IT services, or research computing team. REDCap: Access is typically tied to an approved REB project at Canadian institutions. Contact your supervisor or the institution's clinical research unit. Access requests at CIHR-funded institutions often require your ethics approval letter.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do Canadian graduate students need REB approval for surveys?+

Yes. All research involving human participants at Canadian universities requires REB (Research Ethics Board) approval under TCPS 2 before data collection. Contact your supervisor and your institution's Research Ethics Office to begin the REB application process. Many universities have expedited review for minimal-risk surveys.

How do Canadian graduate students access Qualtrics?+

Access Qualtrics via institutional SSO at [youruni].qualtrics.com. If survey creation rights are not granted automatically, contact your library or IT services. Some institutions require your supervisor to submit an access request. Allow a few business days for provisioning.

Should a Canadian PhD student use Qualtrics or REDCap?+

Use Qualtrics for social science, psychology, education, and humanities surveys. Use REDCap if your research involves sensitive health information, your REB protocol specifies institutional data management, or if you are conducting CIHR-funded clinical research. REDCap access at Canadian institutions is typically tied to an approved REB project.
