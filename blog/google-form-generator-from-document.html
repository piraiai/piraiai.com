Google Form Generator: Turn Any Document Into a Form Automatically

Arun Raj•July 31, 2026•5 min read•Guide
Quick Answer

A Google Form generator either creates questions from a prompt (Google Gemini, Weavely) or converts an existing document into a form (Pirai AI, Formswrite). If you already have questions in a Word, PDF, or Excel document, use a document-based generator like Pirai AI. If you need to create questions from scratch, use Google's built-in Gemini on paid Workspace plans.

Two Types of Google Form Generator

Prompt-based generators write new questions from a text description. Google Gemini (built into Forms on paid Workspace plans) and Weavely work this way. Best when starting from scratch with no existing questions.

Document-based generators read an existing document and convert its questions into a Google Form. Pirai AI, Formswrite, and several Workspace add-ons work this way. Best when you have an existing survey, quiz, or questionnaire in a document format. Most people searching for a "Google Form generator" actually want this type.

Google Gemini in Google Forms

Since mid-2025, Google has integrated Gemini AI into Google Forms on paid Workspace plans (Business Standard and above - not available on free accounts). Click "Help me create a form," describe what you want, and Gemini generates a set of questions.

Where it works: Creating a new form from a description when you have no existing questions. Where it falls short: It generates new questions from your description - it does not read your existing Word document or PDF and reproduce your exact questions verbatim. For validated research instruments or approved survey templates where exact wording matters, Gemini is the wrong tool.

How Pirai AI Generates a Google Form

Upload your document at platform.piraiai.com. Select Google Forms. Download the Google Apps Script. Open a blank Google Form, click the three-dot menu, select Script editor, paste the script, click Run. The script creates all your questions inside your own Google Form using your own Google account. Pirai AI never accesses your Drive.

This supports all Google Forms question types: Multiple Choice, Checkboxes, Short Answer, Paragraph, Linear Scale, Multiple Choice Grid (matrix), and Checkbox Grid. A 100-question survey with matrix questions generates in the same time as a 10-question quiz.

Which Generator to Use

Use Google Gemini if you have a paid Workspace plan and want to generate a new form from a description. Best for simple internal forms with no existing questions to convert.

Use Formswrite if you have a Google Doc or basic Word file with simple multiple choice questions and are comfortable granting Drive access. Best for education quizzes targeting LMS formats.

Use Pirai AI if your document has complex question types (matrix, Likert batteries, validated research instruments), if you prefer not to grant Drive access, if you also need output for Qualtrics or REDCap, or if you want a free daily tier without a lifetime cap.
