Alchemer vs Qualtrics: Which Survey Platform Should You Choose?

Arun Raj•August 13, 2026•5 min read•Guide
Alchemer vs Qualtrics

Alchemer and Qualtrics are both used by MR agencies and enterprise research teams for advanced survey programming. They are not direct substitutes - Alchemer is a mid-market survey platform; Qualtrics is an enterprise XM suite. The choice depends on team size, budget, academic vs commercial use, and whether you need the Qualtrics XM ecosystem or just a capable survey programming tool. Pirai AI converts documents to both platforms - so if you are on either and need to move, the structural migration is straightforward.

Choose Alchemer When

  • You are a small-to-mid MR agency that does not need Qualtrics' XM analytics layer
  • Per-seat pricing works for your team size - typically fewer than 10 programmers
  • You need FedRAMP-authorised status for US government research contracts
  • You need custom JavaScript scripting for advanced survey behaviours
  • You want Alchemer Workflow for closing the loop on customer feedback within the same platform
  • Your clients or stakeholders use Alchemer and expect deliverables in that format

Choose Qualtrics When

  • You are at a university - most have institutional licences making Qualtrics free for researchers
  • You need Text iQ, Stats iQ, or Qualtrics' advanced analytics and dashboard capabilities
  • You are running an enterprise XM programme (EX, CX, BX, ProductXM) that needs the full XM ecosystem
  • Your team is large enough that a site licence is more cost-effective than Alchemer's per-seat pricing
  • You need Qualtrics Panels for integrated online sample access
  • You need deep Salesforce / ServiceNow integrations within the Qualtrics XM platform

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Alchemer better than Qualtrics?+

Neither is universally better - they serve different contexts. Alchemer is better for mid-market MR agencies needing advanced survey logic at a lower cost than Qualtrics. Qualtrics is better for universities (institutional licences), enterprise XM programmes, and large research teams needing advanced analytics. Most universities use Qualtrics; most mid-market MR agencies choose between Alchemer, SurveyMonkey, and QuestionPro.

How does Alchemer pricing compare to Qualtrics?+

Alchemer uses per-seat pricing (~$55-$275/user/month depending on plan). Qualtrics uses site licences for academic institutions and enterprise contracts for commercial use. For small agencies (2-5 programmers), Alchemer is typically less expensive. For universities and large organisations with site licences, Qualtrics is often included in existing institutional agreements at no additional cost to researchers.

Do universities use Alchemer or Qualtrics?+

Most universities use Qualtrics under institutional site licences - it is the dominant academic research survey platform. Alchemer is rarely available as an institutional tool at universities. Researchers at universities without Qualtrics licences may use Alchemer as a commercial alternative, but this is uncommon.
