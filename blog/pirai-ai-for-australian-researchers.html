What Pirai AI Does for Australian Researchers

The most time-consuming part of any research survey is not writing the questions - it is getting those questions into the survey platform accurately. A 40-item validated scale written in Word for HREC submission takes 4-6 hours to build in Qualtrics or REDCap by hand. A 60-field clinical CRF takes most of a working day. Every manually entered character is a potential transcription error - and on a validated scale, altered item wording invalidates the instrument.

Pirai AI converts the HREC-approved Word instrument to the target platform's import format in under 15 minutes, with verbatim text from the source document preserved in every field label. Australian researchers use it to:

  • Convert validated clinical scales (DASS-21, PHQ-9, GAD-7, EQ-5D-5L, SF-36) from published PDFs to REDCap Data Dictionary CSV
  • Convert dissertation survey instruments from Word to Qualtrics QSF for HDR research
  • Convert the same instrument document to Qualtrics for one research arm and REDCap for the clinical arm of a mixed-methods study
  • Convert MR questionnaire scripts from client Word documents to SurveyMonkey or QuestionPro format for agency programming
  • Convert class quiz documents from Word to Google Forms, Kahoot, and Moodle from a single upload for Australian educators

Privacy Architecture: Why It Matters for Australian Research

Australian researchers frequently handle sensitive instrument documents - survey batteries for mental health research, CRFs for clinical trials, disability assessment scales, Indigenous health questionnaires. The documents themselves, even before any participant data is collected, may be confidential or require handling consistent with HREC protocols.

Pirai AI's processing architecture:

  • Client-side processing: Uploaded documents are processed in memory without persistent server storage. Your instrument document is not retained after processing.
  • No AI training on your content: The OpenAI API is configured with data sharing explicitly disabled. Pirai AI's processing of your documents does not contribute to AI model training.
  • No Google Drive access required: For Google Forms output, Pirai AI generates an Apps Script run in your own Google account - no third-party Drive access is granted.

This architecture is appropriate for researchers handling instrument documents under HREC confidentiality requirements. It is consistent with the ARC/NHMRC April 2026 AI guidance requirement that AI tools protect confidentiality and privacy.
