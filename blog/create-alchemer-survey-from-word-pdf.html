Create an Alchemer Survey From a Word or PDF Document

Arun Raj•August 13, 2026•5 min read•Guide
From Document to Alchemer Survey

Most Alchemer surveys start life as a document - a Word script, a PDF questionnaire, an Excel question bank. Getting from that document to a live Alchemer survey means either entering every question manually or using Alchemer's Import from Word feature with correctly formatted text. Pirai AI converts your source document to the required format and generates the import-ready .txt file. The survey builds in Alchemer in minutes, not hours.

The Document-to-Alchemer Challenge

Alchemer does not accept Word or PDF file uploads for survey creation. Its Import from Word feature is paste-based and requires Alchemer-specific syntax: ( ) for radio buttons, [ ] for checkboxes, ::NewPage:: for page breaks, underscores for open text. A plain Word document or PDF does not contain this syntax, so pasting it directly produces nothing usable.

The manual workaround is to open your document and recreate each question in the Alchemer Survey Builder. For a 30-question survey with multiple choice options, that is 2-3 hours of clicking and typing - with transcription errors that are easy to introduce and hard to catch before fieldwork begins.

The Pirai AI Workflow

Upload: Upload your Word (.docx), PDF (native or scanned), or Excel (.xlsx) file at platform.piraiai.com. Or paste survey text directly from any source.

Review: Every extracted question appears in the workspace with its detected type, answer options, and page assignment. Edit question text, change question types, fix any issues. The AI Survey Editor makes conversational changes - "change Q5 to a matrix question" or "add a not applicable option to Q8".

Export: Select Alchemer. Download the .txt file. Open it to see your survey in complete Alchemer Word Import syntax.

Import: Copy all content from the .txt file. In Alchemer: Create Survey > enter name > Import from Word > paste > Start Building. Your survey is created. Review in the Survey Builder and configure logic, required fields, and theme.

After Creating Your Alchemer Survey

Once Alchemer builds your survey from the import, configure these settings in the Survey Builder before publishing:

  • Skip logic and conditional display (Logic tab on each question)
  • Required field toggles on mandatory questions
  • Answer piping and text substitution variables
  • Survey theme, fonts, and colour scheme (Style tab)
  • Completion page redirect or thank you message
  • Quota settings if limiting responses by group

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I upload a Word document directly to Alchemer?+

No. Alchemer does not accept Word file uploads. The Import from Word feature is paste-based - you paste formatted text into Alchemer's import field. Use Pirai AI to convert your Word document to the required Alchemer syntax, then copy and paste the Pirai AI .txt output into Alchemer.

How long does it take to create an Alchemer survey from a Word document?+

With Pirai AI: 10-15 minutes for a typical 30-question survey (upload, review, export, paste, configure). Without Pirai AI: 2-3 hours of manual entry in the Survey Builder. For longer surveys, the time difference is proportionally larger.

Can I migrate a Qualtrics survey to Alchemer using Pirai AI?+

Yes. Upload the Qualtrics QSF file to Pirai AI, select Alchemer as the output, and download the .txt file. Paste into Alchemer via Create Survey > Import from Word > Start Building. Question text and answer options transfer. Display logic must be reconfigured in Alchemer's Logic tab after import.

What settings do I configure in Alchemer after the document import?+

After import, configure skip logic and branching in the Logic tab, required field toggles, answer piping, survey theme (Style tab), completion page settings, and quotas if needed. The Import from Word feature creates question structure only - logic and settings are configured in the Survey Builder.
