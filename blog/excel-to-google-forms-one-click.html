Question banks maintained in Excel convert to Google Forms in one click. Every question from your spreadsheet is in the form instantly.

Excel Layouts That Convert to Google Forms

Pirai AI reads the first sheet of your .xlsx file. Common layouts it handles:

  • Column A = question, Columns B-F = answer options: One question per row, answer options in adjacent columns. The most reliable structure.
  • Type-flagged columns: A column indicating question type (MC, OT, Matrix). Pirai AI uses the type flag to assign the correct Google Forms question type.
  • Survey scripts with coding: Question text, response options, and recode values across multiple columns. Pirai AI extracts question and option text.
  • Simple numbered lists: Questions in column A with options listed in rows below each question.

If your questions are on a sheet other than Sheet 1, move them to Sheet 1 before uploading.

Excel to Google Forms: Steps

Excel vs Google Sheets

Pirai AI converts .xlsx files directly. If your question bank is in Google Sheets, export it as Excel (File > Download > Microsoft Excel), then upload the .xlsx. The conversion produces a Google Form - not a Google Sheet. The form structure comes from your question bank; Google Forms then connects to a separate response Sheet automatically when you enable response collection.
