Survey Tools for Australian HDR Students: Qualtrics, REDCap, and Google Forms

Arun Raj•August 04, 2026•5 min read•Guide
For Australian HDR Students

Higher Degree by Research (HDR) students - PhD and Masters by Research candidates - are among the most frequent survey researchers at Australian universities, yet often have the least institutional support for survey setup. You typically have access to your university's Qualtrics licence and, for clinical or sensitive research, REDCap. Getting your survey instrument from a Word document into the right platform - accurately and quickly - is where most HDR students lose days they cannot afford.

Getting Access to Qualtrics and REDCap as an HDR Student

Qualtrics: Most Australian universities require your supervisor to submit an access request on your behalf before you can create surveys in Qualtrics. At UWA, the supervisor must submit the request via the library's Qualtrics access form and you are required to declare that your use is for academic research purposes only. At other institutions, HDR students log in directly via institutional SSO - contact your library or eResearch team if your account does not have survey creation rights. Allow 2-5 business days for access to be provisioned.

REDCap: At most Australian universities, HDR students access REDCap by emailing the ethics or research office. At CQUniversity, email ethics@cqu.edu.au with your CQU account to request access. At UniSA, complete the access request form via the Research Office. Access is usually tied to an approved HREC project - you will typically need your HREC approval letter before REDCap access is granted for your study.

HREC Approval and Your Survey Platform Choice

All research involving human participants at Australian universities requires Human Research Ethics Committee (HREC) approval before you recruit participants or collect data. Your HREC application should specify the survey platform you intend to use and justify the data handling arrangements. Key considerations in your HREC application:

  • Where will data be stored? Qualtrics stores data on US-based servers (with Australian data centre options on some plans). REDCap typically stores data on your institution's own servers. Google Forms stores data on Google's infrastructure. Your HREC will want to know which country the data will reside in.
  • What are the data security arrangements? Institutional Qualtrics and REDCap instances are covered by your university's information security framework. Personal-account Google Forms is not.
  • Is the survey anonymous or identifiable? Anonymous surveys have lower data governance requirements. Identifiable surveys require stronger justification for the platform choice.

Most HREC applications for surveys using institutional Qualtrics or REDCap are approved straightforwardly. Using a personal Google account for research collecting personal information is more likely to require additional justification.

Setting Up Your Survey Instrument Quickly

Frequently Asked Questions

How do HDR students get access to Qualtrics at Australian universities?+

Most Australian universities require your supervisor to submit an access request before you can create Qualtrics surveys. Contact your library, eResearch team, or IT helpdesk to find your institution's process. You may need to declare that your use is for academic research purposes only. Allow 2-5 business days for access to be provisioned.

Do HDR students need HREC approval to run a survey?+

Yes. All research involving human participants at Australian universities requires HREC (Human Research Ethics Committee) approval before data collection. Your HREC application should specify your survey platform and data storage arrangements. Most institutional Qualtrics and REDCap surveys receive approval without issue - using personal-account Google Forms for research collecting personal information requires more justification.

Should an Australian PhD student use Qualtrics or REDCap?+

Use Qualtrics for general academic research surveys including social science, education, psychology, and population studies. Use REDCap if your research involves sensitive health data or your HREC protocol or funding body specifically requires it. REDCap access is typically tied to an approved HREC project. For a low-risk pilot study with no personally identifiable information, Google Forms is the fastest option.

For Qualtrics: upload the QSF output via Tools > Import Survey. For REDCap: upload the Data Dictionary CSV via Project Setup > Data Dictionary.
