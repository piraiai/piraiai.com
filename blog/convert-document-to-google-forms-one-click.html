Convert Any Document to Google Forms in One Click

Arun Raj•August 20, 2026•5 min read•Guide
One-Click Google Forms Conversion

Pirai AI converts Word, PDF, and Excel documents to Google Forms in one click. Upload your document, review the extracted questions, click Convert to Google Forms, authorise with your Google account, and your form is created directly in your Google Drive. No Script Editor. No pasting. No coding. The same way you would expect any modern converter to work.

How It Works

Step 1 - Upload your document. Go to platform.piraiai.com. Upload your Word (.docx), PDF, or Excel (.xlsx) file. Pirai AI reads every question, answer option, and section from the document.

Step 2 - Review in the workspace. Every extracted question appears with its detected type and answer options. Edit, reorder, or adjust question types before converting. The AI Survey Editor makes changes in plain language - "change Q5 to a dropdown" or "add a prefer not to answer option to Q3".

Step 3 - Click Convert to Google Forms. Select Google Forms as your output platform and click Convert.

Step 4 - Authorise with Google. A Google authorisation window opens. Sign in to your Google account and grant Pirai AI permission to create a form in your Drive. This is a standard Google permission flow - the same process used by Google Workspace apps. You only need to authorise once.

Step 5 - Your form is created in Google Drive. Pirai AI creates the Google Form directly in your Drive directly in your Google Drive. All questions, answer options, and sections are built automatically. Click the link to open your new form in Google Forms.

What Pirai AI Creates

Every question in your document becomes a question in Google Forms with the correct type - Multiple Choice, Checkboxes, Short Answer, Paragraph, Linear Scale, Multiple Choice Grid, Date, or Dropdown. Sections from your document become sections in the form. No question type is left as plain text.

Does this work with scanned PDFs?+

Yes. Enable Advanced PDF Processing before uploading your scanned PDF. Pirai AI uses OCR to extract question text from the scanned image and creates the Google Form directly in your Drive.

What Google Forms question types does Pirai AI create?+

Multiple Choice, Checkboxes (multi-select), Short Answer, Paragraph (open text), Linear Scale (rating), Multiple Choice Grid (matrix), Checkbox Grid, Date, Time, and Dropdown. All 8 standard Google Forms question types are created from the detected question structure in your source document.

Do I need to give Pirai AI ongoing access to my Google account?+

No. The Google permission grants Pirai AI permission to create the form in your Drive at the time of conversion. You can revoke access at any time via your Google Account settings under Security > Third-party apps with account access.
