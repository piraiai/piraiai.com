Pirai AI for NSW Public Schools: Save Hours of Admin Time Every Week

Arun Raj•August 26, 2026•5 min read•Guide
For NSW Department of Education Schools

NSW public schools operate on Google Workspace for Education - giving every teacher and administrator access to Google Forms, Google Classroom, Google Sheets, and Google Drive through their NSW DoE Google account. Pirai AI connects directly to that ecosystem. Upload any Word document, PDF, or scanned paper form, click Export to Google Form, sign in with your NSW DoE Google account, and the form appears in your Drive ready to share. No IT involvement, no approval process, no manual question entry.

The NSW DoE Context

NSW public schools manage significant administrative volume - NESA compliance documentation, school plan reporting, Best Start assessments, parent communication, P&C consultation surveys, faculty meeting agendas, excursion forms, wellbeing check-ins, and end-of-term assessments. Most of these start in Word or as paper documents. Rebuilding them digitally in Google Forms takes time that teachers and administration staff do not have.

Pirai AI eliminates that rebuild. The form that used to take 45 minutes to enter question by question into Google Forms is ready in under 5 minutes. Across a 40-teacher school, that is hours of administrative burden removed every week.

Teaching Uses Across Stage Groups

Stage 1 and 2 (K-4) - Primary: Spelling and reading comprehension quizzes converted from Word to Google Forms for digital practice. Best Start Kindergarten Assessment-style question sets converted for early reading and numeracy check-ins. Parent communication forms for K­indergarten orientation and information evenings.

Stage 3 (Years 5-6): Literacy and numeracy practice in NAPLAN style converted to Google Forms for digital format preparation. End-of-unit assessment packs in Word or PDF converted to self-marking Google Forms quizzes. Student interest surveys for gifted and extension programmes.

Stage 4 and 5 (Years 7-10): Faculty-level assessment tasks converted from Word to Google Forms. Subject selection preference surveys for Year 10 students. Wellbeing team check-in forms. VET course interest surveys.

Stage 6 (Years 11-12 / HSC): Preliminary and HSC topic revision quizzes converted to Google Forms for self-paced study. Study habits and workload surveys for Year 12 coordinators. Scholarship and leadership nomination forms.

School Operations Uses

  • Excursion and camp consent forms: Word templates converted to Google Forms - parent responses feed directly into a Google Sheet for the class teacher and SAM (School Administrative Manager)
  • P&C consultation surveys: School plan and community consultation questionnaires converted from DoE templates to Google Forms for parent completion
  • Staff professional learning: Pre- and post-PL survey instruments converted from Word to Google Forms for teaching and learning team analysis
  • Student wellbeing check-ins: Positive Behaviour for Learning (PBL) check-in surveys and wellbeing pulse instruments converted from Word for regular whole-school or year group deployment
  • Transition and enrolment: Kindergarten transition information forms, Year 7 transition questionnaires, and new enrolment detail forms converted to Google Forms for digital completion

Sentral and Google Classroom Integration

NSW public schools use Sentral for student management and Google Classroom for learning management. Google Forms integrates natively with Google Classroom - converted assessment forms can be assigned directly to students as Google Classroom assignments. Responses collect in Google Sheets linked to each form, giving teachers real-time visibility of student completion and results without leaving the Google Workspace environment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can NSW public school teachers use Pirai AI with their DoE Google account?+

Yes. Pirai AI creates Google Forms in your Drive using your NSW Department of Education Google Workspace account. When you click Export to Google Form, sign in with your @det.nsw.edu.au or @education.nsw.gov.au account and click Allow. The form appears in your school Drive and can be shared via Google Classroom.

Does Pirai AI comply with NSW privacy requirements for student data?+

Pirai AI does not store uploaded documents after conversion. All processing is in memory and no school content, assessment text, or student information is retained. Pirai AI does not use school documents to train AI models. The converted Google Form is created in your own school Google Drive under your school's Google Workspace data governance arrangements.

Can the School Administrative Manager (SAM) use Pirai AI for operations forms?+

Yes. Excursion consent forms, enrolment information forms, P&C consultation surveys, and parent feedback forms all convert from Word or PDF to Google Forms using the same process as teacher assessment conversion. Any structured Word or PDF document with questions or fields converts to Google Forms.

How much time does Pirai AI save per NSW public school teacher per week?+

A typical 20-question unit assessment that takes 30-45 minutes to rebuild manually in Google Forms takes under 5 minutes with Pirai AI. Teachers who regularly create Google Forms for assessments, exit tickets, and parent surveys typically save 2-3 hours of administrative time per week.
