Photo of Exam Paper to Google Forms: Convert Any Printed Quiz Instantly

Arun Raj•August 21, 2026•5 min read•Guide
Photo to Google Forms

Take a photo of a printed exam paper, worksheet, or questionnaire on your phone. Upload the image to Pirai AI. Pirai AI uses OCR to extract every question from the photo and creates the Google Form directly in your Drive in one click. No scanning app, no manual transcription, no retyping. The most common use case: a teacher photographs a paper quiz from a textbook and has it as a Google Form in minutes.

How Photo to Google Forms Works

1. Take a photo of the printed quiz or exam. Use your phone camera. Photograph the printed page clearly - flat on a desk works best, good lighting, no shadows across the text. Multiple pages: photograph each page separately and upload as a multi-image set, or use a scanning app to combine pages into a single PDF first.

2. Upload the image at platform.piraiai.com. Accepted formats: JPG, PNG, HEIC (iPhone), and PDF of scanned pages. Enable Advanced PDF Processing for scanned PDFs. For direct image uploads, the OCR runs automatically.

3. Review extracted questions in the workspace. Pirai AI's OCR extracts question text from the image. Review every question in the workspace - OCR accuracy is high for clean printed text but pay attention to unusual fonts, handwritten additions, or low-contrast printing.

4. Click Export to Google Form. Authorise with your Google account. The form is created in your Drive. A direct edit link opens immediately.

Getting the Best Results From Photos

OCR accuracy from photos depends on image quality. For best results:

  • Flat surface: Place the paper flat on a desk rather than holding it in the air - eliminates distortion
  • Good lighting: Natural daylight or overhead lighting. Avoid shadows cast by your hand or phone
  • Fill the frame: Position the camera so the full page fills most of the photo frame
  • Straight angle: Shoot directly above the paper rather than at an angle - reduces perspective distortion
  • Clean paper: Faded ink, pencil marks, or handwritten annotations reduce OCR accuracy. Use the workspace review step to correct any extraction errors

Mobile Workflow: Phone to Google Form in Minutes

The full workflow from a phone:

  • Open platform.piraiai.com in your phone browser
  • Tap Upload and select the photo from your camera roll - or take a new photo directly
  • Review extracted questions in the mobile workspace
  • Tap Export to Google Form
  • Sign in to your Google account and tap Allow
  • The Google Form opens in your Drive - share the link with students immediately

Total time from photo to shareable Google Form: typically 5-8 minutes including review time. For a clean 20-question multiple choice paper, often under 5 minutes.

Enable Quiz Mode After Creation

After the form is created from your photo, open it in Google Forms and go to Settings > Quizzes > Make this a quiz. Set correct answers and point values per question. Students taking the quiz online see their score immediately upon submission. The review workspace in Pirai AI also shows any answer key markers detected from the photographed document before the form is created.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I take a photo of an exam paper and convert it to Google Forms?+

Yes. Photograph the printed exam paper on your phone and upload the image to Pirai AI at platform.piraiai.com. Pirai AI uses OCR to extract question text from the photo and creates the Google Form directly in your Drive with one click and one Google authorisation step.

What image quality does Pirai AI need for photo-to-Google-Forms conversion?+

Best results come from photos taken flat on a desk with good lighting, camera directly above the paper, and no shadows across the text. Clean laser-printed documents photograph well. Low-contrast photocopies or pages with handwriting may have lower OCR accuracy - review extracted questions in the workspace before creating the form.

Can I convert a multi-page exam paper photographed on my phone?+

For multi-page exams, either photograph each page separately and upload as individual images (Pirai AI processes each), or use a scanning app (iOS Document Scanner, Adobe Scan, or similar) to combine the pages into a single PDF before uploading. Enable Advanced PDF Processing for scanned PDFs.

Does this work for textbook quiz pages?+

Yes. Textbook quiz pages photographed from a phone or tablet work well. Two-column quiz layouts and small-print textbooks may need close-up photos or a scan for best OCR accuracy. Review all extracted questions in the Pirai AI workspace before creating the Google Form to catch any OCR errors.
