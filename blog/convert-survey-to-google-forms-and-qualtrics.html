Frequently Asked Questions

Can I export the same survey to Google Forms and Qualtrics?

Yes. Upload your document once to Pirai AI at platform.piraiai.com. From the same session, export to Google Forms (one-click OAuth, form in your Drive) and Qualtrics (download QSF, import via Create Survey > Import). No re-uploading required.

Why would I need the same survey on both platforms?

Common reasons: general audience on Google Forms (no respondent account required, free) and clinical or research arm on Qualtrics (advanced logic, IRB-appropriate). Employee feedback on Google Forms for field staff and Qualtrics for corporate teams. Multi-site studies where different sites use different platforms.

Does the Qualtrics export preserve question types?

Yes. Pirai AI exports a native QSF file. Multiple choice, matrix, Likert, text entry, slider, rank order and other Qualtrics question types are created from the document. Import via Create Survey > Import a QSF file in your Qualtrics account.

Can I also export to REDCap from the same session?

Yes. After exporting to Google Forms and Qualtrics, select REDCap as the output and download the REDCap Data Dictionary CSV. Import into your REDCap project via Project Setup > Data Dictionary. All from the same upload.

Is this free?

Yes. Free daily credits that reset every day. Google Forms export, Qualtrics QSF, and all other platform exports are available in the free tier. No credit card required.

What formats can I upload?

Word (.docx), PDF (native and scanned), Excel (.xlsx), CSV, Qualtrics QSF, XML, JSON, and plain text. If your survey is currently in Qualtrics, export it as a QSF and upload to Pirai AI to deploy to Google Forms or any other platform.
