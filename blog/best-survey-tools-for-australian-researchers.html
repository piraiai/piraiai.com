Best Survey Tools for Australian Researchers in 2026

Arun Raj•August 04, 2026•5 min read•Guide
The Australian Research Context

Australian researchers typically have access to two or three survey platforms through their institution - most commonly Qualtrics for general research surveys and REDCap for sensitive or clinical data. The choice of platform is rarely optional: HREC protocols, funding body requirements, and institutional data governance policies often determine which tool you must use. Pirai AI converts research instruments between any of these platforms, so the document you write once can be deployed on whichever platform your ethics approval or funding body specifies.

Qualtrics - The Standard Research Survey Platform

Qualtrics is the dominant research survey platform across Australian universities. The Group of Eight (Go8), Australian Technology Network (ATN), and most regionals provide Qualtrics access through institutional licences with Single Sign-On. At the University of Western Australia, Qualtrics can only be used for surveys with a clear academic research purpose - non-research operational surveys must run on an alternative platform, and non-compliance risks loss of access for all UWA users. Similar restrictions apply at most Australian institutions.

Best for: General academic research surveys requiring display logic, block randomisation, validated instruments, and SPSS/CSV data export. ARC-funded social science and humanities research. Market research studies. Online panels via Qualtrics Audience.

Access at Australian universities: Usually via your institution's SSO at [youruni].qualtrics.com. HDR students typically need their supervisor to submit an access request on their behalf. Contact your library or eResearch team if you cannot log in.

REDCap - For Sensitive and Clinical Research

REDCap is the standard electronic data capture platform for NHMRC-funded clinical research and studies involving sensitive health information. CQUniversity describes REDCap as "sometimes the tool of choice stipulated by your funding body" - if you need to use REDCap, you will typically be directed to do so by your ethics protocol or grant agreement. UniSA, UNSW, the University of Melbourne, and most academic medical centres operate their own REDCap instances under institutional data governance arrangements.

Best for: NHMRC-funded clinical trials. Studies involving health information or sensitive data requiring institutional data sovereignty. Longitudinal studies with multi-timepoint participant tracking. Research requiring GCP-aligned audit trails. Validated clinical assessment instruments (PHQ-9, GAD-7, PROMIS measures).

Access: Request access from your institution's research informatics team or ethics office. At UniSA, access is via a specific form through the Research Office (Ethics). Email ethics@cqu.edu.au at CQUniversity. Most Group of Eight universities have REDCap administered by their eResearch or IT division.

Google Forms - Teaching and Low-Risk Surveys

Google Forms is appropriate for low-risk surveys, course evaluations, student feedback, and pilot questionnaires where data sensitivity is low and institutional data governance requirements do not apply. Monash provides Google Forms alongside Qualtrics specifically for quick and collaborative work. The key consideration under the Australian Privacy Act: Google Forms data resides on Google's US-based infrastructure. For surveys collecting personally identifiable information, check with your institution's Privacy Officer before using Google Forms.

Best for: Course evaluations. Pilot questionnaires. Anonymous student feedback. Workshop registration. Surveys with no personally identifiable data.

Microsoft Forms - M365 Enterprise and Teaching

Microsoft Forms is included in every Microsoft 365 Education and Enterprise licence and is the natural internal survey tool for institutions standardised on M365. UniSA lists Microsoft Forms alongside Qualtrics and REDCap as an institutional survey option. For research involving personal information, confirm that your institution's M365 tenant is configured for Australian data residency before collecting data through Microsoft Forms.

Best for: Internal staff surveys. Teaching assessments and quizzes. Non-research feedback collection. M365-integrated workflows (SharePoint, Teams, Power BI).

LimeSurvey - Open Source, Self-Hosted

LimeSurvey is a free, open-source survey platform with a notable Australian connection - it was created by Australian developer Jason Cleeland in 2003. Some Australian universities with strong IT capacity self-host LimeSurvey instances, providing complete data sovereignty with no offshore data transfer. It is particularly common at institutions where European-style open-source tools are preferred for data governance reasons. Its multilingual support is strong - relevant for Australian research programmes involving Indigenous communities or international participant cohorts.

Best for: Institutions with IT capacity for self-hosting. Research requiring complete data sovereignty (on-premises). Multilingual surveys. Government and NFP research organisations with strict data localisation requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which survey platform do Australian universities use for research?+

Most Australian universities provide Qualtrics for general academic research surveys and REDCap for sensitive or clinical data collection. Microsoft Forms and Google Forms are available for teaching and low-risk surveys. The choice is often determined by your HREC protocol or funding body - NHMRC-funded clinical research frequently stipulates REDCap, while ARC-funded research typically uses Qualtrics.

Do Australian researchers need HREC approval to run a survey?+

Yes. All research involving human participants at Australian universities requires approval from a Human Research Ethics Committee (HREC) before data collection begins. The National Statement on Ethical Conduct in Human Research guides HREC review. Low-risk surveys may qualify for expedited or delegated review, but HREC approval is still required.

Is Google Forms suitable for Australian research surveys?+

Google Forms is appropriate for low-risk surveys without personally identifiable information. For research involving PII, health information, or sensitive data, Google Forms stores data on US-based servers, which may not satisfy Australian Privacy Principles regarding cross-border data disclosure. Confirm with your institution's Privacy Officer before using Google Forms for research collecting personal information.
