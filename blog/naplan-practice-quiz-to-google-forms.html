NAPLAN Practice Quiz to Google Forms: Digital Practice for Australian Students

Arun Raj•August 25, 2026•5 min read•Guide
NAPLAN Practice in Google Forms

NAPLAN (National Assessment Program - Literacy and Numeracy) has been fully online since 2023 for most students. Preparing Year 3, 5, 7, and 9 students for NAPLAN online means giving them practice experience with digital question formats - multiple choice on a screen, drag-and-drop, short typed answers. Converting your NAPLAN-style practice materials from Word or PDF to Google Forms gives students that digital practice experience using a tool already in your school's Google Workspace.

Why Digital NAPLAN Practice Matters

Since NAPLAN moved fully online, one of the most consistent pieces of teacher feedback is that students who have not regularly worked with digital assessment formats perform below their actual ability in the online format - not because of knowledge gaps, but because of unfamiliarity with answering questions on screen. Regular digital practice using Google Forms familiarises students with:

  • Reading comprehension questions on a screen
  • Typing short answers rather than writing
  • Selecting multiple choice answers digitally
  • Moving between questions and reviewing responses
  • Receiving immediate feedback on completion

Google Forms is familiar from other classroom activities - using it for NAPLAN practice reduces the tool-related anxiety for students who find the online format stressful.

NAPLAN Practice Materials to Convert

  • Published NAPLAN practice tests: The ACARA website and commercial publishers provide NAPLAN-style practice tests as PDFs - upload directly to Pirai AI
  • Teacher-created NAPLAN revision packs: Word documents with literacy and numeracy questions in NAPLAN style
  • AI-generated NAPLAN practice: Questions generated by ChatGPT or Gemini in NAPLAN format, pasted directly into Pirai AI
  • School-made practice papers: Year-level question banks compiled by curriculum coordinators in Excel or Word

Convert NAPLAN Practice to Google Forms

1. Collect your NAPLAN practice questions in a Word document or PDF.

2. Upload at platform.piraiai.com. Review extracted questions.

3. Click Export to Google Form. Sign in with your school Google account. Form created in Drive.

4. Enable Quiz mode: Settings > Quizzes > Make this a quiz. Set correct answers for multiple choice and true/false questions.

5. Share via Google Classroom as an assignment. Students complete digitally and receive feedback immediately.

Practice by Year Level

Year 3 (first NAPLAN): Focus on familiarisation - short sessions of 10-15 questions in Google Forms. Emphasis on the digital format itself, not performance. Simple multiple choice and short answer question types.

Year 5 (second NAPLAN): Structured practice closer to NAPLAN format. Timed Google Forms sessions (use Google Forms' timer via a classroom instruction). Mix of question types matching the Year 5 NAPLAN format.

Year 7 and 9: More rigorous preparation. Google Forms with self-marking for immediate feedback. Review class results in Google Sheets to identify common errors for whole-class re-teaching.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use Google Forms for NAPLAN practice in Australian schools?+

Yes. Google Forms is available in Australian public schools through Google Workspace for Education. Convert your NAPLAN-style practice questions to Google Forms using Pirai AI, enable Quiz mode for immediate marking, and distribute via Google Classroom. Students get digital practice experience that directly mirrors the online NAPLAN format.

How do I convert NAPLAN practice PDFs to Google Forms?+

Upload your NAPLAN practice PDF to Pirai AI at platform.piraiai.com. Select Google Forms. Click Export to Google Form and sign in with your school Google account. The form is created in your Drive. Enable Quiz mode (Settings > Quizzes > Make this a quiz) and set correct answers for multiple choice questions.

Can I create NAPLAN practice from AI-generated questions?+

Yes. Generate NAPLAN-style practice questions in ChatGPT or Gemini, copy the output, and paste it into the Pirai AI editor. Select Google Forms and export. Pirai AI converts the AI-generated questions to a Google Form in your Drive without any reformatting.

What NAPLAN question types can Google Forms handle?+

Multiple choice (A, B, C, D), short answer (typed text), extended response (paragraph), and true/false questions all work well in Google Forms Quiz mode with automatic marking. Drag-and-drop and hotspot question types from the official NAPLAN platform are not available in Google Forms - these require separate preparation activities.
