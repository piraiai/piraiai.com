The Best Free Google Forms Converters in 2026 (Tested)

Arun Raj•July 18, 2026•3 min read•Glossary
Definition

A free Google Forms converter converts a Word, PDF, or Excel document into a Google Form without charging per conversion. The 'free' tier varies significantly between tools - from unlimited conversions to a one-time trial - and the quality of conversion for complex question types varies even more.

What We Tested

We converted the same 20-question survey through each tool's free tier. The test survey included: 5 multiple choice questions, 3 open text questions, 2 Multiple Choice Grid (matrix) questions with 6-item scales, a 7-item Likert battery, 2 branching logic paths, and a Linear Scale NPS question. This reflects a real research or CX survey rather than a simple quiz.

We evaluated: free tier limits, which question types survived the conversion, whether the output was a real Google Form or a different format, whether Google Drive access was required, and overall quality of the resulting form.

1. Pirai AI

Free tier: 2 conversions per day, resets daily. No credit card required.

Test results: All 20 questions converted correctly. Matrix questions converted to Multiple Choice Grid with all row statements and column labels preserved. The 7-item Likert battery converted to a Multiple Choice Grid. Branching logic converted to section-based routing. NPS converted to Linear Scale (0-10) with endpoint labels. Output is a Google Apps Script - no Drive access required.

Additional platforms: The same document can be converted to Qualtrics, REDCap, SurveyMonkey, and 24 more platforms from the same free conversion slot.

Verdict for free users: 2 conversions per day is enough for most individual researchers and small teams. The daily reset means it is effectively unlimited over time for low-volume use.

2. Formswrite

Free tier: 5 conversions total (not daily reset). Pro from $19/month, Lifetime $149.

Test results: Multiple choice and open text questions converted well. Matrix questions were not preserved as Multiple Choice Grid - they were converted to individual questions or simplified. Branching logic was not converted. Strong for simple quizzes and education platforms (Canvas, Moodle, Kahoot).

Best for: Educators converting simple quiz documents to Google Forms and LMS platforms. Not suitable for professional survey research with matrix or Likert batteries.

3. Automagical Forms

Free tier / pricing: Monthly license from $26/month. Annual from $260/year. Requires a Chrome extension install and Google Drive access grant.

Test results: Handles simple question types within Google Workspace. Matrix questions and Likert batteries lost structure. Output is created directly in Google Drive (requires Drive permissions). Google Forms output only - no other platforms supported.

Best for: Google Workspace power users converting simple documents to Google Forms who are comfortable granting Drive access and paying for a monthly license.

4. Doc2Form

Free tier / pricing: Open source (free to self-host). Hosted version: Starter $5 (10 credits), Pro $12 (30 credits), Power $29 (100 credits). Credits never expire.

Test results: Google Forms output only. Simple question types converted correctly. Matrix questions and Likert batteries were not preserved as grid question types - converted to individual questions. Good for simple forms where Google Forms is the only target.

Best for: Users who need Google Forms only and want a pay-per-credit model with no subscription. Open source for teams able to self-host.

5. Weavely

Free tier: Free plan available.

Test results: Weavely generates Weavely forms from prompts - it is not a document converter. It does not accept Word or PDF uploads for conversion. Output is a Weavely form, not a Google Form. Not a direct comparison to the other tools but frequently appears in searches for "Google Forms converters."

Best for: Building new simple forms from prompts within the Weavely platform. Not applicable for document-to-Google-Forms conversion.
