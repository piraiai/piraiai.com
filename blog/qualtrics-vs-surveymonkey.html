Qualtrics vs SurveyMonkey: Which Is Better for Research?

Arun Raj•September 1, 2026•6 min read•Guide
Qualtrics vs SurveyMonkey

Qualtrics and SurveyMonkey are the two most recognised survey platforms globally. They are genuinely different products with different strengths. Qualtrics is an enterprise XM (experience management) platform with advanced research features, complex question types, and powerful analytics. SurveyMonkey is a widely accessible survey tool with a strong free tier, simple interface, and large audience panel. The right choice depends on research complexity, budget, and institutional access.

Choose Qualtrics When

  • Your institution has a Qualtrics licence - most major universities do
  • Your research requires advanced question types: conjoint, MaxDiff, heat map, timing
  • You need complex Survey Flow: block randomisation, branching by condition, embedded data piping
  • You want in-platform statistical analysis via Stats iQ or text analysis via Text iQ
  • Your study requires validated system features for GCP-aligned research
  • You need to survey an external panel via Qualtrics XM Directory

Choose SurveyMonkey When

  • You need a free or low-cost option without institutional access to Qualtrics
  • Your survey is simple - standard question types, basic branching, quick turnaround
  • Your audience is general consumer or employee - SurveyMonkey's simple interface increases completion rates
  • You want a large consumer panel quickly via SurveyMonkey Audience
  • You are doing market research that does not require advanced experimental design features

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Qualtrics better than SurveyMonkey for academic research?+

For academic research requiring complex question types (Matrix, Slider, Rank Order), advanced Survey Flow branching, URL parameter capture, and institutional data governance, Qualtrics is the better choice - and most universities provide it via institutional licence. SurveyMonkey is better for simple surveys, consumer panels, and researchers without institutional Qualtrics access.

Does SurveyMonkey have a Matrix (Likert) question type?+

Yes, SurveyMonkey includes a Matrix question type for Likert-style rating grids. Qualtrics has more Matrix sub-types (Likert, Bipolar, Rating Scale, Dropdown Matrix) and more configuration options. For standard Likert scales, both platforms work - the difference is visible in complex matrix designs and scale sub-type options.

Is Qualtrics HIPAA compliant?+

Qualtrics offers HIPAA compliance on enterprise plans with a Business Associate Agreement (BAA). SurveyMonkey also offers HIPAA compliance on enterprise plans with a BAA. For university research involving PHI without a confirmed BAA in place, REDCap on institutional servers is the most straightforward HIPAA-compliant option.
