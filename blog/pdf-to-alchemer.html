PDF to Alchemer: Convert a PDF Survey or Questionnaire to Alchemer

Arun Raj•August 13, 2026•5 min read•Guide
PDF to Alchemer

PDFs are one of the most common formats for survey instruments, questionnaires, and research scales - published by vendors, government agencies, academic journals, and internal teams. But Alchemer has no PDF import feature. The path from PDF to Alchemer survey is: extract the questions from the PDF, format them in Alchemer's Word Import syntax, paste into Alchemer. Pirai AI handles all three steps automatically.

Types of PDFs Pirai AI Converts to Alchemer

Native digital PDFs - PDFs created electronically (from Word, InDesign, LaTeX, or a CRM export). Text is selectable. Pirai AI extracts questions directly with high accuracy.

Scanned paper surveys - Physical questionnaires scanned to PDF. Text is an image, not selectable. Enable Advanced PDF Processing in Pirai AI. OCR extracts the question text before conversion to Alchemer syntax.

PDF forms with fillable fields - Forms with interactive fields (checkboxes, text boxes). Pirai AI extracts both the field labels and the response options and converts to the correct Alchemer question type.

Multi-page PDF questionnaires - Long instruments spanning many pages. Pirai AI maintains page structure and applies ::NewPage:: markers in the Alchemer output to preserve section breaks.

Converting Validated Instruments From PDF to Alchemer

Validated research instruments and standardised questionnaires are almost always distributed as PDFs. Converting them to Alchemer manually introduces the risk of transcription errors - a changed word, a missed option, a reordered scale - that can invalidate the instrument's psychometric properties.

Pirai AI extracts verbatim text from the source PDF and applies it directly to the Alchemer output without paraphrasing or rewriting. The question text and answer options in Alchemer match the source PDF exactly. For instruments where exact wording is critical to scoring and validity, this is the safest conversion path.

PDF to Alchemer: Step by Step

1. Go to platform.piraiai.com. Upload your PDF. For scanned surveys, toggle on Advanced PDF Processing before uploading.

2. Pirai AI extracts all questions and answer options. Review in the workspace - check question types, wording, and page structure. Correct anything that needs adjustment.

3. Select Alchemer. Download the .txt file.

4. Open the .txt file, copy all content. In Alchemer: Create Survey > name the survey > Import from Word > paste content > Start Building.
