Print and Export, But Nothing Scans Back In

Microsoft Forms fits neatly into Microsoft 365: results flow to Excel automatically, and printing a blank form or a summary of responses is a couple of clicks away. That covers two directions, building a form and exporting what it collects, but not a third: reading a completed paper copy back in.

If a Microsoft Forms survey gets printed and handed out, on a factory floor, in a classroom, at an event without reliable Wi-Fi, those completed pages sit outside Forms entirely. Someone still has to open each one and type the answers into Excel by hand.

How Lens Fills the Gap for Microsoft Forms

Lens reads the printed, marked form directly and checks it against your survey's own questions. Convert your survey to a base format, map the printed layout from one blank copy, then upload scanned response PDFs in bulk.

The result exports straight to Excel or CSV, in a shape that matches the same workbook Microsoft Forms already exports to, so paper responses can sit in the same sheet as everything collected digitally.

Checked against your survey, not guessed

Every answer Lens extracts is verified against your survey's own known questions, options, and items. If something on the page can't be matched with confidence, it's flagged for review, not silently recorded.
