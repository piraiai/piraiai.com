Pirai AI for Sydney Private and Independent Schools: Less Admin, More Teaching

Arun Raj•August 26, 2026•5 min read•Guide
For Sydney's Independent and Catholic Schools

Sydney's independent and Catholic schools - from large TAS (The Association of Heads of Independent Schools of Australia) schools on the North Shore to K-12 Catholic schools in Western Sydney - operate with significant operational autonomy. Procurement decisions happen at school level, IT environments vary (many are M365, some are Google Workspace, some run both), and teachers and administration teams face the same weekly administrative burden as public schools: converting existing documents into digital platforms one question at a time. Pirai AI converts any document to any platform in minutes.

Catholic Schools: Microsoft 365 Environment

Catholic Education Diocese of Sydney (CEDP), Diocese of Parramatta, Diocese of Broken Bay, and Diocese of Maitland-Newcastle provide Microsoft 365 Education to member schools. Teachers and administrators have Microsoft Forms, Teams, SharePoint, and OneDrive through their school M365 accounts.

Pirai AI converts Word documents, PDFs, and paper forms to Microsoft Forms Quick Import format for direct import into Microsoft Forms. The converted form integrates with Teams class assignments, the Teams gradebook, and SharePoint sites. For schools using both M365 and Google Workspace, Pirai AI exports to both platforms from a single document upload.

Independent Schools: Mixed Environments

AISNSW (Association of Independent Schools NSW) member schools range from large GPS (Great Public Schools) associations on the North Shore and Eastern Suburbs to smaller community independent schools in Western Sydney and regional NSW. Many run Google Workspace for Education, some run M365, and larger schools often run both. Pirai AI works with whichever platform the school uses:

  • Google Workspace schools: one-click Google Forms creation in your school Drive
  • M365 schools: Microsoft Forms Quick Import output for Teams assignment integration
  • Both environments: export to both platforms from a single upload in one session

Private School Use Cases

Academic operations: HSC trial examination marking rubrics and feedback forms. Preliminary course assessment tasks in Word converted to Microsoft Forms or Google Forms. Subject selection forms for Year 10 students. Academic counselling and learning support questionnaires.

School administration: Enrolment application supplementary information forms. Scholarship interest and application forms. International student intake questionnaires. Before and after school care registration forms. Uniform shop preference surveys. School fee payment plan preference forms.

Co-curricular and boarding: Sport selection and preference surveys for GPS and CAS competition. Boarding house wellbeing check-ins and leave request workflows converted to digital forms. Excursion, camp, and international travel consent and medical information forms.

Parent and community engagement: Parent satisfaction surveys for school councils. Strategic plan consultation questionnaires. Old Scholars Association event registration and feedback forms. P&F (Parents and Friends) fundraising preference surveys.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do Sydney Catholic schools using M365 work with Pirai AI?+

Yes. Pirai AI exports to Microsoft Forms for M365 schools in the Catholic Education Diocese of Sydney, Diocese of Parramatta, and other dioceses. Upload your Word or PDF document, select Microsoft Forms, download the Quick Import output, and import into Microsoft Forms. The form integrates with Teams class assignments and the Teams gradebook.

Can independent schools with both Google and M365 use Pirai AI for both platforms?+

Yes. From a single document upload in Pirai AI, you can export to both Google Forms (one-click creation in Drive) and Microsoft Forms (Quick Import output) in the same session. Schools running dual environments can produce both form versions without uploading the document twice.

Can Pirai AI help with enrolment and administrative forms for private schools?+

Yes. Enrolment supplementary forms, scholarship applications, international student intake questionnaires, and boarding house forms all convert from Word or PDF to Google Forms or Microsoft Forms using the same process as assessment conversion. Any structured document with questions or fields converts.

Does Pirai AI work for HSC and Preliminary course assessment tasks?+

Yes. HSC and Preliminary assessment tasks in Word or PDF convert to Google Forms or Microsoft Forms for digital delivery. For HSC extended response tasks, open text (paragraph) question types are created in the form. Multiple choice, short answer, and structured response questions all convert to their correct digital equivalents.
