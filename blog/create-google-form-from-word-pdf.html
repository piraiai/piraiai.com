Quick Answer

Google Forms cannot open Word or PDF files directly. To create a Google Form from an existing document: upload to platform.piraiai.com, select Google Forms, download the Apps Script, open a blank Google Form, three-dot menu > Script editor, paste and click Run. The form is created from your document in under 3 minutes. No Drive access granted to any third party.

Can Google Forms Open a Word or PDF File?

No. Google Forms has no file import button for external documents. The only supported import is questions from another Google Form in your Drive. Word files, PDFs, and Excel spreadsheets must either be manually recreated question by question, or processed through a third-party tool.

Method 1: Manual Creation in Google Forms

Open forms.google.com and your document side by side. Click + to add each question, select the type, paste the text, and add answer options one by one. Time: ~90 seconds per multiple choice question. A 30-question Word survey takes 45 minutes. A 60-question PDF instrument takes 90 minutes. Best when: under 10 simple questions.

Method 2: Convert to Google Doc + Add-On

For Word documents: upload to Google Drive, convert to Google Docs format, install a Workspace add-on (Automagical Forms, Form Builder for Docs), grant Drive access, run the add-on on the Doc to generate the Google Form.

Limitations: Requires granting Drive read access. Matrix questions and Likert batteries are typically dropped or converted to individual questions. The intermediate Google Doc step adds time and formatting errors.

Method 3: Pirai AI - Fastest for Complex Surveys

Upload your Word or PDF directly to Pirai AI without converting to Google Docs first. Pirai AI generates a Google Apps Script from the document. You paste and run it in a blank Google Form. No Drive access granted at any point.

What Often Gets Lost in Conversion

Matrix questions: unless you use Pirai AI, which converts to Multiple Choice Grid. Most tools drop these or flatten them. Scale labels: endpoint labels on Likert scales are often not captured by add-ons. Branching logic: skip logic from survey documents is not automatically converted - configure manually in the Google Forms editor after import. Question numbering: original numbers (Q1, Q2...) appear as part of question text unless removed before conversion - clean these in Pirai AI's Survey Editor before downloading.
