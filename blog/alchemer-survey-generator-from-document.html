Alchemer Survey Generator: Build Surveys From Any Document

Arun Raj•August 13, 2026•5 min read•Guide
Automated Alchemer Survey Generation

Alchemer has no built-in AI survey generator - it offers templates and a manual Survey Builder. Pirai AI fills this gap: upload any document containing survey questions and Pirai AI generates the complete Alchemer survey structure, formatted and ready to paste. The survey generator workflow replaces hours of manual question entry with a 10-15 minute automated process.

What Pirai AI Generates for Alchemer

From any uploaded document, Pirai AI generates:

  • Every question in the source document, with correct question type assigned
  • All answer options for each question, with correct ( ) or [ ] syntax
  • Open text fields with underscore markers
  • Page breaks between sections with ::NewPage:: markers
  • A complete .txt file ready to paste directly into Alchemer's Import from Word field

The generated survey is not a template with dummy content - it is your actual survey, with the exact questions and answer options from your source document, in the format Alchemer requires.

Document Sources for Alchemer Survey Generation

  • Word documents: Survey scripts, research instruments, questionnaire drafts
  • PDFs: Published instruments, client-provided questionnaires, scanned paper surveys
  • Excel files: Question banks with answer options in adjacent columns
  • Qualtrics QSF: Existing Qualtrics surveys migrated to Alchemer
  • Pasted text: ChatGPT-generated surveys, copied email content, any plain text question list

The Review Workspace Before Generation

Between document upload and Alchemer export, every survey goes through the Pirai AI review workspace:

  • Outline panel: Every extracted question listed with type and page assignment
  • Validation panel: Structural issues flagged before export - missing answer options, ambiguous question types, incomplete matrices
  • AI Survey Editor: Make conversational changes - "add a prefer not to say option to Q3" or "split Q7 into two questions"
  • Live preview: See exactly how the survey will look to respondents before generating the Alchemer output

Who Uses Pirai AI as an Alchemer Survey Generator

MR agencies: Client Word scripts converted to Alchemer for programming - replacing the manual build phase that typically takes the majority of survey setup time.

CX teams: Standardised measurement instruments (NPS, CSAT, CES) maintained as Word documents, generated to Alchemer each survey cycle.

In-house insights teams: Recurring employee engagement and pulse surveys maintained as Excel question banks, generated to Alchemer at each wave launch.

Consultancies: Client-facing questionnaire scripts in PDF converted to Alchemer without client seeing the manual programming step.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there an AI survey generator for Alchemer?+

Alchemer does not have a built-in AI survey generator. Pirai AI acts as an Alchemer survey generator - upload any document (Word, PDF, Excel, QSF, or pasted text), and Pirai AI generates the complete Alchemer Word Import syntax ready to paste. No manual question entry required.

Can I generate an Alchemer survey from a ChatGPT output?+

Yes. Copy the ChatGPT survey output and paste it into the Pirai AI editor. Pirai AI converts it to Alchemer Word Import syntax. Download the .txt file and paste into Alchemer via Create Survey > Import from Word > Start Building.

How accurate is the Alchemer survey generation?+

Pirai AI preserves verbatim text from the source document - question wording and answer options are not paraphrased or rewritten. Question type detection (radio vs checkbox vs open text) is AI-driven with a validation step in the workspace where you can correct any misclassified questions before export.

Can I generate an Alchemer survey from a Qualtrics file?+

Yes. Upload the Qualtrics QSF file to Pirai AI, select Alchemer as the output, and download the .txt. Paste into Alchemer via Import from Word. Question text and answer options transfer. Alchemer display logic must be configured after import.
