Assessment Creator Tool for Australian Teachers: Build Digital Quizzes in Minutes

Digital Assessment Creation for Australian Teachers

The most time-consuming part of digital assessment for Australian teachers is not writing the questions - it is getting those questions into the platform. A 20-question reading comprehension assessment written in Word takes 30-45 minutes to rebuild in Google Forms. Pirai AI eliminates that rebuild: upload the Word document, and the assessment is in Google Forms in under 5 minutes. The same document also exports to Kahoot for live review and Moodle for LMS deployment - from a single upload.

What Pirai AI Does for Australian Teachers

Pirai AI is a document-to-platform converter for survey and assessment tools. For Australian teachers, the practical use is converting existing assessment documents - built in Word, generated by AI, or sourced from PDFs - to the digital platforms already in use at their school:

  • Google Forms (Google Workspace for Education schools) - one-click creation in your Drive
  • Microsoft Forms (M365 Catholic and independent schools) - Quick Import output
  • Kahoot - competitive quiz game for live class review
  • Moodle - AIKEN format for TAFE and senior secondary LMS
  • Qualtrics - for senior secondary research projects or school-wide survey programmes

Australian Curriculum Assessment Types

Pirai AI handles all common assessment formats used in the Australian Curriculum (AC 9.0):

  • Formative assessment: Exit tickets, weekly quizzes, comprehension checks - converted to Google Forms for immediate digital delivery and marking
  • Summative assessment: Unit tests and end-of-term assessments in Word or PDF - converted to Google Forms or Moodle for structured digital delivery
  • Diagnostic assessment: Beginning-of-year or beginning-of-unit diagnostic questions - converted to Google Forms for quick whole-class data
  • NAPLAN preparation: Practice questions in NAPLAN style converted to Google Forms for digital format familiarisation

Working With AI-Generated Assessment Content

Many Australian teachers now use ChatGPT, Gemini, or Copilot to draft assessment questions. The problem: the AI output is in plain text - it still needs to be manually entered into Google Forms or Kahoot question by question. Pirai AI solves this. Copy the AI-generated questions and paste them directly into the Pirai AI editor. Select your target platform. The questions are converted instantly - no manual reformatting of the AI output.
