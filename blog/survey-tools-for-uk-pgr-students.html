Survey Tools for UK PGR Students: Qualtrics, REDCap, and REC Approval

Arun Raj•August 05, 2026•5 min read•Guide
For UK PGR Students

Postgraduate Research (PGR) students - PhD and MRes candidates - are among the most frequent survey researchers at UK universities, but typically receive the least platform support. You have access to your institution's Qualtrics licence and, for clinical research, potentially REDCap. Getting your survey instrument from a Word document into the right platform quickly and accurately is where most PGR students lose time they cannot spare.

REC Approval - The Essential First Step

All research involving human participants at UK universities requires Research Ethics Committee (REC) approval before you recruit or collect data. Most universities have a Faculty or School REC for standard social science and humanities surveys. For research involving NHS patients, NHS staff, or NHS data, you also need HRA (Health Research Authority) approval - a separate process that typically takes 60-90 days.

In your REC application, specify your survey platform, data storage location, and data handling arrangements. Institutional Qualtrics (US cloud, covered by institutional Data Processing Agreement) and institutional REDCap (UK servers) are both straightforward to justify in a REC application. Using a personal Google account for research collecting personal information requires additional justification.

Getting Qualtrics Access as a PGR Student

Most UK universities provide Qualtrics access to PGR students via institutional SSO. Some institutions require your supervisor to submit an access request on your behalf. Contact your library, IT services, or digital research support team to find your institution's process. At many Russell Group universities, PGR students log in directly at [youruni].qualtrics.com - but may need to request survey creation rights separately from their supervisor.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do UK PGR students need REC approval for surveys?+

Yes. All research involving human participants requires Research Ethics Committee (REC) approval before data collection begins. For research involving NHS patients, staff, or data, additional HRA (Health Research Authority) approval is required in England. Most university social science and education surveys go through a Faculty or School REC with typical approval times of 4-8 weeks.

How do UK PGR students access Qualtrics?+

Most UK universities provide Qualtrics via institutional Single Sign-On at [youruni].qualtrics.com. Some institutions require your supervisor to request survey creation rights on your behalf. Contact your library, IT services, or digital research support team if your account does not have creation rights after login.

Should a UK PhD student use Qualtrics or REDCap?+

Use Qualtrics for social science, psychology, education, and humanities surveys. Use REDCap if your research involves NHS data or sensitive health information, or if your REC/HRA protocol specifies it. REDCap access for PGR students is typically tied to an approved ethics protocol at UK university medical schools.
