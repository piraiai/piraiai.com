Excel to SurveySparrow: Convert a Spreadsheet Question Bank

Arun Raj•August 13, 2026•5 min read•Guide
Excel to SurveySparrow

SurveySparrow has no Excel import for questions. Pirai AI converts your .xlsx question bank to formatted .txt file for direct SurveySparrow import - no manual rebuilding, no transcription risk.

Excel Structures Pirai AI Reads

  • Question + answer columns: Column A = question text, Columns B-F = answer options
  • Type-flagged columns: Question type column (MC, OT, Matrix) used to assign the correct SurveySparrow question type
  • Simple numbered lists: Q1, Q2 with options in subsequent rows or columns
  • Survey scripts with coding: Multiple columns including question text, options, and recode values

Pirai AI reads Sheet 1 of the .xlsx by default. Move questions to Sheet 1 before uploading if they are on another sheet.

Who Converts Excel to SurveySparrow

  • CX teams converting NPS and CSAT question banks from Excel to SurveySparrow for conversational surveys
  • Sales teams converting win/loss and pipeline question lists from Excel to SurveySparrow
  • HR teams converting employee pulse survey questions from Excel to SurveySparrow
  • Marketing teams converting market research questionnaires from Excel to SurveySparrow

Excel to SurveySparrow: Steps

1. Upload your .xlsx at platform.piraiai.com.

2. Review extracted questions - confirm types and answer options.

3. Select SurveySparrow. Download the formatted .txt file.

4. Import into SurveySparrow: SurveySparrow > Create Survey > paste formatted content into the bulk question builder field.

5. Complete setup: review auto-detected question types - surveysparrow detects types from the pasted content but confirm each.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I import Excel into SurveySparrow?+

SurveySparrow has no Excel import for questions. Use Pirai AI to convert your .xlsx to formatted .txt file, then import via SurveySparrow.

What Excel layout works best for SurveySparrow conversion?+

Column A = question text, Columns B onward = answer options, one question per row. Also handles type-flagged columns and survey script layouts. All questions on Sheet 1.

Can I maintain questions in Excel and convert to SurveySparrow each wave?+

Yes. This is a common workflow. Maintain the question bank in Excel for version control, upload to Pirai AI at each launch, convert to SurveySparrow format, and import. The Excel file remains the approved source.

Does Pirai AI work with Google Sheets exported to Excel for SurveySparrow?+

Yes. Export your Google Sheet as .xlsx (File > Download > Microsoft Excel). Upload to Pirai AI, select SurveySparrow, and download the formatted .txt file for import.
