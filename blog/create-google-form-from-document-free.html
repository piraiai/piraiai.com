Steps to Create a Google Form From a Document for Free

1. Go to platform.piraiai.com - no account needed to start.

2. Upload your Word, PDF, or Excel file.

3. Review the extracted questions in the workspace. Make any adjustments.

4. Click Convert to Google Forms.

5. Sign in to your Google account and click Allow (standard Google permission).

6. Your Google Form is created in your Drive. Click the link to open it.

Creating a Free Google Forms Quiz From a Document

After your form is created in Drive, enable Quiz mode: open the form, go to Settings > Quizzes > toggle Make this a quiz on. Set correct answers and point values on each question. Google automatically calculates scores and can show results to respondents immediately or after manual review. The quiz form is created from your document for free via Pirai AI, and Quiz mode is free within Google Forms itself.
