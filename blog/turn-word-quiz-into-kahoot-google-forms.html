Turn a Word Quiz Into Kahoot and Google Forms: One Upload, Two Platforms

Arun Raj•August 25, 2026•5 min read•Guide
One Quiz, Two Platforms

Australian teachers use Kahoot for live whole-class review games and Google Forms for individual self-marking assessments. They are different tools for different purposes - but they start from the same questions. Most teachers build each separately, doubling the setup time. Pirai AI converts the same Word or PDF quiz to both Kahoot format and Google Forms from a single upload in one session. Write the quiz once, deploy it two ways.

When to Use Kahoot vs Google Forms

Use CaseBest Platform
Live whole-class review - students competing on screenKahoot - projected leaderboard, engagement
Individual self-paced assessment with marking recordGoogle Forms - responses stored, marks auto-calculated
End-of-unit revision - fun engagementKahoot first, then Google Forms for formal record
Homework quiz - students complete independentlyGoogle Forms - works on any device, responses tracked
Quick vocabulary checkKahoot (fast, in-class) or Google Forms (take-home)

One Upload, Two Exports

1. Upload your Word or PDF quiz at platform.piraiai.com.

2. Review extracted questions in the workspace. Confirm question types and answer options before exporting to either platform.

3. Export to Google Forms first. Click Export to Google Form, sign in with your school Google account, and the form is created in your Drive. Enable Quiz mode (Settings > Quizzes > Make this a quiz) and set correct answers for each question.

4. Return to Pirai AI and export to Kahoot. Select Kahoot and download the .xlsx spreadsheet. Upload it at kahoot.com via Create > Import spreadsheet. Your Kahoot game is ready with all the same questions.

Total time from Word document to both platforms: under 15 minutes. Building each separately in their respective editors would take 60-90 minutes.

Kahoot Technical Limits to Know

Kahoot has character limits on questions and answers that Pirai AI flags in the workspace before export:

  • Question text: 95 characters maximum
  • Each answer option: 60 characters maximum
  • Maximum 4 answer options per question
  • Only multiple choice format (no open text or matrix questions)

For long questions, Pirai AI flags them in the review workspace. Edit question text down before exporting to Kahoot - the Google Forms version retains the full-length question text.

Classroom Workflow: Monday to Friday

A practical workflow for Australian teachers:

Monday: Upload the week's vocabulary or topic quiz from your planning document. Export to both Google Forms (for individual assessment) and Kahoot (for Friday's review). Both are ready for the week.

Wednesday: Distribute the Google Form via Google Classroom as a mid-week formative check. Students complete individually. You review responses in Google Sheets to identify who needs support.

Friday: Launch the Kahoot game for whole-class review. Students already know the content from Wednesday's Google Form practice. Higher engagement, better performance, clearer teaching data.

What question types work in both Kahoot and Google Forms?+

Multiple choice questions with up to 4 options work in both Kahoot and Google Forms. Google Forms also supports open text, paragraph, rating scale, and matrix grid questions that Kahoot does not support. Pirai AI creates the appropriate question types for each platform from the same source document.
