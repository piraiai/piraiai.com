QuestionPro vs Qualtrics: Feature Comparison for Research and Agencies

Arun Raj•September 1, 2026•6 min read•Guide
QuestionPro vs Qualtrics

QuestionPro and Qualtrics are the two most fully-featured survey platforms for professional research. Both offer advanced question types, analytics, and panel access. The differences matter: Qualtrics is dominant at universities through institutional licences and excels at complex experimental survey design; QuestionPro has a stronger offline capability, a competitive academic pricing angle, and a direct integration with Pirai AI as an official partner. This guide helps you choose.

Choose QuestionPro When

  • Your institution does not have a Qualtrics licence and you need advanced survey capabilities
  • Your research involves offline field data collection - in-store, clinical, or remote settings
  • You want built-in cross-tabulation and statistical significance testing without exporting to SPSS
  • You prefer direct Pirai AI integration - surveys created in QuestionPro automatically from any document
  • You need a research edition without institutional Qualtrics access costs

Choose Qualtrics When

  • Your institution provides Qualtrics via an institutional licence - use what is already available
  • Your study requires complex Survey Flow: block randomisation, counter-balancing, advanced embedded data piping
  • You need Qualtrics-specific advanced types: Heat Map, Hot Spot, Timing questions
  • You want the most widely deployed research platform for cross-institutional study replication
  • Your team uses Qualtrics XM Platform integrations across multiple research and CX functions

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I use QuestionPro or Qualtrics for research?+

If your institution has a Qualtrics licence, use Qualtrics - you already have access. If you need a research-grade platform without institutional Qualtrics access, QuestionPro's Research edition is a strong alternative with MaxDiff, conjoint, offline surveys, and built-in cross-tabulation. QuestionPro also integrates directly with Pirai AI as an official partner.

Is QuestionPro as good as Qualtrics?+

For most research survey use cases - standard question types, Likert scales, Matrix questions, skip logic, quotas, panel access - both platforms are comparable. Qualtrics has stronger Survey Flow for complex experimental designs. QuestionPro has stronger offline capability and competitive pricing for researchers without institutional Qualtrics access.

Does QuestionPro have a free plan unlike Qualtrics?+

Yes. QuestionPro has a free plan with unlimited surveys and basic features. Qualtrics has no free tier - access is through institutional licences or paid plans. For researchers without institutional Qualtrics access, QuestionPro's free and paid plans offer a research-grade alternative.

Can I use Pirai AI with both QuestionPro and Qualtrics?+

Yes. Pirai AI is an official QuestionPro partner - it creates surveys directly in your QuestionPro account from any document. For Qualtrics, Pirai AI generates a QSF file for direct import. From a single document upload you can produce output for both platforms in one session.
