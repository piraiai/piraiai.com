Word to QuestionPro: Convert a Word Document to a QuestionPro Survey

Arun Raj•September 1, 2026•6 min read•Guide
Word to QuestionPro

Most survey instruments start in Word - drafted for client review, team sign-off, or ethics committee approval. Getting that Word document into QuestionPro has historically meant re-entering every question manually. As an official QuestionPro partner, Pirai AI eliminates this step: upload the Word document, review extracted questions in the workspace, and the survey is created directly in your QuestionPro account with all questions, question types, and answer options in place.

Word to QuestionPro: Steps

1. Upload your Word document at platform.piraiai.com. Pirai AI reads the document and extracts every question, answer option, section heading, and instruction text.

2. Review extracted questions. The workspace displays every question with its detected QuestionPro question type. Confirm that Single Choice questions are correctly identified, Matrix questions have their row items and column scale options, and open-ended questions are set to Open Text. Edit any question or type if needed.

3. Select QuestionPro. Connect your QuestionPro account if this is your first conversion. Pirai AI creates the survey directly in your account.

4. Open in QuestionPro. Find the newly created survey in your QuestionPro survey list. All questions are in place. Configure skip logic, display conditions, quotas, theme, and distribution.

For Market Research Agencies

Market research agencies frequently receive client questionnaire scripts as Word documents. Historically, programming these into QuestionPro (or any survey tool) required a dedicated survey programmer spending 4-6 hours per questionnaire on data entry.
