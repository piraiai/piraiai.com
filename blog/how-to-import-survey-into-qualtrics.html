How to Import a Survey Into Qualtrics: QSF File Method

Arun Raj•September 1, 2026•6 min read•Guide
Importing Into Qualtrics

Qualtrics does not accept Word, PDF, or Excel files directly. To import a survey from any document, you must first convert the document to a QSF (Qualtrics Survey Format) file, then import the QSF via Qualtrics Tools > Import/Export > Import Survey. This guide covers the full import workflow - from generating the QSF to completing post-import configuration.

Step 1 - Generate the QSF File

From a Word, PDF, or Excel document using Pirai AI:

1. Go to platform.piraiai.com. Upload your document - Word (.docx), PDF (native or scanned), or Excel (.xlsx). For scanned PDFs, enable Advanced PDF Processing.

2. Review extracted questions in the workspace. Verify question types (Multiple Choice, Matrix, Text Entry, Slider, etc.) and answer options before generating the QSF.

3. Select Qualtrics and download the .qsf file.

From an existing Qualtrics survey: Open the survey > Tools > Import/Export > Export Survey > Qualtrics Survey Format (.qsf). Use this to back up or duplicate surveys across accounts.

Step 2 - Import Into Qualtrics

Method 1 - From the Survey Builder:

1. Open any existing Qualtrics survey in the Survey Builder.

2. Click the Tools icon (gear) in the top right corner of the Survey Builder.

3. Go to Import/Export > Import Survey.

4. Click "Choose file" or drag your .qsf file.

5. Click Import. Qualtrics replaces the current survey content with the imported QSF, or creates a new survey depending on your Qualtrics version.

Method 2 - From the Projects list:

1. On the Qualtrics homepage, click Create a new project.

2. Select "Survey" as the project type.

3. Choose "From a file" or "Import a QSF" from the creation options.

4. Upload your .qsf file and complete project creation.

Step 3 - Post-Import Configuration

After the QSF import creates all questions and blocks, complete these settings in the Survey Builder:

  • Display Logic: Add conditional question display based on previous answers - click the gear icon on any question > Add Display Logic
  • Skip Logic: Jump respondents to later questions based on answers - gear icon > Add Skip Logic
  • Survey Flow: Configure block randomisation, embedded data, branches, and end-of-survey actions in the Survey Flow panel
  • Embedded Data: Add URL parameters and custom variables in Survey Flow > Embedded Data
  • Quotas: Set response limits by criteria in Survey Options > Quotas
  • Look and Feel: Apply survey theme, progress bar, and styling in Look and Feel

Common Import Issues

IssueCause and Fix
Import button not visibleSome Qualtrics account types require navigating to Tools inside the Survey Builder. Create a blank survey first, then import via Tools > Import/Export > Import Survey.
Questions import but display incorrectlyCheck question types in the Survey Builder after import. Some complex types (Drill Down, Heat Map) require manual configuration after QSF import.
Survey Flow is empty after importThe imported QSF may not have included Survey Flow. Rebuild Survey Flow manually - all questions and blocks are present, only the flow logic needs configuration.
File size errorVery large QSF files (100+ questions with complex logic) may exceed upload limits. Split into multiple surveys or contact your Qualtrics admin.

Getting Qualtrics Access at Your Institution

Most universities provide Qualtrics via institutional SSO at [youruni].qualtrics.com or qualtrics.com with your institutional credentials. Contact your library, IT services, or research computing team if you are unsure how to access your institution's Qualtrics licence. Graduate students may need their supervisor or PI to request survey creation access.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I import a Word document into Qualtrics?+

Qualtrics does not accept Word document imports directly. Convert your Word document to a QSF file using Pirai AI at platform.piraiai.com. Select Qualtrics and download the QSF. Then in Qualtrics, open a survey and go to Tools > Import/Export > Import Survey, select your QSF file, and click Import. All questions are created instantly.

Where is the Import Survey option in Qualtrics?+

In the Survey Builder, click the Tools icon (gear) in the top right. Select Import/Export > Import Survey. Alternatively, on the Qualtrics projects homepage, click Create a new project > Survey > From a file. Both paths accept QSF files.

How long does it take to import a survey into Qualtrics using a QSF file?+

The QSF import itself takes under 30 seconds in Qualtrics. Generating the QSF from a Word or PDF document using Pirai AI takes 15-20 minutes for a 40-question survey (including upload, review, and download). Total time from document to Qualtrics: under 25 minutes versus 4-6 hours of manual entry.

Can I import a PDF survey into Qualtrics?+

Not directly - Qualtrics only accepts QSF files. Upload your PDF to Pirai AI at platform.piraiai.com (enable Advanced PDF Processing for scanned PDFs). Select Qualtrics and download the QSF. Import the QSF into Qualtrics via Tools > Import/Export > Import Survey.
