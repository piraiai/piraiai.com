Convert a Worksheet or Assessment to Google Forms: Guide for Australian Teachers

Arun Raj•August 25, 2026•5 min read•Guide
Worksheet to Google Forms

Australian primary and secondary teachers spend a significant portion of their planning time rebuilding existing assessments in Google Forms - copying questions from Word documents or PDFs one by one into the form builder. Pirai AI converts your existing worksheet or assessment directly to a Google Form in one click. Upload your document, click Export to Google Form, sign in with your school Google account, and the form appears in your Drive. Every question is in the right place with the right type - no manual entry.

What Converts to Google Forms

  • Word documents (.docx): Unit tests, weekly quizzes, reading comprehension activities, spelling checks, exit tickets - any assessment you have drafted in Word
  • PDF worksheets: Published worksheets, textbook activities, school-made PDF assessments - both digital PDFs and scanned paper documents
  • AI-generated questions: Questions drafted in ChatGPT or Gemini pasted directly into Pirai AI
  • Excel question banks: Year-level question banks maintained in spreadsheets with answer options in adjacent columns

Converting a Worksheet to Google Forms: The Steps

1. Upload your document at platform.piraiai.com. For scanned paper worksheets, enable Advanced PDF Processing for OCR extraction.

2. Review your questions in the workspace. Every extracted question appears with its detected type. Check that multiple choice options are complete and that open-ended questions are set to the correct type.

3. Click Export to Google Form. A Google sign-in screen appears.

4. Sign in with your school Google Workspace account and click Allow. Pirai AI creates the form directly in your Drive.

5. Open the form in Google Forms. Share the link via Google Classroom or as a QR code.

Enable Self-Marking Quiz Mode

After the Google Form is created in your Drive, enable Quiz mode for automatic marking: open the form, go to Settings > Quizzes > Make this a quiz. Set correct answers and point values for each question. Students see their score immediately after submission. Results appear in Google Sheets with one click on the Responses tab. Perfect for formative assessment, practice tests, and exit tickets where immediate feedback matters.

Distributing to Students

Share the form via Google Classroom (Add > Assignment > Google Forms), paste the link in an email or Teams message, print a QR code pointing to the form link for paper distribution, or embed it on a class website or Seesaw activity. Student responses are visible in real time on the Responses tab and exportable to Google Sheets.

Also Convert to Kahoot From the Same Upload

After creating the Google Form for individual assessment, stay in Pirai AI and also export the same document to Kahoot for a live class game. Same questions, two formats - individual self-marking assessment and live whole-class review - from one document upload.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Australian teachers use their school Google account with Pirai AI?+

Yes. Pirai AI creates Google Forms in your Drive using standard Google authorisation. Sign in with your school Google Workspace for Education account when prompted. The form is created in your school Drive - share via Google Classroom, QR code, or link.

Can I convert a scanned paper worksheet to Google Forms?+

Yes. Enable Advanced PDF Processing before uploading your scanned worksheet PDF. Pirai AI runs OCR to extract question text from the scanned image and creates the Google Form in your Drive. Works well for clean black-and-white printed worksheets.

How do I make the Google Form automatically mark student responses?+

After Pirai AI creates the form in your Drive, open it in Google Forms and go to Settings > Quizzes > Make this a quiz. Set the correct answer and point value for each question. Students see their score immediately after submission.

Can I convert the same worksheet to both Google Forms and Kahoot?+

Yes. From the same document upload in Pirai AI, export to Google Forms (one-click creation in Drive) and download the Kahoot format file in the same session. Use the Google Form for individual student assessment and the Kahoot for a live whole-class review game.
