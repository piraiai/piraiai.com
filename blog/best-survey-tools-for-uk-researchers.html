Best Survey Tools for UK Researchers in 2026

Arun Raj•August 05, 2026•5 min read•Guide
The UK Research Context

UK researchers typically access two or three survey platforms through their institution - most commonly Qualtrics for general research surveys and REDCap for sensitive or clinical data in NHS-linked studies. Since Brexit, UK GDPR (retained EU law under the Data Protection Act 2018) governs personal data handling, with the ICO (Information Commissioner's Office) as the national regulator. Qualtrics - The Standard Research Survey Platform

Qualtrics is the dominant research survey platform across UK universities including the Russell Group (Oxford, Cambridge, Imperial, UCL, Edinburgh, Manchester, and 18 others). Institutional licences are managed by library or IT services and accessed via Single Sign-On. Most UK institutions restrict Qualtrics to academic research purposes - operational surveys must use alternative platforms.

Best for: ESRC, AHRC, and EPSRC-funded social science and humanities research. Complex surveys requiring display logic, block randomisation, and SPSS export. Online panels via Qualtrics Audience for quantitative studies.

REDCap - For NHS and Clinical Research

REDCap is the standard electronic data capture platform for NHS-linked and NIHR-funded clinical research in the UK. Health Research Authority (HRA) approval is required for research involving NHS patients, staff, or data. UK university medical schools and NHS Trusts commonly operate REDCap instances. ICH GCP compliance requirements for clinical trials are satisfied by REDCap's field-level audit trail.

Best for: MRC and Wellcome Trust-funded clinical research. NIHR-funded studies. NHS patient and staff surveys requiring HRA approval. Clinical trials requiring GCP-aligned audit trails.

LimeSurvey - GDPR-Compliant Self-Hosting

LimeSurvey's German headquarters and GDPR-native design make it popular at UK universities prioritising data sovereignty post-Brexit. Self-hosted instances keep all data on UK institutional servers, avoiding ICO questions about international data transfers. Strong multilingual support relevant for UK research with international participant cohorts.

Best for: Research requiring UK data residency. Institutions with IT capacity for self-hosting. Multilingual surveys. Qualitative researchers needing open-ended question depth.

Microsoft Forms and Google Forms

Both are widely used in UK higher education for teaching, student feedback, and low-risk surveys. UK universities standardised on M365 use Microsoft Forms for internal surveys. Google Workspace institutions use Google Forms. Under UK GDPR, surveys collecting personal data must disclose where data is stored - both platforms use US-based infrastructure by default, which requires consideration under UK GDPR's international transfer provisions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which survey platform do UK universities use for research?+

Most UK universities provide Qualtrics for general academic research and REDCap for clinical or sensitive data. Microsoft Forms and Google Forms are used for teaching and low-risk surveys. The platform choice is often determined by your REC protocol or funding body - NIHR and MRC-funded clinical research frequently uses REDCap.

Do UK researchers need REC approval to run a survey?+

Yes. All research involving human participants at UK universities requires Research Ethics Committee (REC) approval. For research involving NHS patients, staff, or data, additional HRA (Health Research Authority) approval is required in England. Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland have separate equivalent processes.

How does UK GDPR affect survey platform choice?+

UK GDPR (the retained EU GDPR under the Data Protection Act 2018) requires that transfers of personal data to countries outside the UK/EEA have adequate protections. US-based platforms like Qualtrics and Google Forms use Standard Contractual Clauses for UK-to-US transfers. For surveys collecting sensitive personal data, a self-hosted option like REDCap or LimeSurvey may be preferable.
