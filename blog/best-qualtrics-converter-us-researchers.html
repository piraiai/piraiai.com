Qualtrics Conversion for US Research

Qualtrics is the dominant research survey platform at US universities - virtually every AAU institution has an institutional licence. The bottleneck is not access; it is the time required to rebuild an IRB-approved Word instrument in the Qualtrics Survey Builder. A 40-item validated scale takes 4-6 hours to build manually with consistent risk of transcription errors. Pirai AI converts the Word instrument to a Qualtrics QSF file in under 15 minutes, with verbatim text from the IRB-approved document preserved in every question label.

US Research Use Cases

  • NSF-funded psychology and social science: Validated instruments (Big Five, PCL-5, Beck Depression Inventory) converted from published PDFs to Qualtrics QSF for online study deployment
  • NIH-funded population health surveys: Patient experience and quality of life scales (PROMIS, SF-36, EQ-5D) converted from sponsor PDFs to Qualtrics for non-clinical CTSA-affiliated studies
  • Dissertation research: Graduate student survey instruments drafted in Word for IRB submission converted to Qualtrics after IRB approval
  • MR agency programming: Client Word questionnaire scripts converted to Qualtrics QSF, replacing the manual programming phase for US survey research firms
  • Multi-site studies: The same instrument converted to Qualtrics QSF and distributed to multiple consortium sites for consistent deployment across institutions

Why Verbatim Text Matters for US Research

IRB-approved protocols in the US specify the exact wording of survey instruments. Any deviation from the approved wording constitutes a protocol modification requiring IRB amendment. Pirai AI extracts verbatim text from the source document without paraphrasing. The Qualtrics question text matches the IRB-approved Word instrument exactly. Any changes are made in the Pirai AI workspace before the QSF is generated - with the source document text visible alongside the extracted version for direct comparison.

Getting Qualtrics Access at US Universities

Most US universities with Qualtrics institutional licences access the platform via SSO at [youruni].qualtrics.com. Graduate students typically need their supervisor or PI to request survey creation rights. Contact your library, IT services, or research computing team. Some institutions (particularly those where Qualtrics is managed by the library) provide self-service access with a research purpose declaration.
