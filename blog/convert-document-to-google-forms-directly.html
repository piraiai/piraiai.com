How Pirai AI Creates Google Forms Directly in Your Drive

Pirai AI uses Google's official Forms API with OAuth 2.0 authentication. When you click Export to Google Forms, you authorise Pirai AI to create a new form in your Drive. The form is built server-side using the Google Forms API and appears in your Drive instantly - the same way a native Google product would create it.

1
Upload your document Go to platform.piraiai.com. Upload your Word (.docx), PDF, Excel (.xlsx), or CSV file. For scanned paper surveys, enable Advanced Processing for OCR. Select Google Forms as the output.
2
Click Export to Google Forms Pirai AI extracts all questions, answer options, and question types from your document. Click Export to Google Forms. You are shown Google's permission screen to authorise.
3
Authorise with Google Sign in with your Google account if prompted. Grant Pirai AI scoped access: drive.file (to create a new form in your Drive) and forms.body (to add questions to the form). These are the minimum scopes required by Google's Forms API.
4
Your Google Form is in your Drive Pirai AI creates the native Google Form directly in your Google Drive. A direct edit link is returned. Open it, configure any display logic or settings, and share. The entire process takes under a minute.
About the permissions: The drive.file scope gives Pirai AI access only to files it creates on your behalf - not to any existing files, folders, or documents in your Drive. The forms.body scope is required by the Google Forms API to add questions to a form. Pirai AI does not read, store, or process any other content in your Google account. You can revoke access at any time from your Google Account settings.
