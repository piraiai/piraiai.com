Pirai AI for NSW Teachers: Save 2-3 Hours of Admin Time Every Week

Arun Raj•August 26, 2026•5 min read•Guide
For NSW Classroom Teachers

NSW teachers face some of the highest administrative workloads in the Australian state system. Between NESA compliance documentation, Sentral data entry, Google Classroom management, parent communication, and marking, the time available for the actual preparation that improves student outcomes keeps shrinking. Pirai AI targets a specific slice of that administrative burden: the time spent rebuilding existing documents in Google Forms. A unit assessment that already exists in Word should not need to be re-entered question by question into Google Forms. It takes under 5 minutes with Pirai AI.

Across All KLAs

English: Reading comprehension task PDFs converted to Google Forms for self-marking practice. Close reading activities from Word converted for digital completion. HSC essay structure and analytical response rubrics converted to Google Forms for peer and self-assessment.

Mathematics: Weekly problem sets and skill check quizzes from Word converted to Google Forms. Stage 4 and 5 topic test papers converted to Google Forms for mid-topic diagnostic data. Year 12 Extension practice questions for self-paced digital revision.

Science: Practical investigation observation and data recording forms from Word converted to Google Forms. Theory knowledge check quizzes before and after practical lessons. HSC depth study survey instruments converted for student research projects.

HSIE (Human Society and Its Environment): Geography fieldwork data collection forms for local area studies. History source analysis response sheets converted to Google Forms for structured student responses. Stage 5 community research survey instruments for students designing their own studies.

PDHPE: Wellbeing and health habit check-ins converted from Word to anonymous Google Forms. Fitness assessment recording forms. Stage 4 and 5 health literacy quiz instruments for the mandatory component.

Creative Arts, TAS, Languages: Peer feedback and self-assessment rubrics converted to Google Forms. Portfolio reflection prompts. Language learning vocabulary and comprehension checks.

A NSW Teacher's Weekly Workflow With Pirai AI

Sunday planning: Draft or locate assessment materials in Word or PDF during lesson planning. Upload to Pirai AI. Export to Google Forms for the week's formative assessment and to Kahoot for Friday's revision game. Both ready before Monday.

Monday morning: Assign the Google Form via Google Classroom. Students complete during class or as homework. Responses visible in Google Sheets in real time.

Wednesday: Review Google Sheets data. Identify students needing support before end of week. Adjust teaching accordingly.

Friday: Launch the Kahoot game built from the same document. Students review content in a structured, engaging format.

NSW Administrative Compliance Documents

Beyond classroom assessment, NSW teachers manage recurring administrative documents that Pirai AI converts digitally:

  • Excursion risk assessment and consent: Convert the school's standard Word excursion form to Google Forms - parent responses feed into a Google Sheet for the organising teacher
  • Parent-teacher interview booking notes: Post-interview feedback and action items captured via Google Forms rather than paper
  • Student learning plan progress notes: Structured progress update forms for Learning and Support Teams
  • Transition documentation: Stage transition information forms for students moving from K to Stage 1, Stage 3 to Stage 4, or Stage 5 to Stage 6

Frequently Asked Questions

How much admin time does Pirai AI save NSW teachers per week?+

Teachers who regularly create Google Forms for assessments, exit tickets, excursion forms, and parent surveys typically save 2-3 hours of administrative time per week. A 20-question assessment that takes 30-45 minutes to enter manually into Google Forms takes under 5 minutes with Pirai AI. Teachers who also create Kahoot games save an additional 30-45 minutes per quiz.

Which KLAs benefit most from Pirai AI in NSW schools?+

Every KLA benefits - English, Maths, Science, HSIE, PDHPE, Creative Arts, TAS, and Languages all generate assessment documents that convert to Google Forms. Teachers who create the most Google Forms (regular quizzes, exit tickets, parent forms) save the most time. Science and PDHPE teachers running student research surveys also benefit from conversion to Qualtrics or Google Forms.

Can NSW casual and relief teachers use Pirai AI?+

Yes. Pirai AI works with any Google account - school accounts, personal Gmail, and casual staff accounts. Free daily credits require no account registration to start. Casual teachers can upload a lesson's assessment material and have a Google Form ready in minutes.

Does Pirai AI work with NSW DoE Google Workspace accounts?+

Yes. Pirai AI creates Google Forms in your Drive using standard Google authorisation. Sign in with your @det.nsw.edu.au or @education.nsw.gov.au account when prompted. The form is created in your NSW DoE Google Drive under your school's data governance arrangements.
