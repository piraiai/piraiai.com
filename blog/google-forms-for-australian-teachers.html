Google Forms for Australian Teachers and Educators in 2026

Arun Raj•August 04, 2026•5 min read•Guide
Google Forms in Australian Education

Google Forms is one of the most widely used tools in Australian schools and universities for quizzes, course evaluations, and student surveys. It is free with any Google account, works on all devices, and integrates with Google Classroom. For Australian teachers, the key considerations are privacy (student data going to US servers), institutional platform choice (M365 vs Google Workspace), and how to convert existing Word or PDF quiz documents to Google Forms without retyping everything.

Google Workspace vs M365 in Australian Schools

Australian schools and TAFEs are split between Google Workspace for Education and Microsoft 365 Education - and your institution's platform determines which forms tool you should use:

Your Institution UsesBest Forms Tool
Google Workspace for EducationGoogle Forms - integrates with Classroom, Drive, Sheets
Microsoft 365 EducationMicrosoft Forms - integrates with Teams, SharePoint, gradebook
Both (some state government schools)Use whichever integrates better with your specific class workflows

For game-based review sessions alongside either forms tool, Kahoot is the most popular choice in Australian classrooms. Pirai AI converts the same quiz document to Google Forms, Microsoft Forms, and Kahoot from a single upload.

Privacy Considerations for Australian Student Data

Australian schools have obligations under the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles when collecting student information. Key considerations for using Google Forms with Australian students:

  • Anonymous surveys: If no student names or identifiers are collected, Google Forms is generally low-risk for anonymous feedback and polls.
  • Identifiable student data: Google Forms stores data on US-based servers. For surveys collecting identifiable student information, check with your school's privacy coordinator or IT department whether Google Forms is approved for this use in your school system.
  • State and territory guidelines: Education departments in Victoria, NSW, Queensland, and other states have issued guidance on the use of cloud services for student data - some have specific approved platforms lists. Check your state education department's ICT guidelines.
  • Google Workspace for Education licences: Schools using Google Workspace for Education accounts (not personal Google accounts) have additional data protection terms covering student data under the Google Workspace for Education agreement.

Google Forms Quiz Mode for Australian Classrooms

Google Forms Quiz mode is free and works well for self-paced formative assessment. Enable it in Settings > Quizzes > Make this a quiz. Set correct answers and point values. Students see their score immediately after submission. Results collect in Google Sheets for the teacher. No paid account required.

For live competitive quiz games with the whole class, Kahoot is more engaging than Google Forms - students can see each other's scores on a shared screen. Use Google Forms for take-home assessment and Kahoot for in-class review. Convert the same quiz document to both using Pirai AI.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Google Forms free for Australian teachers?+

Yes. Google Forms is free with any Google account. Schools using Google Workspace for Education also have free access with additional data protection terms for student data. There is no limit on the number of forms, questions, or responses.

Is Google Forms safe for Australian student data?+

For anonymous surveys without student identifiers, Google Forms is generally low-risk. For surveys collecting identifiable student information, Google Forms stores data on US-based servers - which may not comply with your school system's cloud data policies. Check with your school's privacy coordinator or your state education department's ICT guidelines. Schools using Google Workspace for Education accounts (not personal Gmail) have stronger data protection terms.
