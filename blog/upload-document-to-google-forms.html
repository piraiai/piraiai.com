Upload a Document to Google Forms: The Direct Method

Arun Raj•August 20, 2026•5 min read•Guide
Uploading a Document to Google Forms

Google Forms does not have a native document upload or import feature. You cannot open Google Forms, click Upload, and have your Word or PDF document become a form. Pirai AI adds this capability: upload your document to Pirai AI, and it creates the Google Form directly in your Drive with one click and one Google authorisation step.

Why Google Forms Has No Document Upload

Google Forms was designed as a form builder - each question is created individually through the interface. There is no import button, no file upload for questions, and no document connector. The only question import Google Forms supports natively is copying questions from another existing Google Form in your account. Everything else requires either a third-party converter or manual entry.

The Direct Upload Method With Pirai AI

Pirai AI is the missing upload button for Google Forms. The process:

1. Go to platform.piraiai.com and click Upload. Select your Word (.docx), PDF, or Excel (.xlsx) file from your device. The document is processed immediately.

2. Review the questions. Every question Pirai AI extracted from your document appears in the workspace with its question type and answer options. This is the review step - the equivalent of checking the form before publishing. Make any adjustments needed.

3. Select Google Forms and click Convert. A Google sign-in window appears. This is the authorisation step that allows Pirai AI to create a form in your Drive.

4. Sign in with your Google account and click Allow. Pirai AI creates the form in your Google Drive directly in your Drive. You are redirected to a link to the new form.

5. Open your new Google Form. The form is in your Drive with all questions, types, and options already in place. It behaves exactly like a form you built manually - you can edit it, share it, enable Quiz mode, and collect responses.

Document Formats for Upload

  • Word (.docx): Survey scripts, questionnaire drafts, research instruments
  • PDF (native): Published instruments, client scripts, digital questionnaires
  • PDF (scanned): Paper forms scanned to PDF - enable Advanced PDF Processing
  • Excel (.xlsx): Question banks with answer options in columns
  • Pasted text: Copy questions from any source and paste into Pirai AI's editor

What Happens to Your Uploaded Document

Pirai AI processes your document to extract question structure and generates the Google Form directly in your Drive. Your document is not stored permanently after processing. Pirai AI's client-side processing and no-retention architecture means your document content is used for conversion only.
