REDCap at Australian Universities: Access, NHMRC Requirements, and Instrument Setup

Arun Raj•August 04, 2026•5 min read•Guide
REDCap in Australian Research

REDCap is the standard electronic data capture platform for NHMRC-funded clinical research in Australia. It is deployed at most Group of Eight universities, major academic medical centres, and hospital research institutes under institutional licences with the Vanderbilt REDCap Consortium. Australian researchers often have no choice in platform - if their funding body or HREC protocol specifies REDCap, they must use it. The challenge is that setting up a REDCap instrument from a Word or PDF questionnaire still requires significant manual effort without a conversion tool.

Which Australian Universities Have REDCap

REDCap is confirmed at the following Australian institutions (note: institutional availability changes - verify with your research office):

  • University of South Australia (UniSA) - REDCap access via research.unisa.edu.au. Request form available on the Research Office portal.
  • CQUniversity - Available to all staff and students with a CQU account. Email ethics@cqu.edu.au to request access.
  • University of Melbourne - REDCap operated by Research Computing.
  • UNSW Sydney - REDCap available through Research Technology Services.
  • University of Queensland - REDCap access through the Research Data Management team.
  • Monash University - REDCap via the Monash eResearch Centre.
  • University of Western Australia (UWA) - REDCap access via the library and research computing team.
  • Australian Catholic University, Deakin, Griffith - REDCap confirmed via consortium membership.

If your institution is not listed, contact your research informatics team, eResearch office, or library to ask whether your institution is a REDCap Consortium member. Most Australian universities with medical or health faculties have REDCap.

NHMRC and HREC Requirements for REDCap

The NHMRC National Statement on Ethical Conduct in Human Research requires that electronic research data be stored securely and that data handling arrangements be described in your HREC application. REDCap on institutional infrastructure satisfies these requirements for most Australian clinical research studies because:

  • Data resides on the institution's own servers (or institutional cloud under the university's data governance arrangements)
  • REDCap's full audit trail satisfies ICH GCP requirements for clinical trials
  • Institutional REDCap is covered by the university's information security and privacy frameworks
  • Data does not pass through offshore commercial infrastructure

Some NHMRC grants and clinical trial sponsor agreements specifically require REDCap or an equivalent GCP-aligned EDC system. If your grant agreement or sponsor specifies REDCap, you will be directed to use it by your Chief Investigator or trial coordinator.

Australian Privacy Act and Data Sovereignty

The Australian Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) govern the handling of personal information in research. APP 8 covers cross-border disclosure of personal information - relevant when research data is stored on offshore servers. REDCap on institutional Australian servers does not involve cross-border data transfer. Platforms like Qualtrics (US cloud), SurveyMonkey (US), and Google Forms (US) involve offshore storage of research participant data - which requires consideration under APP 8 and may require additional HREC justification for research involving sensitive information.

Setting Up Research Instruments in REDCap

REDCap imports instruments via its Data Dictionary - a CSV file defining every field's name, type, label, and answer choices in REDCap's column structure. For a 40-item validated clinical scale (e.g. PHQ-9, GAD-7, DASS-21), building this Data Dictionary manually takes 3-5 hours and introduces transcription errors that can alter item wording - a critical problem for validated instruments where the psychometric properties are established for specific wording.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Australian universities have REDCap?+

REDCap is available at most Australian universities with health or medical research, including UniSA, CQUniversity, University of Melbourne, UNSW, University of Queensland, Monash, UWA, Griffith, and Deakin. Contact your research informatics team, eResearch office, or library to confirm your institution's REDCap access process.

Is REDCap required for NHMRC-funded research in Australia?+

NHMRC does not universally mandate REDCap, but some grant agreements, clinical trial sponsor requirements, and HREC protocols specify REDCap or equivalent GCP-aligned EDC systems for studies collecting sensitive health data. If your funding agreement specifies REDCap, you will typically be directed to use it by your Chief Investigator.

Does using REDCap satisfy Australian Privacy Principles for research data?+

REDCap on your institution's own servers avoids the cross-border data transfer concerns under APP 8 because data stays on Australian institutional infrastructure. For research involving sensitive health information, this is a significant advantage over US-cloud-hosted platforms like Qualtrics or SurveyMonkey, which involve offshore data storage that may require additional HREC justification.
