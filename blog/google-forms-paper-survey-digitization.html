Why Printing a Google Form Is a Dead End

Google Forms will happily print a blank form, or export the responses it already has to a spreadsheet. What it will not do is take a completed printed page and read it back in. Forms was built for screens; a paper copy has no return path. Whoever collects those printed forms has to open each one and retype the answers by hand.

There is no optical mark recognition for checkboxes, no handwriting recognition for open text, and no way to match a multi-page paper form back to the right respondent automatically. Printing is one-directional.

How Lens Fills the Gap for Google Forms

Lens reads the printed, marked form directly and checks what it finds against your Form's own questions and options. Convert your survey to a base format, map the printed layout from one blank copy, then upload your scanned response PDFs in bulk.

The output is a structured CSV or Excel file, shaped to sit alongside the same Google Sheet your Form already writes to, so paper and digital responses can live in one combined dataset instead of two separate ones.

Checked against your survey, not guessed

Every answer Lens extracts is verified against your survey's own known questions, options, and items. If something on the page can't be matched with confidence, it's flagged for review, not silently recorded.
