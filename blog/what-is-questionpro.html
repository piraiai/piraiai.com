What Is QuestionPro? Survey Platform Guide for Researchers and Agencies

Arun Raj•September 1, 2026•6 min read•Guide
QuestionPro

QuestionPro is a full-featured survey platform used by market research agencies, academic researchers, enterprise CX teams, and HR departments worldwide. It offers a comprehensive range of question types, audience panel access, advanced analytics, offline survey capability, and dedicated research and academic editions. Pirai AI is an official QuestionPro partner, enabling one-click conversion of Word, PDF, and Excel documents directly into QuestionPro surveys.

Who Uses QuestionPro

Market research agencies: Professional MR agencies use QuestionPro for client quantitative studies - consumer surveys, brand tracking, concept testing, and pricing research. The platform's panel integrations and quota management support structured fieldwork. QuestionPro's advanced question types (MaxDiff, conjoint, semantic differential) cover the full range of standard MR methodologies.

Academic and social researchers: Universities use QuestionPro through the Academic plan for dissertation surveys, longitudinal studies, and research with custom panel management. The Research Edition includes advanced features for complex study designs.

Customer experience teams: QuestionPro CX provides customer satisfaction, NPS, and customer effort measurement with real-time dashboards and closed-loop feedback workflows.

HR and employee experience: QuestionPro Workforce manages employee engagement, pulse surveys, 360-degree feedback, and HR research with specialised analytics.

Key Platform Features

Offline surveys: QuestionPro's offline capability allows surveys to be completed without an internet connection - data syncs when connectivity is restored. Used for field research, in-store intercepts, clinical site visits, and data collection in remote areas.

QuestionPro Audience: Built-in panel access for recruiting survey respondents from a pre-screened consumer panel. Available directly in the platform without a separate vendor relationship.

Analytics and reporting: Real-time response dashboards, cross-tabulation, statistical significance testing, text analysis for open-ended responses, and automated report generation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is QuestionPro used for?+

QuestionPro is used for market research surveys, academic and social research, customer experience measurement, employee engagement surveys, and product and concept testing. It serves professional research agencies, universities, enterprise CX teams, and HR departments.

Is QuestionPro free?+

QuestionPro has a free plan with basic features and response limits. Paid plans (Essentials, Advanced, Team Edition, Research, Academic) provide higher response volumes, advanced question types, panel access, offline capability, and analytics. The Academic plan provides research-specific features for university researchers.

What makes QuestionPro different from SurveyMonkey?+

QuestionPro has more advanced question types (MaxDiff, conjoint, semantic differential, offline surveys), stronger research and academic editions, built-in panel access (QuestionPro Audience), and more comprehensive analytics including statistical significance testing. SurveyMonkey has a simpler interface and broader name recognition. QuestionPro is generally preferred for professional research; SurveyMonkey for simpler or smaller-scale surveys.
