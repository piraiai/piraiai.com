Google Forms for Australian Schools: From Student Assessments to School Operations

Arun Raj•August 25, 2026•5 min read•Guide
Google Forms Across Australian Schools

Google Forms is the most widely used digital form tool in Australian public schools through Google Workspace for Education - and increasingly in Catholic and independent schools that use Google alongside M365. Teachers use it for student assessments and quizzes. Administration teams use it for parent surveys, excursion permission, and enrolment interest. Wellbeing coordinators use it for student check-ins. The problem in every case is the same: the questions exist in a Word document or PDF and have to be rebuilt in Google Forms by hand. Pirai AI converts any existing document to a Google Form in one click.

Teaching and Learning Uses

Student quizzes and assessments: End-of-topic multiple choice tests, reading comprehension checks, science revision quizzes, vocabulary tests, spelling practice - all converted from teacher-created Word documents or published PDF resources to self-marking Google Forms quizzes.

NAPLAN preparation: Practice questions in NAPLAN style converted to Google Forms for digital format familiarisation ahead of the online NAPLAN assessment for Year 3, 5, 7, and 9.

Exit tickets: 3-5 question reflection forms at the end of a lesson - quick to build from a Word template and distributed via Google Classroom.

Student research surveys: Senior secondary students designing research surveys for HSIE, Science, or Health units - Pirai AI converts their Word survey drafts to Google Forms for peer or community data collection.

School Administration Uses

Parent and community surveys: Annual parent satisfaction surveys, event feedback forms, curriculum consultation questionnaires - drafted by the principal or executive team in Word and converted to Google Forms for digital distribution.

Excursion and permission forms: Excursion details, permission checkboxes, medical information questions, and payment acknowledgements in Google Forms instead of paper slips. Responses feed into a Google Sheet for the organising teacher.

Enrolment and expression of interest: Prospective family contact information, sibling details, and school preference questions converted from the school's Word template to Google Forms for the school website.

Student wellbeing check-ins: Regular wellbeing pulse surveys for pastoral care coordinators - anonymous or identified, depending on the purpose. Questions drafted by the wellbeing team in Word, converted to Google Forms for quick student completion.

Staff professional development surveys: Pre- and post-PD feedback forms, staff satisfaction surveys, and needs analysis questionnaires converted from Word to Google Forms for distribution to teaching staff.

The School Google Forms Workflow With Pirai AI

Teachers: Write assessment questions in Word (as usual for planning). Upload to Pirai AI. Click Export to Google Form. Sign in with school Google account. Share the form link via Google Classroom. Done - without rebuilding anything.

Administration teams: Draft survey or form in Word. Upload to Pirai AI. Export to Google Forms. Share link via school email, website, or newsletter. Responses appear in Google Sheets automatically.

School leadership: Receive the Word template survey. Upload to Pirai AI. Export to Google Forms. Distribute. No reliance on the IT team to build the form.

Privacy Compliance for Australian Schools

Australian schools handling student and parent data through Google Forms must comply with the Privacy Act 1988 and Australian Privacy Principles. For school Google Workspace accounts, Google's terms for Google Workspace for Education provide additional protections for student data beyond standard consumer Google accounts. Pirai AI does not store uploaded school documents or forms after conversion - no assessment content, parent data, or student information is retained in Pirai AI's systems.

For M365 Schools: Microsoft Forms

Australian Catholic and independent schools standardised on Microsoft 365 can convert the same Word documents to Microsoft Forms using Pirai AI. The same workflow - upload, convert, share - produces a Microsoft Forms form integrated with Teams and SharePoint instead of Google Drive and Classroom.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Google Forms available in Australian public schools?+

Yes. Australian public schools access Google Forms through their Google Workspace for Education accounts provided by their State Department of Education. Teachers and administrators can create and distribute forms using their school Google accounts at no additional cost.

How do Australian schools convert Word documents to Google Forms?+

Upload the Word document to Pirai AI at platform.piraiai.com. Click Export to Google Form. Sign in with your school Google Workspace account and click Allow. The form is created directly in your Drive. Share via Google Classroom, email, or QR code.

Can Pirai AI convert school admin forms as well as assessments?+

Yes. Excursion consent forms, parent feedback surveys, enrolment expressions of interest, wellbeing check-ins, and staff PD surveys all convert from Word or PDF to Google Forms using the same process. Any structured document with questions or fields converts to Google Forms.

What about Catholic and independent schools using Microsoft 365?+

Pirai AI also exports to Microsoft Forms for M365 schools. Upload the same Word document and select Microsoft Forms instead of Google Forms. The Quick Import output integrates with Teams assignments and the M365 gradebook.
