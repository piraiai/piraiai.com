Turn an Existing Questionnaire Into Google Forms in Seconds

Arun Raj•August 13, 2026•5 min read•Guide
From Questionnaire to Google Form

You have an existing questionnaire - a validated research instrument, a customer feedback survey, a paper form used for years, a Word document reviewed and approved by your team. Getting it into Google Forms without retyping every question is the problem. Google Forms has no document import. Pirai AI converts your existing questionnaire to a Google Apps Script that recreates the entire form automatically when you run it. Seconds from upload to form - not hours.

Types of Existing Questionnaires That Convert

Paper-based questionnaires: Scan the paper form to PDF and upload. Enable Advanced PDF Processing for scanned documents. Pirai AI extracts question text from the scanned image. The same instrument your team has used offline moves into Google Forms without retyping a single question.

Word document questionnaires: Survey scripts, research instruments, and questionnaire drafts in .docx. The most reliable conversion - all text is clean and directly readable.

PDF questionnaires: Published instruments, client-provided questionnaires, government forms, academic scales. Native digital PDFs extract cleanly. Scanned PDFs require Advanced PDF Processing.

Excel-based question banks: Questionnaires maintained as rows of questions with answer options in adjacent columns. Upload the .xlsx and Pirai AI reads the structure.

Questionnaires from other platforms: Qualtrics QSF files. Export the QSF from Qualtrics and upload to Pirai AI to generate a Google Forms version of the same questionnaire.

Why Retyping an Existing Questionnaire Is Risky

For validated questionnaires - standardised scales, psychometrically tested instruments, regulatory-approved forms - the exact wording of every question and answer option matters. A changed word, a missed option, a reordered scale invalidates the instrument's comparability with existing data.

Pirai AI extracts verbatim text from the source document. Question wording and answer options in Google Forms match the source questionnaire exactly. The Apps Script uses the original text character-for-character - no paraphrasing, no rewriting.

Existing Questionnaire to Google Forms: Step by Step

1. Upload the questionnaire at platform.piraiai.com. For scanned paper forms, enable Advanced PDF Processing before uploading.

2. Pirai AI extracts all questions. Review in the workspace - confirm question types, check wording matches the source.

3. Select Google Forms. Download the Apps Script file.

4. Open a blank Google Form. Click the three-dot menu (top right) > Script editor.

5. In the Script Editor, delete any existing code. Paste the Pirai AI script. Click Run. Authorise the script if prompted (standard Google Apps Script permission). Your questionnaire is now a Google Form.

After Converting Your Questionnaire

Once the script runs, review the Google Form: check section breaks match the original questionnaire structure, confirm answer options are complete and correctly ordered, and enable Quiz mode in Settings > Quizzes if graded responses are needed. If the questionnaire uses skip logic (routing respondents to different sections based on answers), set this up using Google Forms' Go to section based on answer option in the three-dot menu of each relevant question.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I convert a paper questionnaire to Google Forms?+

Yes. Scan the paper questionnaire to PDF and upload to Pirai AI at platform.piraiai.com with Advanced PDF Processing enabled. Pirai AI runs OCR to extract question text from the scanned image and generates a Google Apps Script. Run the script in a blank Google Form's Script Editor to build the form.

Does Pirai AI preserve exact question wording from existing questionnaires?+

Yes. Pirai AI extracts verbatim text from the source document without paraphrasing or rewriting. The question text and answer options in Google Forms match the source questionnaire exactly - important for validated instruments where wording modifications affect data comparability with previous collections.

Can I convert a questionnaire from another platform (like Qualtrics) to Google Forms?+

Yes. Upload the Qualtrics QSF file to Pirai AI, select Google Forms, download the Apps Script, and run it in a blank Google Form's Script Editor. For SurveyMonkey surveys, export the question text to Word or PDF first, then upload to Pirai AI.

Does Google Forms have a questionnaire import feature?+

No. Google Forms has no native document or questionnaire import feature. The only built-in question import is from other existing Google Forms. Pirai AI's Apps Script approach is the standard workaround - it builds the form programmatically from any source document.
