Pirai AI for Australian Teachers: Convert Assessments and Worksheets Instantly

Arun Raj•August 25, 2026•5 min read•Guide
For Australian Teachers

Australian teachers spend 3 to 4 hours every week converting paper assessments, Word documents, and AI-drafted content into digital platforms. A spelling quiz, a reading comprehension check, a parent survey, an excursion consent form - each one needs to be rebuilt by hand in Google Forms, Kahoot, or Moodle. Pirai AI eliminates that rebuild. Upload the document you already have, and it is in Google Forms in minutes. The same document also exports to Kahoot for a live classroom game, Moodle for your school LMS, and Microsoft Forms for M365 schools - from a single upload.

What Australian Teachers Convert With Pirai AI

Assessment and quizzes: Unit tests, spelling lists, reading comprehension questions, maths problem sets, multiple choice practice papers - in Word, PDF, or generated by AI tools. Upload once and create a Google Form for individual completion and a Kahoot for class review in the same session.

NAPLAN preparation materials: Practice questions in NAPLAN style converted to Google Forms for digital practice, giving students experience with online testing format ahead of the official assessment.

School operations forms: Excursion consent forms, parent feedback surveys, student wellbeing check-ins, enrolment questionnaires, and staff meeting agendas converted from Word to Google Forms for Google Workspace schools or Microsoft Forms for M365 schools.

AI-generated content: Questions drafted in ChatGPT, Gemini, or Copilot copied and pasted into Pirai AI for instant conversion to any platform. No reformatting required.

Google Workspace Schools (Public and Government)

Most Australian public school teachers access Google Forms through their school's Google Workspace for Education account. Pirai AI creates Google Forms directly in your Drive with one click - upload your document, click Export to Google Form, sign in with your school Google account, and the form appears in Drive ready to share with students. No Script Editor, no add-on installation, no manual question entry.

The form link can be distributed via Google Classroom, email, or a QR code printed on a worksheet. Student responses collect automatically in Google Sheets for analysis.

Microsoft 365 Schools (Catholic and Independent)

Many Australian Catholic and independent schools are standardised on Microsoft 365. Pirai AI converts the same document to Microsoft Forms for M365 schools. Upload your Word assessment, select Microsoft Forms, download the Quick Import output, and import into Microsoft Forms. The form integrates with Teams assignments and the M365 gradebook.

Kahoot for Live Classroom Review

Kahoot is used in virtually every Australian secondary school and many primary schools for live review games. Pirai AI converts the same quiz document to Kahoot format from the same upload. Create the Google Form for individual take-home assessment and the Kahoot for Friday's class review - from the same Word or PDF document, in one session.

Moodle for TAFE and Senior Secondary

Moodle is the dominant LMS at Australian TAFEs and many senior secondary colleges. Pirai AI exports to Moodle AIKEN format for quiz questions. Upload your question document, select Moodle, and download the AIKEN-formatted text file for import into your Moodle course as a quiz activity.

Student Data Privacy

Australian schools operate under the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles. Pirai AI does not store uploaded assessment documents after conversion. All processing is client-side in memory. Pirai AI does not use school content to train AI models - the OpenAI API is configured with data sharing disabled. No student names, school details, or assessment content is retained.
