GetFeedback Shutting Down: Migration Guide for Survey and CX Teams (2026)

Arun Raj•August 02, 2026•5 min read•Guide
What Is Happening

GetFeedback Direct - the Salesforce-integrated survey and NPS platform - is shutting down completely on December 31, 2026. After that date, all account access terminates and customer data is permanently deleted. SurveyMonkey (which owns GetFeedback) has not committed to any post-shutdown data retrieval process. If you use GetFeedback Direct for NPS, CSAT, CES, or lifecycle surveys, you need to migrate before year-end. GetFeedback Digital (website feedback, formerly Usabilla) is not affected and continues operating.

Immediate Actions: Before You Choose a Platform

Export your data now. Do not wait until you have chosen a replacement platform. Export all response history as CSV from GetFeedback Direct immediately - SurveyMonkey has not guaranteed data retrieval after the December 31 shutdown. Go to GetFeedback Direct > Reports > Export and download all survey response data in CSV format. Store these exports in a secure location independent of the GetFeedback platform.

Document your Salesforce mappings. Catalogue every GetFeedback survey that triggers from Salesforce workflows, every field that response data writes back to (Opportunity, Contact, Case), and every automation that acts on survey responses. These mappings need to be rebuilt in your replacement platform - the more complete your documentation, the faster the rebuild.

Identify surveys still in field. Close or pause any active GetFeedback surveys before migrating to avoid split response data between two platforms.

Migration Platform Options

SurveyMonkey Enterprise (Official Migration Path)

SurveyMonkey owns GetFeedback and recommends SurveyMonkey Enterprise as the migration path. The transition typically takes 90 days according to SurveyMonkey's guidance. For US-based customers, SurveyMonkey offers data migration services. Key note: SurveyMonkey Enterprise uses an external Salesforce connector rather than GetFeedback's native Salesforce architecture - validate field mapping, object relationships, and automation timing carefully before switching off GetFeedback.

Qualtrics CustomerXM

For enterprise CX programmes requiring deeper analytics, Text iQ for verbatim analysis, and custom dashboard layers, Qualtrics CustomerXM is the enterprise-grade alternative. More configuration required than SurveyMonkey, and significantly higher cost. Best for programmes where the analytics capability needs to grow alongside the survey operation.

SurveySparrow, Zonka Feedback, Responsly

Mid-market alternatives with Salesforce integrations offering faster deployment and lower cost than Qualtrics. Evaluate based on your Salesforce architecture needs and the complexity of your current GetFeedback workflows.

Converting GetFeedback Surveys to a New Platform

GetFeedback surveys are typically NPS, CSAT, and CES instruments - straightforward question structures with 1-3 questions per survey. Converting these to another platform is the simpler part of the migration. The harder part is the Salesforce integration rebuild.

To convert GetFeedback survey content to SurveyMonkey or Qualtrics using Pirai AI:

  1. Export or screenshot your GetFeedback surveys. There is no standard export format for GetFeedback survey structure. Screenshot or copy the question text and answer options from each live survey.
  2. Create a Word document with the survey questions. One survey per document, questions formatted clearly with answer options listed below each.
  3. Upload to Pirai AI at platform.piraiai.com. Select SurveyMonkey (for the official migration path) or Qualtrics (if moving to Qualtrics CustomerXM) as the target.
  4. Download the output file and import into your chosen platform. For SurveyMonkey: Paste Your Content .txt file. For Qualtrics: QSF file via Tools > Import Survey.
  5. Rebuild Salesforce integrations in the new platform using your documented workflow mapping.

Historical Delighted response data should have been exported before June 30; if it was not, contact SurveyMonkey support immediately to enquire about data retrieval options.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is GetFeedback shutting down?+

GetFeedback Direct - the Salesforce-integrated survey product - is shutting down completely on December 31, 2026. After that date, all account access terminates and customer data is permanently deleted. GetFeedback Digital (website and in-app feedback, formerly Usabilla) is not shutting down and continues operating under the GetFeedback brand.

What happens to my GetFeedback data after December 31, 2026?+

Account access ends on December 31, 2026, and SurveyMonkey has not publicly committed to a data retention period after shutdown. The safest course is to export all response data as CSV from GetFeedback Direct now, while access is still available. Do not rely on post-shutdown data retrieval being possible.

What is the best GetFeedback alternative?+

SurveyMonkey Enterprise is the official migration path promoted by SurveyMonkey (which owns GetFeedback). Other options include Qualtrics CustomerXM for enterprise analytics depth, SurveySparrow and Zonka Feedback for mid-market Salesforce integration, and Responsly for a closer architectural equivalent to GetFeedback's Salesforce-native model. The right choice depends on your Salesforce architecture and CX programme maturity.

Upload to Pirai AI at platform.piraiai.com, select SurveyMonkey, and download the Paste Your Content .txt file. Import it into SurveyMonkey via Import Questions. How long does a GetFeedback to SurveyMonkey migration take?+

SurveyMonkey estimates 90 days for a full transition. The survey content conversion is fast - most GetFeedback NPS/CSAT surveys are 1-3 questions and can be rebuilt in the new platform in hours. The majority of the 90 days goes to rebuilding and testing Salesforce workflow triggers, field mappings, object relationships, and automation logic that GetFeedback had configured.
