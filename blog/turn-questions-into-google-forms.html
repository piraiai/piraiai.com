Turn Questions Into Google Forms: From Any Source in Minutes

Arun Raj•August 13, 2026•5 min read•Guide
The Google Forms Import Problem

Google Forms has no native document import. You cannot upload a Word file, paste plain text, or import a PDF. The only built-in option is importing questions from another existing Google Form - which only works if you already have the questions inside Google's ecosystem. Pirai AI solves this with an Apps Script approach: upload any document, and Pirai AI generates a script that builds your entire form automatically when you run it in Google's Script Editor. No add-ons. No OAuth. Free with any Google account.

Turn Questions Into Google Forms From Any Source

From a Word document (.docx): Upload your questionnaire draft, survey script, or research instrument. Pirai AI reads every question, identifies its type, and generates the Apps Script.

From a PDF: Upload native digital PDFs or scanned paper surveys (enable Advanced PDF Processing). Question text and answer options are extracted verbatim.

From an Excel spreadsheet (.xlsx): Upload question banks with answer options in adjacent columns. Pirai AI reads the first sheet and converts the structure.

From pasted text: Copy questions from ChatGPT, a Google Doc, an email, or anywhere - paste directly into Pirai AI's editor and export to Google Forms.

From a Qualtrics QSF: Upload an existing Qualtrics survey file and generate a Google Forms version without rebuilding from scratch.

How Pirai AI Turns Questions Into Google Forms

Step 1 - Upload your source. Go to platform.piraiai.com. Upload your file or paste your questions directly.

Step 2 - Review in the workspace. Every extracted question appears with its detected type and answer options. Edit anything before export.

Step 3 - Select Google Forms and download the Apps Script. Pirai AI generates a .gs script file - not a form file, because Google Forms has no file import. The script creates your form programmatically when run.

Step 4 - Run the script in Google Forms. Open a blank Google Form. Click the three-dot menu (top right) and select Script editor. A new tab opens with the Apps Script editor. Delete any placeholder code, paste the Pirai AI script, and click Run (the triangle button). Your form is built instantly - all questions, all answer options, all sections created automatically.

Step 5 - Enable Quiz mode if needed. Go to Settings (gear icon) > Quizzes tab > Make this a quiz. Set correct answers and point values for each question.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you turn any question list into a Google Form?+

Yes. Upload a Word document, PDF, Excel spreadsheet, or paste questions from any source into Pirai AI at platform.piraiai.com. Select Google Forms and download the Apps Script. Open a blank Google Form, go to the three-dot menu > Script editor, paste and run the script, and your form is built instantly.

Why can't I just paste questions into Google Forms?+

Google Forms does not have a paste import feature. Plain text pasted into Google Forms creates a description block, not questions. The only way to import questions programmatically is via Apps Script - which is what Pirai AI generates automatically from your source document.

Do I need any add-ons or extensions to use Pirai AI with Google Forms?+

No. Pirai AI generates a standard Google Apps Script that runs in the built-in Script Editor inside Google Forms. No add-on installation, no browser extension, no Google Drive permissions are required. Works with any Google account (free Gmail or Google Workspace).

Can I turn a Qualtrics survey into a Google Form?+

Yes. Upload the Qualtrics QSF file to Pirai AI, select Google Forms, download the Apps Script, and run it in a blank Google Form's Script Editor. Question structure and answer options transfer. Display logic (skip logic) must be configured manually in Google Forms after creation.
