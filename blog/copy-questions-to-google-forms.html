Copy Questions Into Google Forms: The Right Way (Apps Script Method)

Arun Raj•August 13, 2026•5 min read•Guide
The Copy-Paste Problem in Google Forms

If you have tried to copy questions from a Word document and paste them into Google Forms, you already know the result: the pasted text appears as a description or title block, not as individual questions. Google Forms does not have a paste import feature. The text you paste is treated as plain text, not as a structured question list. The correct method uses Google Apps Script - and Pirai AI generates that script automatically from any copied or uploaded content.

Why Copy-Paste Does Not Work in Google Forms

Google Forms was designed as a point-and-click form builder. Every question is created individually through the interface. There is no "paste questions here" input field. When you paste text into the form editor, it fills the description field of whatever element is currently selected - not a new set of questions.

This is a fundamental design limitation, not a bug. The workaround is Google Apps Script - a JavaScript-based platform built into Google Workspace that can create form questions programmatically. This is exactly what Pirai AI generates.

The Correct Method: Paste Into the Script Editor

Instead of pasting questions into Google Forms directly, paste an Apps Script into the Script Editor. The script creates all your questions when it runs. Here is the difference:

How Pirai AI Generates the Pasteable Script

1. Copy your questions from any source - a Word document, a PDF, an Excel file, or even a plain text list - and paste them into the Pirai AI editor at platform.piraiai.com. Or upload the file directly.

2. Select Google Forms and download the Apps Script. This is what you will paste - not into the form editor, but into the Script Editor.

3. Open a blank Google Form. Click the three-dot menu (top right) > Script editor.

4. Paste the Apps Script into the Script Editor (replacing any existing code). Click Run.

5. Refresh your Google Form. All questions are now in the form exactly as they appeared in your source document.

The Other Option: Import From Another Google Form

Google Forms does have one built-in question import: from another Google Form in your account. Click the import questions icon (overlapping squares) in the right toolbar of the form editor. This lets you copy questions between forms - but only from forms already in your Google account, not from Word documents, PDFs, or external question sources. For anything outside Google Forms, the Apps Script method is required.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why can't I paste questions into Google Forms?+

Google Forms does not have a paste import feature. Pasting text into the form editor places it in a description field, not as questions. The correct method is to paste an Apps Script into the Google Forms Script Editor and run it - the script creates all questions programmatically. Pirai AI generates this script from any source document.

How do I copy questions from Word into Google Forms?+

Upload your Word document to Pirai AI at platform.piraiai.com. Select Google Forms and download the Apps Script. Open a blank Google Form > three-dot menu > Script editor. Paste the script and click Run. All questions from your Word document are created in the form automatically.

Is there a way to bulk paste questions into Google Forms?+

Not via direct paste - Google Forms ignores bulk-pasted question text. The Apps Script method is the standard approach for bulk question creation. Pirai AI generates the script from your document or pasted question list in 1-3 minutes, then running the script creates all questions at once.

Can I copy questions from one Google Form to another?+

Yes - natively. Click the import questions icon (overlapping squares) in the right toolbar of any Google Form. Select the source form from your Google Drive and choose which questions to import. This works only between existing Google Forms in your account, not from external documents.
