PDF to Qualtrics: Convert Research PDFs and Clinical Scales to Qualtrics Surveys

Arun Raj•September 1, 2026•6 min read•Guide
PDF to Qualtrics

Research instruments are commonly distributed as PDFs - published validated scales, sponsor-provided questionnaires, and institutional research instruments. Qualtrics does not accept PDFs for import. Pirai AI converts any PDF to a Qualtrics QSF file: upload the PDF, review extracted questions, download the QSF, and import into Qualtrics. Both native digital PDFs and scanned paper surveys are supported.

Types of Research PDFs That Convert to Qualtrics

  • Published validated scales: GAD-7, PHQ-9, DASS-21, SF-36, UCLA Loneliness Scale, BFI-10, SWLS, and hundreds more downloaded as PDFs and converted to Qualtrics for online deployment
  • Sponsor-provided questionnaires: Industry or government sponsor instruments provided as Word or PDF templates for adaptation into Qualtrics
  • Ethics committee approved instruments: Instruments approved by HRECs, IRBs, or ethics committees, distributed as PDFs to research teams for Qualtrics deployment
  • Multi-site study instruments: A single PDF instrument distributed to multiple institutional sites that each implement the survey in their own Qualtrics account
  • Legacy paper surveys being digitised: Older paper instruments scanned to PDF and converted to Qualtrics for online or longitudinal follow-up studies

Converting a PDF to Qualtrics: Steps

1. Go to platform.piraiai.com. For scanned paper PDFs, enable Advanced PDF Processing. Upload your PDF.

2. Review extracted questions in the workspace. For validated scales, compare extracted question labels against the published instrument to confirm verbatim accuracy.

3. Select Qualtrics and download the QSF file.

4. In Qualtrics: Survey Builder > Tools > Import/Export > Import Survey > select QSF > Import.

5. In the Survey Builder, configure display logic, add scoring embedded data if the instrument has a scoring algorithm, and set up Survey Flow before distribution.

Converting Validated Scales - Key Considerations

Validated psychometric and clinical instruments have published psychometric properties that depend on exact item wording and response option coding. Before using a Qualtrics survey built from a validated scale PDF:

  • Verify item wording: Compare each Qualtrics question label against the published instrument. Pirai AI extracts verbatim text but review confirms accuracy.
  • Verify response codes: For scales with specific numeric coding (PHQ-9: Not at all=0, Several days=1, etc.), check that the Qualtrics answer option codes match the published scoring key.
  • Add scoring: Add scoring embedded data or a survey end scoring formula in Survey Flow for instruments with total or subscale scores.
  • Check permissions: Confirm your use of the validated instrument is permitted under the scale's licence terms. Most research-use validated scales are free for non-commercial research use.

Scanned Survey PDFs

Scanned paper surveys - physical questionnaires photographed or scanned to PDF - require OCR extraction. Enable Advanced PDF Processing before uploading. Pirai AI runs OCR on the scanned image and extracts question text. Best results come from clean black-and-white prints at 300dpi or higher. Review all extracted questions in the workspace carefully before generating the QSF - OCR accuracy is high for clean scans but review is essential for clinical instruments where every word matters.
