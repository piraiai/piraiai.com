Excel to Google Forms: Convert a Spreadsheet to a Google Form

Arun Raj•August 13, 2026•5 min read•Guide
Excel to Google Forms

Google Forms can link to Google Sheets to collect responses - but it cannot import questions from a spreadsheet. Question banks maintained in Excel stay in Excel unless you rebuild them manually in Google Forms. Pirai AI converts your .xlsx question bank to a Google Apps Script that creates the entire form when you run it. Upload once, form built in seconds.

Excel Structures Pirai AI Reads

Pirai AI reads the first sheet of your .xlsx file. Common survey spreadsheet layouts it handles:

  • Question + answer columns: Column A = question text, Columns B-F = answer options. Pirai AI reads each row as a question.
  • Type-flagged columns: A column indicating MC (multiple choice), OT (open text), or Matrix. Pirai AI uses the type flag to assign the correct Google Forms question type.
  • Survey scripts with coding: Question text, response options, and value codes. Pirai AI extracts question and option text columns.
  • Simple numbered lists: Q1, Q2 in column A with options listed in subsequent columns or rows below.

Excel to Google Forms: Steps

1. Upload your .xlsx file at platform.piraiai.com. Pirai AI reads the first sheet.

2. Review extracted questions in the workspace. Confirm question types, check answer option completeness, reorder if needed.

3. Select Google Forms. Download the Apps Script.

4. Open a blank Google Form at forms.google.com. Click the three-dot menu > Script editor.

5. Delete existing code. Paste the Pirai AI script. Click Run. Authorise if prompted.

6. Refresh the Google Form - all questions from your Excel file are now in the form.

Who Converts Excel Question Banks to Google Forms

Teachers and educators: Quiz question banks maintained in Excel - one row per question, columns for each answer option. Convert the bank to Google Forms for a new quiz or test without retyping.

HR and operations teams: Employee surveys, onboarding assessments, and feedback questionnaires tracked in Excel. Convert to Google Forms at the time of each survey cycle.

Researchers: Multi-wave study instruments stored in Excel with tracked changes across versions. Convert the current wave to Google Forms for the new data collection round.

Training and L&D teams: Course assessments maintained as question banks in Excel. Export to Google Forms for each training cohort.

Excel vs Google Sheets for Google Forms - Key Difference

Google Forms connects to Google Sheets to store responses - but cannot import questions from Google Sheets either. Whether your question bank is in Excel or Google Sheets, the import limitation is the same. Pirai AI accepts both .xlsx files and can also process content exported from Google Sheets as Excel. The Apps Script approach works regardless of which spreadsheet tool you use.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I import questions from Excel into Google Forms?+

Google Forms has no Excel or spreadsheet import feature for questions. Use Pirai AI: upload your .xlsx file at platform.piraiai.com, select Google Forms, download the Apps Script, open a blank Google Form > three-dot menu > Script editor, paste and run the script. All questions from the Excel file are created in the form automatically.

Can I import questions from Google Sheets into Google Forms?+

Google Forms cannot import questions from Google Sheets natively. Export your Google Sheet as Excel (.xlsx), upload to Pirai AI, select Google Forms, and run the resulting Apps Script. Alternatively, copy the question content into a Word document and upload that to Pirai AI.

What Excel format works best for Google Forms conversion?+

The most reliable layout: Column A = question text, Columns B onward = answer options, one question per row. Pirai AI also handles type-flagged columns and more complex survey script layouts. Place all questions on the first sheet (Sheet 1) before uploading.

Does Pirai AI work if my questions are on a second Excel sheet?+

Pirai AI reads the first sheet by default. If your questions are on Sheet 2 or later, move them to Sheet 1, or export just that sheet as a new .xlsx file before uploading.
