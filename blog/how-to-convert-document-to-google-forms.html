How to Convert a Document to Google Forms (2026 Guide)

Arun Raj•August 20, 2026•5 min read•Guide
Document to Google Forms in 2026

The fastest way to convert a document to Google Forms in 2026 is with Pirai AI's one-click conversion. Upload your document, click Convert to Google Forms, authorise with your Google account, and your form is created in your Drive instantly. No Script Editor. No pasting. No manual question entry. This guide covers the complete process.

Before You Start: What You Need

  • Your survey or quiz document in Word (.docx), PDF, or Excel (.xlsx) format
  • A Google account (Gmail or Google Workspace) where you want the form created
  • Access to platform.piraiai.com - free, no account required to start

How to Convert a Document to Google Forms: Step by Step

Step 1 - Upload your document. Go to platform.piraiai.com. Click Upload or drag and drop your Word, PDF, or Excel file. For scanned paper surveys in PDF format, toggle Advanced PDF Processing on before uploading.

Step 2 - Review your extracted questions. Pirai AI reads your document and displays every question in the workspace. Each question shows its detected type (Multiple Choice, Checkboxes, Short Answer, Linear Scale, etc.) and all answer options. This is your chance to edit before the form is created. Change question types, edit wording, add options, or reorder questions. The AI Survey Editor handles changes in plain language.

Step 3 - Select Google Forms and click Convert. In the platform list, select Google Forms. Click the Convert button. A Google sign-in window opens.

Step 4 - Authorise with your Google account. Select your Google account from the list. Review what Pirai AI is requesting permission to do - it asks to create a form in your Google Drive. Click Allow. This is a standard Google permission step, the same as any Google Workspace app.

Step 5 - Open your new Google Form. Pirai AI creates the form directly in your Google Drive directly in your Google Drive. A link to your new form appears in Pirai AI. Click it to open the form in Google Forms. All questions are there, with the correct types and answer options, in the order they appeared in your document.

After the Form Is Created

From the Google Forms editor, complete any additional setup: add a form title and description if not already included; enable Quiz mode under Settings > Quizzes > Make this a quiz for graded assessments; set up section-based branching by clicking the three-dot menu on any question and selecting Go to section based on answer; and customise the theme with the palette icon. When ready, click Send to distribute the form.
