Pirai AI for Sydney Universities: Convert Research Instruments and Admin Forms in Minutes

Arun Raj•August 26, 2026•5 min read•Guide
For Sydney University Researchers and Staff

Sydney's universities - the University of Sydney (USyd), UNSW Sydney, University of Technology Sydney (UTS), Macquarie University, and Western Sydney University - are among Australia's most research-active institutions. Each operates Qualtrics under institutional licences and most operate REDCap for clinical and health research. Researchers write survey instruments in Word for HREC submission, then rebuild them manually in Qualtrics or REDCap - a process that takes 4-6 hours per instrument and risks transcription errors that alter validated scale wording. Pirai AI converts any instrument document to any platform in under 15 minutes.

Survey Platforms at Sydney Universities

University of Sydney (USyd): Qualtrics available to all researchers and HDR students via institutional SSO. REDCap operated by the Sydney Informatics Hub for health and clinical research. HREC approval through the Human Research Ethics Committee.

UNSW Sydney: Qualtrics via institutional licence. REDCap through Research Technology Services for clinical and sensitive data. Kensington-based HREC plus Medicine and Health HREC for clinical research.

University of Technology Sydney (UTS): Qualtrics via UTS library institutional licence. REDCap available for health research. HREC through the UTS Human Research Ethics Committee.

Macquarie University: Qualtrics via institutional licence (library-managed). REDCap through Macquarie Health for health research. HREC through the Macquarie University Human Research Ethics Committee.

Western Sydney University (WSU): Qualtrics via institutional licence. REDCap available for health science research. Large HDR cohort across Parramatta, Campbelltown, Penrith, and Bankstown campuses. HREC through the Western Sydney University Human Research Ethics Committee.

HDR Students at Sydney Universities

Sydney universities enrol thousands of Higher Degree by Research (HDR) candidates - PhD and Masters by Research students - many of whom run primary research involving surveys. The workflow is consistent: draft the instrument in Word for supervisor review and HREC submission, wait for HREC approval, then rebuild in Qualtrics or REDCap from scratch. Pirai AI converts the HREC-approved Word instrument to Qualtrics QSF or REDCap Data Dictionary in under 15 minutes - preserving verbatim wording from the HREC-approved document exactly.

Faculty and School Administration

Beyond research, Sydney universities generate significant administrative form volume at faculty, school, and department level:

  • Student feedback surveys: End-of-semester subject evaluations and student experience surveys beyond the institutional MyExperience instrument
  • Course review and curriculum consultation: Curriculum committee consultation documents converted to Google Forms or Qualtrics for academic staff input
  • Research grant application support: Project needs assessment and stakeholder consultation questionnaires for ARC and NHMRC grant development
  • Staff wellbeing and workload surveys: Faculty-level staff surveys beyond the institution-wide People Matter survey
  • Industry partnership surveys: External stakeholder consultation instruments for professional accreditation and industry advisory boards

Western Sydney University - The Equity and Diversity Angle

Western Sydney University's research focus on equity, social justice, and Western Sydney communities means many research instruments address culturally and linguistically diverse (CALD) populations, first-in-family students, and community health. Pirai AI supports multilingual instrument conversion - survey documents in Arabic, Vietnamese, Mandarin, and other community languages convert to Qualtrics, REDCap, or Google Forms with verbatim text preserved. For WSU researchers conducting community-facing research in Western Sydney, this multilingual conversion capability is directly relevant.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Sydney universities have Qualtrics for researchers?+

USyd, UNSW, UTS, Macquarie, and Western Sydney University all provide Qualtrics via institutional licences. Access is via institutional SSO - contact your library, eResearch team, or IT services for access. HDR students typically need their supervisor to request survey creation rights at some institutions.

Yes. HDR students at USyd, UNSW, UTS, Macquarie, and Western Sydney University use Pirai AI to convert HREC-approved Word instruments to Qualtrics QSF or REDCap Data Dictionary.
