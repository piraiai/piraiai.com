Best Survey Tools for European Researchers in 2026

Arun Raj•August 05, 2026•5 min read•Guide
European Research Context

European researchers work within one of the world's strongest data protection frameworks - GDPR - while conducting research funded by Horizon Europe, ERC grants, and national funding agencies including DFG (Germany), ANR (France), NWO (Netherlands), and others. Survey platform selection is driven by GDPR compliance requirements, institutional availability, and the data management plans required by Horizon Europe funding. LimeSurvey and REDCap on EU institutional servers and Qualtrics with EU data residency are the primary options for compliant European research.

LimeSurvey - The European Open-Source Standard

LimeSurvey is headquartered in Hamburg, Germany. Self-hosted on EU servers, it is the most GDPR-compliant survey platform available - no international data transfer, no US cloud processor, full institutional data control. It is free (open source), multilingual by default, and used across European research universities particularly in Germany, France, the Netherlands, and Scandinavia.

Best for: Research requiring maximum GDPR compliance. German universities under BDSG requirements. Multilingual European studies. Institutions with IT capacity for self-hosting. DFG, ANR, NWO, and other national-funded projects requiring EU data residency.

Qualtrics - Enterprise Research Platform

Qualtrics is used at European universities under institutional licences, including many Horizon Europe research projects. With EU data centre designation (Frankfurt or Dublin) and a Data Processing Agreement, Qualtrics satisfies GDPR requirements for most research contexts. It offers superior analytics, display logic, and integrations compared to LimeSurvey - at the cost of institutional licence fees and third-party cloud processor status.

Best for: ERC-funded social science and humanities research requiring complex survey logic. European MR agencies and corporate research. Cross-institutional projects where all partners have Qualtrics access. Large-scale longitudinal studies with participant management needs.

REDCap - Clinical and Health Research

REDCap is widely used at European medical schools and research hospitals for clinical trials and health research. Major European research institutions operating REDCap include Charité Berlin, KU Leuven, Karolinska Institutet, University of Amsterdam, and dozens of NHS Trusts in the UK. For European clinical research requiring GCP compliance, REDCap on EU institutional servers is the standard.

Best for: EU clinical trials under EMA (European Medicines Agency) oversight. GDPR special category health data. Multicentre European trials requiring centralised EDC. European health research under IMI (Innovative Medicines Initiative) or EU-LIFE Alliance funding.

Survey Platforms and Horizon Europe Requirements

Horizon Europe (the EU's primary research funding programme) requires a Data Management Plan (DMP) for funded projects. The DMP must specify how research data will be collected, stored, and made available for open access where possible. Survey platforms specified in the DMP should:

  • Comply with GDPR for personal data collected from EU participants
  • Enable data export in standard open formats (CSV, SPSS) for archiving and reuse
  • Support the FAIR data principles (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable) to the extent possible for survey data

LimeSurvey and Qualtrics (with EU data residency) both support GDPR-compliant data export for Horizon Europe DMP requirements. REDCap exports CSV and SPSS-compatible formats for clinical research data.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which survey platform is best for Horizon Europe research?+

For Horizon Europe research, LimeSurvey (self-hosted on EU servers) provides the strongest GDPR compliance position and is free. Qualtrics with EU data residency and a DPA is also GDPR-compliant and offers better analytics. Both support data export in formats suitable for Horizon Europe DMP requirements. REDCap is standard for clinical research components.

What survey platforms do German universities use?+

German universities commonly use LimeSurvey for its GDPR and BDSG compliance under self-hosted EU infrastructure. Qualtrics is also used at German universities with EU data centre configuration. REDCap is standard at German university hospitals and medical schools for clinical research. LimeSurvey GmbH (the company) is itself based in Hamburg, Germany.

Is Qualtrics compliant with European GDPR for research?+

Qualtrics can be configured for GDPR compliance with EU data residency (Frankfurt or Dublin data centres) and a Data Processing Agreement under Article 28. Without EU data residency, EU-to-US data transfers require Standard Contractual Clauses and a Transfer Impact Assessment. Contact your institution's DPO and Qualtrics account team to confirm your compliance configuration.
