What Weavely Does Well

Weavely is an AI-powered form builder that generates forms from a text prompt or description. If you describe what you want ("a customer satisfaction survey for a software product with NPS and open text feedback"), Weavely generates a form structure within its own platform. For users who want to create a new form quickly without starting from scratch, Weavely's AI generation capability is useful.

Weavely forms can be shared via link, embedded in websites, and have their responses collected within the Weavely dashboard. For teams building simple forms without an existing survey document to convert from, Weavely provides a faster starting point than a blank form builder.

Where Pirai AI Is the Right Fit

The most important distinction: Weavely creates Weavely forms, not Google Forms. If your goal is to end up with a native Google Form - accessible in your Google account, distributable via Google Forms link, with responses going to Google Sheets - Weavely does not produce that output. Pirai AI generates a Google Apps Script that creates an actual Google Form inside your own Google account. The resulting form is a real Google Form, not a Weavely-hosted form.

You have an existing document to convert. Weavely generates new forms from prompts. If you have a Word document, PDF, or Excel file containing a survey that already exists, Pirai AI converts it directly. The question wording from your source document is preserved exactly - critical for validated research instruments where exact wording cannot change.

You need output to multiple platforms. Pirai AI converts the same document to Google Forms, Qualtrics, REDCap, SurveyMonkey, and 23 more platforms. Weavely's output is Weavely forms only. For teams deploying the same survey across multiple platforms simultaneously, Pirai AI is the appropriate tool.

Where Weavely Is the Right Fit

If you want to build a new form from scratch using an AI prompt and you are happy to collect responses within the Weavely platform rather than in native Google Forms or another survey platform, Weavely is a reasonable choice for simple forms. It is not a document conversion tool and should not be compared to Pirai AI for that use case.
