Qualtrics at Australian Universities: Access, HREC, and Research Setup

Arun Raj•August 04, 2026•5 min read•Guide
Qualtrics in Australian Universities

Qualtrics is the primary research survey platform at most Australian universities. Institutional licences are managed by the library, eResearch team, or IT division and provide access via Single Sign-On (SSO). Access is typically restricted to academic research purposes - using Qualtrics for operational surveys (internal staff surveys, event registrations, marketing) can breach your institution's licence agreement and risk access for all users.

How to Access Qualtrics at Your Australian University

Most Australian universities access Qualtrics via institutional SSO at [youruni].qualtrics.com. Common access URLs:

  • University of Western Australia: uwa.qualtrics.com
  • CQUniversity: cqu.qualtrics.com
  • Most other universities: [institution].qualtrics.com

If your account does not have survey creation rights upon first login, you typically need to submit an access request. At UWA, both professional staff and HDR students must submit a request declaring their use is for academic research purposes and that they are aware of the Qualtrics Terms of Service. Supervisors must submit requests on behalf of HDR students at some institutions. Allow 2-5 business days.

Academic Use Restrictions - Read Before Building

Australian university Qualtrics licences are typically restricted to academic research purposes. UWA's policy is explicit: Qualtrics can only be used for surveys with a clear academic research purpose. Surveys with an operational focus must run on an alternative platform. Non-compliance could result in the loss of ongoing access for all UWA users and financial and contractual complications for the university.

Before building a survey in Qualtrics, confirm:

  • Is this survey for academic research with an identifiable research question?
  • Does it involve human participants in a research context (requiring HREC)?
  • Is it being conducted under a research project (NHMRC grant, ARC grant, HDR project)?

If the answer is no to all three, use Microsoft Forms or Google Forms for the survey instead of your institutional Qualtrics licence.

HREC Approval and Qualtrics

All human research at Australian universities requires HREC approval under the National Statement on Ethical Conduct in Human Research. In your HREC application, you should describe your survey platform, data storage arrangements, and access controls. For institutional Qualtrics:

  • Data is stored on Qualtrics' US-based cloud infrastructure (or Australian data centre if your institution has negotiated this)
  • The university's Qualtrics licence includes data processing agreements covering privacy obligations
  • Your HREC will want to know whether participant responses are anonymous or identifiable
  • For surveys involving sensitive information, your HREC may require additional data handling justification

For research requiring data to remain on Australian infrastructure - clinical research, studies involving sensitive Indigenous data, or studies with strict data sovereignty requirements - REDCap on institutional servers is typically more appropriate than Qualtrics.

ARC and NHMRC Research on Qualtrics

ARC-funded research (Discovery Projects, Linkage Projects, DECRA, Future Fellowships, Laureate Fellowships) in social sciences, humanities, and sciences frequently uses Qualtrics for survey data collection. Qualtrics supports the data export formats (CSV, SPSS) required for quantitative analysis in ARC research outputs.

NHMRC-funded research more commonly uses REDCap for studies involving health data. However, NHMRC-funded population studies, public health surveys, and patient experience research without sensitive clinical data can use Qualtrics appropriately.

The ARC and NHMRC's April 2026 joint AI guidance explicitly allows AI as a support tool for research activities, provided accuracy and integrity remain the researcher's responsibility. Using Pirai AI to convert research instruments to Qualtrics QSF format is consistent with this guidance.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I access Qualtrics at my Australian university?+

Most Australian universities provide Qualtrics via institutional Single Sign-On at [youruni].qualtrics.com. If your account does not have survey creation rights, submit an access request through your library or eResearch team. HDR students at many institutions require their supervisor to submit the request on their behalf. Contact your institution's library or IT helpdesk to find your institution's specific process.

Can I use Qualtrics for any survey at my Australian university?+

No. Australian university Qualtrics licences are typically restricted to academic research purposes. Using Qualtrics for operational surveys (internal communications, event registrations, non-research feedback) can breach the institutional licence agreement. Check your institution's policy - at UWA, non-compliant use could result in access being withdrawn for all university users.

Does using Qualtrics comply with Australian Privacy Principles?+

Qualtrics stores data on US-based cloud infrastructure by default, which involves cross-border data transfer under APP 8. Your institution's Qualtrics licence includes data processing agreements that address privacy obligations. For research involving sensitive health information or data that must remain in Australia, REDCap on institutional servers is typically more appropriate. Confirm data storage arrangements with your Privacy Officer for sensitive research.
