Best Google Forms Converter in 2026: Pirai AI vs Formswrite vs Add-ons

Arun Raj•August 20, 2026•5 min read•Guide
Google Forms Converters in 2026

Several tools convert documents to Google Forms in 2026. They differ on what files they accept, how they create the form, and whether they also export to other platforms. This guide covers the main options - Pirai AI, Formswrite, and Workspace add-ons - and which to use for different scenarios.

Pirai AI - Direct File Upload, One Click

Pirai AI accepts Word (.docx), PDF (native and scanned), Excel (.xlsx), Qualtrics QSF, and pasted text. After uploading and reviewing questions in the workspace, click Convert to Google Forms, authorise with your Google account, and the form is created directly in your Drive directly in your Google Drive.

Strengths: Accepts direct file uploads without a Google Docs step. Works with scanned paper surveys via OCR. Also exports to 27 other platforms (Qualtrics, REDCap, LimeSurvey, SurveyMonkey, Microsoft Forms, Alchemer, Kahoot, Moodle, and more) from the same upload. Free daily credits. AI Survey Editor for conversational edits.

Best for: Researchers and educators converting existing documents in Word, PDF, or Excel formats. Teams that also need the same survey in Qualtrics, REDCap, or other platforms alongside Google Forms.

Formswrite - Google Doc Link Method

Formswrite converts Google Docs, Word, PDF, and plain text documents to Google Forms. The primary workflow uses a Google Docs link - paste the Doc URL and click Convert. Word and PDF uploads are also supported. The form is created directly in your Google Drive in your Drive.

Strengths: Fast conversion from Google Docs. Also exports to Moodle, Kahoot, Canvas, and other education platforms. Clean interface. Strong for educators.

Best for: Users who draft surveys in Google Docs and want direct one-click conversion. Education-focused users who need Moodle and Kahoot exports.

Google Workspace Add-ons

Add-ons like Doc to Form install directly into Google Docs via the Workspace Marketplace. They run inside Google Docs and convert the current document to a Google Form. Requires formatting the document in a specific structure the add-on expects, granting the add-on access to your Google account, and running the conversion from within Google Docs.

Strengths: Works entirely within Google Workspace. No third-party website needed.

Best for: Users who work exclusively in Google Docs and want the converter within the Google Workspace environment. Not practical for Word or PDF documents that are not already in Google Docs.

Which to Choose

Choose Pirai AI if your document is in Word, PDF (including scanned), or Excel format, or if you also need the same survey in Qualtrics, REDCap, LimeSurvey, or another platform alongside Google Forms.

Choose Formswrite if you draft surveys in Google Docs and want a simple one-click conversion, or if you need Google Forms and education platform outputs (Moodle, Kahoot, Canvas) but not research platform outputs (Qualtrics, REDCap).

Choose a Workspace Add-on if you exclusively work in Google Docs, need everything within Google Workspace, and your documents are already in Google Doc format.
