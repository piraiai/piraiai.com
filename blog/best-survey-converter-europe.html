Survey Conversion for European Research

European researchers face a unique combination of requirements: GDPR data protection obligations, strong preference for self-hosted survey platforms (particularly LimeSurvey), and Horizon Europe data management requirements. Pirai AI converts survey documents to all platforms used across European research institutions - LimeSurvey LSS, Qualtrics QSF, REDCap Data Dictionary, and more - with GDPR-compatible client-side processing and no persistent document storage.

The European Survey Platform Landscape

LimeSurvey is the leading survey platform for GDPR-compliance-focused European research. Headquartered in Hamburg, Germany, it is self-hosted by universities across Germany, France, the Netherlands, Belgium, Sweden, and Switzerland. German universities are particularly strong adopters - the BDSG (Bundesdatenschutzgesetz) supplements GDPR for sensitive data, and self-hosted LimeSurvey provides the strongest BDSG compliance position. It is free (open source) and involves no international data transfer when hosted on EU institutional servers.

Qualtrics is used at European universities under institutional licences, with EU data centre options (Frankfurt and Dublin) available for GDPR-compliant data residency. A Data Processing Agreement (Article 28 GDPR) is required between the institution and Qualtrics.

REDCap is the standard for European clinical trials under EMA (European Medicines Agency) oversight, used at Charité Berlin, KU Leuven, Karolinska Institutet, Amsterdam UMC, and other major European academic medical centres.

GDPR-Compatible Survey Conversion
