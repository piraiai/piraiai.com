Gemini in Google Forms vs Pirai AI

Gemini in Google Forms and Pirai AI both use AI to create Google Forms from documents. They target different users with different needs. Gemini is built into Google Forms for paid Workspace subscribers and generates forms from files already in Google Drive. Pirai AI accepts direct uploads of Word, PDF, Excel, and 10 other file types, creates the form in one click, and also converts to Qualtrics, REDCap, LimeSurvey, SurveyMonkey, and 23 more platforms from the same upload. This guide covers exactly when to use each.

What Gemini in Google Forms Does

Gemini is Google's AI assistant, integrated directly into Google Forms for eligible Workspace users. From inside Google Forms, you can prompt Gemini to generate a form from scratch or reference a file from your Google Drive. Gemini reads the file, extracts content it identifies as questions and answer options, and builds the form without leaving the Google Forms editor.

Where Gemini works well: You have a Google Doc or Google Slide in Drive containing questions. You want to create a Google Form quickly from that content. You are a paid Google Workspace subscriber. You only need a Google Form - not output to any other platform.

The catch: Gemini in Google Forms requires a paid Google Workspace plan - Business Standard, Business Plus, Enterprise, or a Google One AI Premium subscription. It is not available to free Google account users. If your school, university, or organisation does not have an eligible Workspace plan, Gemini in Google Forms is not accessible to you.

What Pirai AI Does

Pirai AI converts any document to Google Forms in one click. Upload your file at platform.piraiai.com, click Export to Google Form, authorise with your Google account, and the form is created directly in your Drive. No Workspace plan required. No subscription needed for the free daily credits. Accepts Word, PDF (native and scanned), Excel, CSV, QSF, XML, JSON, PowerPoint, and images of printed documents.

The critical difference: From the same document upload, Pirai AI also exports to Qualtrics QSF, REDCap Data Dictionary, LimeSurvey LSS, SurveyMonkey, Microsoft Forms, Alchemer, Kahoot, Moodle, and 19 more platforms. If you need the same survey in Google Forms for one group and Qualtrics for another, Pirai AI does both from one upload. Gemini only creates Google Forms.

Why Verbatim Preservation Matters

Gemini generates questions from your document - it paraphrases and rewrites content using its own language. For casual surveys and quick feedback forms, this is acceptable. For validated research instruments, clinical scales, approved ethics questionnaires, or any survey where exact wording affects data validity, this is a serious problem. Pirai AI extracts verbatim text from the source document without paraphrasing. The question text in your Google Form matches your source document character for character.

Which to Choose

Choose Gemini in Google Forms if: You have a paid Google Workspace plan with Gemini included, your documents are already in Google Drive, you only need Google Forms output, and paraphrased question generation is acceptable for your use case.

Choose Pirai AI if: You need a free option. Your document is in Word, PDF (including scanned), or Excel format that requires direct upload. You need verbatim text preservation for validated instruments. You need the same document converted to Google Forms and another platform (Qualtrics, REDCap, SurveyMonkey). You are not a paid Workspace subscriber.
