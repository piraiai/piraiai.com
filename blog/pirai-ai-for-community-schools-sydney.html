Pirai AI for Community Schools in Sydney: Multilingual, Multicultural, and Admin-Efficient

Arun Raj•August 26, 2026•5 min read•Guide
For Sydney's Community Schools

Sydney is one of the most culturally diverse cities in the world. Community schools - including community language schools (Saturday schools teaching Arabic, Mandarin, Vietnamese, Greek, and dozens of other languages), multicultural community colleges, and P-12 schools in high-density diverse areas like Western Sydney, South-West Sydney, and the Inner West - face a distinctive challenge. Their administrative documents, parent communications, and community consultation materials often need to work across multiple languages and reach communities with varying levels of digital familiarity. Pirai AI converts documents in any language to Google Forms in minutes.

Community Language Schools

NSW has more than 400 community language schools operating under the NSW Community Languages Schools Program. These Saturday schools teach languages including Arabic, Mandarin, Cantonese, Vietnamese, Greek, Italian, Hindi, Tagalog, Korean, Spanish, and many others. They are run by volunteer committees and typically have limited administrative staff.

Pirai AI converts assessment documents, student enrolment forms, parent feedback surveys, and attendance questionnaires from any language to Google Forms. A student comprehension quiz written in Arabic converts to a Google Form. A parent satisfaction survey drafted in Mandarin converts to a Microsoft Form. The verbatim text from the source document - including diacritics, non-Latin characters, and right-to-left scripts - is preserved in the converted form.

Western Sydney Community Schools

Schools in Blacktown, Parramatta, Liverpool, Fairfield, Bankstown, Penrith, and Campbelltown serve communities with high proportions of recently arrived families, refugee students, and English as Additional Language or Dialect (EAL/D) learners. Administrative forms that reach these families need to be simple, clear, and often available in community languages.

Pirai AI helps Western Sydney school administrators:

  • Convert English parent communication forms to Google Forms for digital distribution - reducing paper reliance for families with mobile phone access but limited physical mail engagement
  • Convert multilingual parent notice documents to digital forms that families can complete in their preferred language
  • Convert EAL/D assessment instruments and Language Background Other Than English (LBOTE) student intake questionnaires to Google Forms
  • Convert settlement support questionnaires for newly enrolled students from refugee backgrounds

P&C and School Community Operations

Parents and Citizens (P&C) associations at NSW public schools coordinate significant community activity - fundraising, canteen operations, uniform shops, event management, and school improvement advocacy. P&C committees typically draft forms and surveys in Word without dedicated administrative support. Converting these to Google Forms for digital completion significantly increases response rates from working families who cannot attend P&C meetings in person.

  • Annual P&C AGM agenda and resolution voting forms
  • Canteen preference and menu feedback surveys
  • School uniform shop order forms
  • Community fundraising preference surveys
  • Working bee and volunteer availability forms
  • After-school activity interest registration forms

Multilingual Support

Pirai AI processes documents in all major community languages represented in Sydney's school population: Arabic, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Vietnamese, Hindi, Tagalog, Korean, Turkish, Spanish, Italian, Greek, and more. Verbatim text is preserved from the source document in the converted Google Form - including non-Latin scripts and diacritics. For community schools running bilingual programmes or dual-language newsletters, both language versions can be converted separately and distributed via the same Google Form link.
