REDCap in Canada: CIHR Research, Health Data Sovereignty, and Access

Arun Raj•August 05, 2026•5 min read•Guide
REDCap in Canadian Research

REDCap is the standard electronic data capture platform for CIHR-funded clinical research and studies involving sensitive health information in Canada. Canadian university health science centres, academic hospitals, and research institutes operate REDCap instances under institutional data governance. Provincial health privacy laws (PHIPA in Ontario, PIPA in BC, HIA in Alberta) impose additional requirements beyond PIPEDA for health data - REDCap on Canadian institutional servers satisfies these requirements.

Canadian Institutions with REDCap

REDCap is confirmed at major Canadian health research institutions:

  • University of Toronto / University Health Network - REDCap operated by the UHN Research Institute
  • McGill University / McGill University Health Centre (MUHC) - REDCap for MUHC-affiliated clinical research
  • UBC / BC Children's Hospital Research Institute - REDCap for paediatric and health research
  • University of Alberta / Alberta Health Services - REDCap under the Alberta data governance framework
  • McMaster University - REDCap via the Health Sciences research infrastructure
  • University of Ottawa / Ottawa Hospital Research Institute - REDCap for OHRI-affiliated studies
  • Dalhousie University, Queen's University, University of Calgary - REDCap confirmed via consortium membership

CIHR Requirements and REDCap

CIHR (Canadian Institutes of Health Research) funds health research across four pillars: biomedical, clinical, health systems, and population/public health. For CIHR-funded clinical trials and studies involving health data:

  • Clinical trials under Health Canada oversight must use GCP-compliant EDC systems - REDCap on validated institutional infrastructure satisfies this requirement
  • CIHR's Sex and Gender-Based Analysis Plus (SGBA+) approach may require specific data collection fields that REDCap's flexible data dictionary accommodates
  • CIHR grant agreements for clinical studies may specify institutional data management requirements consistent with REDCap's capabilities

Canadian Health Privacy Laws and REDCap

Health data in Canada is subject to provincial health privacy laws in addition to PIPEDA:

  • PHIPA (Ontario) - Personal Health Information Protection Act. Requires that personal health information be kept on Canadian servers or with adequate offshore protections. REDCap on UHN or University of Toronto servers satisfies PHIPA requirements.
  • HIA (Alberta) - Health Information Act. Similar Canadian-server preference for personal health information held by custodians.
  • Quebec Law 25 - Requires Privacy Impact Assessments for cross-border transfers of personal information including health data. REDCap on Quebec institutional servers avoids cross-border transfer entirely.

REDCap on Canadian institutional servers satisfies all of these provincial health privacy requirements without the complexity of cross-border transfer mechanisms.

Common Canadian Clinical Instruments in REDCap

  • PROMIS measures - widely used in CIHR-funded Canadian clinical trials
  • PHQ-9 and GAD-7 - standard mental health screening in Canadian primary care research
  • SF-36 and EQ-5D-5L - health-related quality of life in Canadian health technology assessment
  • AUDIT-C - alcohol use screening in Canadian population health studies
  • MINI International Neuropsychiatric Interview - clinical assessment in Canadian psychiatry research

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Canadian universities have REDCap?+

REDCap is available at most Canadian health research universities including University of Toronto/UHN, McGill/MUHC, UBC, University of Alberta, McMaster, University of Ottawa/OHRI, Dalhousie, Queen's, and University of Calgary. Contact your institution's research institute, clinical research unit, or health sciences IT team.

Is REDCap required for CIHR-funded research in Canada?+

CIHR does not universally mandate REDCap, but CIHR-funded clinical trials under Health Canada oversight must use GCP-compliant EDC systems - REDCap on validated institutional infrastructure satisfies this. Some CIHR grant conditions or sponsor agreements for clinical studies specify data management requirements consistent with REDCap capabilities.

Does REDCap comply with PHIPA and Canadian provincial health privacy laws?+

REDCap on Canadian institutional servers satisfies PHIPA (Ontario), HIA (Alberta), and similar provincial health privacy requirements by keeping personal health information on Canadian infrastructure with no cross-border transfer. This is a significant compliance advantage over US-hosted cloud platforms for health data in Canadian research.
