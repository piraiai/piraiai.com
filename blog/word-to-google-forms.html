Word to Google Forms: Convert a .docx File to a Google Form

Arun Raj•August 13, 2026•5 min read•Guide
Word to Google Forms

Converting a Word document to Google Forms is not straightforward - Google Forms has no Word import feature. You cannot upload a .docx file and have your questions appear. The practical method is to use Pirai AI to convert the Word document to a Google Apps Script, then run that script in your Google Form to build it automatically. Takes under 15 minutes for most surveys.

Why Google Forms Cannot Import Word Documents Directly

Google Forms is a form builder, not a document processor. Its interface is built around clicking to add individual questions - there is no file upload, no paste import, and no connection to Google Drive documents for question import. The only "import" available is bringing in questions from another existing Google Form within your account.

The Apps Script approach bypasses this limitation entirely. Google Forms runs on Google's Apps Script platform, which has full access to the Forms API. A script can create any question type, add sections, set answer options, and configure the form structure - exactly what Pirai AI generates from your Word document.

Word Document Structures Pirai AI Handles

  • Numbered question lists: Q1, Q2, Q3 format with lettered options below each question
  • Heading-separated sections: Word headings used as section dividers become Google Forms sections
  • Tables: Matrix-style tables with row items and column scale options become Multiple Choice Grid questions
  • Bulleted answer lists: Bulleted or dashed option lists under each question become answer choices
  • Instruction text: "Select all that apply" cues are detected and trigger Checkboxes question type
  • Open-ended indicators: "Please describe", "Explain in your own words", and similar text creates Paragraph questions

Word to Google Forms: Complete Steps

1. Upload your Word file. Go to platform.piraiai.com. Upload your .docx file.

2. Review extracted questions. Pirai AI displays every detected question with its type and answer options. Change any question types if needed - use the AI Survey Editor for changes like "convert Q4 to a dropdown" or "add an Other option to Q7".

3. Export to Google Forms. Select Google Forms. Download the .gs Apps Script file.

4. Open a blank Google Form. Go to forms.google.com. Open a new blank form.

5. Open the Script Editor. Click the three-dot menu in the top-right of the form editor. Select Script editor. A new tab opens with the Apps Script editor.

6. Paste and run the script. Delete any existing code in the editor. Paste the Pirai AI script. Click the Run button (triangle icon). If prompted, click Review Permissions and authorise the script to edit your Google Form - this is a standard Google Apps Script permission request.

7. Return to the form. Go back to your Google Form tab and refresh. All questions from your Word document are now in the form.

After Word-to-Forms Conversion

  • Review all questions for correct type and complete answer options
  • Set up section-based routing (skip logic) via the three-dot menu on each relevant question
  • Enable Quiz mode in Settings > Quizzes > Make this a quiz if graded responses are needed
  • Set required questions using the Required toggle on each question
  • Customise the theme in the palette icon at the top of the editor

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I upload a Word document directly to Google Forms?+

No. Google Forms has no Word or document upload feature. Use Pirai AI to convert your Word document to a Google Apps Script, then run the script in your Google Form's Script Editor. This is the standard method for creating Google Forms from existing Word documents.

How long does Word to Google Forms conversion take?+

Upload and processing takes 1-3 minutes. Review in the Pirai AI workspace takes 5-10 minutes for a typical 25-question document. Running the script in Google Forms takes under a minute. Total: 10-15 minutes from Word document to complete Google Form, versus 45-90 minutes of manual entry.

Does the Word-to-Google-Forms conversion preserve formatting?+

Question text and answer options are preserved verbatim from the Word document. Google Forms has limited formatting compared to Word - bold text in question labels may not transfer, but all question content transfers accurately. Section breaks from Word headings become Google Forms sections.

Do I need a Google Workspace account or will a free Gmail account work?+

A free Gmail account works. The Apps Script generated by Pirai AI runs in the standard Google Apps Script editor available in any Google account. No Google Workspace subscription is required.
