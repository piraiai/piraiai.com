How to Import a Survey Into QuestionPro: Step-by-Step Guide

Arun Raj•September 1, 2026•6 min read•Guide
Importing Into QuestionPro

QuestionPro's native question builder requires entering questions one at a time. For researchers and agencies converting existing instruments from Word, PDF, or Excel, manual entry is time-consuming and error-prone. Pirai AI is an official QuestionPro partner that converts any document directly into a QuestionPro survey - all questions, answer options, and question types created automatically, without manual entry.

Method 1 - Convert via Pirai AI (Recommended)

As an official QuestionPro partner, Pirai AI creates surveys directly in your QuestionPro account from any document.

1. Upload your document at platform.piraiai.com. Accepted formats: Word (.docx), PDF (native or scanned), Excel (.xlsx), Qualtrics QSF, and pasted text.

2. Review in the workspace. Every extracted question appears with its detected QuestionPro question type and answer options. Verify types are correct - Single Choice, Multiple Choice, Rating Scale, Matrix, Open Text, NPS, Rank Order. Edit any question or type before creating the survey.

3. Select QuestionPro. Click QuestionPro as your output platform. If this is your first conversion, connect your QuestionPro account.

4. Your survey is created in QuestionPro. All questions appear in your QuestionPro account ready for configuration. Pirai AI handles the conversion and creation - no manual question entry required.

5. Configure in QuestionPro. Open the created survey in QuestionPro. Add skip logic, configure display conditions, set up quotas, apply your survey theme, and configure distribution settings.

Method 2 - QuestionPro Text Import

QuestionPro's native text import accepts a structured plain-text format for bulk question entry. This is an alternative to manual question-by-question entry in the builder, but requires formatting your questions in QuestionPro's specific text import syntax. The text import is less flexible than Pirai AI's automated conversion and requires manual formatting work for complex question types.

For surveys with more than 10 questions or with Matrix, Rank Order, or Slider question types, Pirai AI's automated conversion is significantly faster and more accurate than manual text import formatting.

Post-Import Configuration in QuestionPro

After questions are created in QuestionPro, complete the survey setup:

  • Skip Logic: Route respondents to different questions based on answers - click on a question > Add Logic > Skip Logic
  • Display Logic: Show/hide questions conditionally - Add Logic > Display Logic
  • Quota Management: Set response quotas by demographic or answer criteria
  • Survey Theme: Apply your brand colours, logo, and styling from the Design tab
  • Distribution: Configure email distribution, shareable link, embedded survey, or offline collection
  • Piping: Insert previous answers into later question text using QuestionPro's piping syntax

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I import a Word document into QuestionPro?+

Upload your Word document to Pirai AI at platform.piraiai.com. Select QuestionPro as the output. As an official QuestionPro partner, Pirai AI creates the survey directly in your QuestionPro account with all questions and answer options in place. No manual entry required.

Does QuestionPro have a direct document import feature?+

QuestionPro does not natively import Word or PDF files. Pirai AI, as an official QuestionPro partner, bridges this gap - converting any document format to a QuestionPro survey automatically. QuestionPro's native text import option requires manual formatting in a specific syntax, which is more time-consuming for complex surveys.

What question types does Pirai AI create in QuestionPro from a Word document?+

Single Choice, Multiple Choice, Rating Scale, Matrix/Grid, Open Text, NPS, Rank Order, Slider, and Dropdown - all auto-detected from question structure in your Word, PDF, or Excel document. Review and adjust detected types in the Pirai AI workspace before creating the survey.

Can I convert a Qualtrics QSF survey to QuestionPro?+

Yes. Export your Qualtrics survey as a QSF file (Tools > Import/Export > Export Survey). Upload the QSF to Pirai AI. Select QuestionPro. Pirai AI converts the Qualtrics question types to QuestionPro equivalents and creates the survey in your QuestionPro account.
