Excel to LimeSurvey: Convert a Spreadsheet to a LimeSurvey Survey

Arun Raj•August 13, 2026•5 min read•Guide
Excel to LimeSurvey

Question banks and survey instruments maintained in Excel are common across research teams, MR agencies, and enterprise survey programmes. LimeSurvey has no Excel import feature. Pirai AI converts your .xlsx question bank to a .lss file for direct LimeSurvey import - no manual rebuilding, no transcription risk.

Excel Structures Pirai AI Reads

  • Question + answer columns: Column A = question text, Columns B-F = answer options. One question per row.
  • Type-flagged columns: A column indicating MC, OT, or Matrix. Pirai AI uses the flag to assign the correct LimeSurvey question type.
  • Survey scripts with coding: Question text, response options, and recode values across multiple columns. Pirai AI extracts question and option text.
  • Simple numbered lists: Q1, Q2 with options listed in subsequent rows or columns.

Pirai AI reads the first sheet of the .xlsx file. If your questions are on another sheet, move them to Sheet 1 before uploading.

Who Converts Excel to LimeSurvey

  • Research teams converting multi-language question banks from Excel to LimeSurvey for multilingual studies
  • NGOs converting programme evaluation questionnaires from Excel to self-hosted LimeSurvey
  • IT administrators migrating legacy question banks into new LimeSurvey instances
  • Academics converting validated instrument spreadsheets to LimeSurvey for GDPR-compliant data collection

Excel to LimeSurvey: Steps

1. Upload your .xlsx file at platform.piraiai.com. Pirai AI reads the first sheet and extracts questions and answer options.

2. Review extracted questions in the workspace. Confirm question types, check answer option completeness.

3. Select LimeSurvey. Download the .lss file.

4. Import into LimeSurvey via LimeSurvey Admin panel > + button > Import > upload the .lss file.

5. Complete setup in LimeSurvey: verify the db version selection matches your limesurvey installation, activate the survey in the admin panel before sending to participants.

Using Excel as the Source of Truth

Many teams maintain their question bank in Excel for version control, review, and tracking across study waves or survey cycles. Upload the current version to Pirai AI at each survey launch, convert to LimeSurvey, and import. The Excel file remains the approved master; LimeSurvey receives the current deployment version.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I import Excel into LimeSurvey directly?+

LimeSurvey has no Excel import feature for questions. Use Pirai AI to convert your .xlsx file to a .lss file, then import into LimeSurvey via LimeSurvey Admin panel.

What Excel format works best for LimeSurvey conversion?+

The most reliable layout: Column A = question text, Columns B onward = answer options, one question per row. Pirai AI also handles type-flagged columns and survey script layouts. Place all questions on Sheet 1 before uploading.

Can I maintain my survey in Excel and convert to LimeSurvey each wave?+

Yes. This is a common workflow for multi-wave studies and recurring survey programmes. Maintain the question bank in Excel for version control and team review, upload to Pirai AI at each launch, convert to LimeSurvey format, and import. The Excel file is the approved source; LimeSurvey receives each wave's questions.

Does Pirai AI work with Google Sheets exported to Excel?+

Yes. Export your Google Sheet as .xlsx (File > Download > Microsoft Excel). Upload the .xlsx to Pirai AI, select LimeSurvey, and download the .lss file for import.
