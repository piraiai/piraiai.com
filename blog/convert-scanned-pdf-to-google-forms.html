Scan Quality and OCR Accuracy

OCR accuracy depends directly on scan quality. Here is what affects results:

ConditionOCR Result
Clear, straight, high-resolution scanExcellent - near-perfect extraction
Good phone photo, well-lit, flat documentVery good - minor corrections may be needed
Slightly angled scan or photoGood - most text captured, some edge words may need checking
Low-resolution scan (below 150 DPI)Fair - review all extracted questions
Handwritten textPartial - printed words extracted, handwriting may need manual correction
Heavy shadow, crumpled paper, or blurred photoReduced - rescan or rephotograph recommended
Tips for best results: Photograph on a flat surface in natural or even artificial light. Avoid shadows across the page. Hold the phone directly above the document rather than at an angle. A free scanner app set to 200 DPI or higher will give better OCR results than a basic phone camera photo.

Question Types Detected from Scanned PDFs

Pirai AI detects and creates all 8 native Google Forms question types from scanned content:

  • Multiple Choice - questions with lettered (A, B, C) or numbered (1, 2, 3) options
  • Checkboxes - "select all that apply" and multi-select questions with checkbox symbols
  • Short Answer - blank lines indicating a written response expected
  • Paragraph - larger blank areas for essay or long-form responses
  • Linear Scale - numbered rating scales (1 to 5, 1 to 10)
  • Multiple Choice Grid - Likert scale tables with row items and column scale labels
  • Checkbox Grid - multi-select matrix grids
  • Section titles - headings and instruction blocks become form section titles

Matrix and Likert scale grids are the question type most other converters miss entirely. Pirai AI reads the row and column structure from the scan and creates a native Multiple Choice Grid in Google Forms.

Scanned PDF vs Native PDF - Which to Use

If you have access to the original digital document, use that instead. Native PDFs (where you can highlight and copy the text) convert faster and with higher accuracy than scanned PDFs. Advanced Processing is only needed when:

  • The document was originally printed and never existed as a digital file
  • You only have a physical copy of the questionnaire
  • The PDF was created by a scanner or photocopier
  • The PDF was faxed and saved as an image
  • You are photographing a paper survey for quick digitisation

For native PDFs, upload without enabling Advanced Processing. Pirai AI reads the text layer directly - faster and no OCR errors.
