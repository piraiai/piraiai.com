REDCap vs Qualtrics: Which Survey Platform Should Researchers Use?

Arun Raj•August 28, 2026•6 min read•Guide
REDCap vs Qualtrics

REDCap and Qualtrics are the two dominant research survey platforms at universities worldwide. They are not interchangeable - they serve different research contexts and have fundamentally different architectures. REDCap is open-source, runs on institutional servers, and is the standard for clinical and health research. Qualtrics is enterprise SaaS, runs in the cloud, and is standard for social science, CX, and population survey research. This guide covers when each is the right choice.

Use REDCap When

  • Your research involves Protected Health Information (PHI) or sensitive health data
  • You are running an FDA-regulated clinical trial requiring GCP-aligned audit trails
  • Your study is longitudinal with multiple time points, repeat instruments, or multiple arms
  • Your ethics approval or institutional policy requires data on institutional servers
  • You need built-in randomisation, data quality rules, or clinical trial management features
  • You are at an institution in the REDCap Consortium (6,500+ institutions - no additional cost)

Use Qualtrics When

  • Your research is social science, psychology, marketing, education, or CX focused
  • You need advanced survey features: conjoint, MaxDiff, heatmap questions, video embedding
  • You want real-time dashboard analytics and cross-tabulation in the platform
  • You need to distribute to an external panel (Qualtrics XM Directory or partner panels)
  • Your study does not involve PHI and cloud data storage is acceptable to your institution
  • Your institution has a Qualtrics licence (most major universities now do)

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I use REDCap or Qualtrics for my research?+

Use REDCap for clinical research involving PHI, GCP-regulated trials, longitudinal studies with repeat instruments, and research requiring data on institutional servers. Use Qualtrics for social science, psychology, education, marketing, and CX research where cloud data storage is acceptable and advanced survey features (conjoint, panels, dashboards) are needed.

Is REDCap HIPAA compliant?+

REDCap on institutional servers is HIPAA compliant - data stays within your institution's own infrastructure covered by your HIPAA Security Rule compliance programme. No Business Associate Agreement with a third-party cloud vendor is required. This is REDCap's key advantage over Qualtrics for US health research involving PHI.

Can I use Qualtrics for clinical research?+

Qualtrics can be used for non-GCP clinical research surveys when HIPAA compliance is configured (enterprise plan with BAA). For FDA-regulated clinical trials requiring GCP-aligned audit trails, longitudinal data with defined events and arms, and validated system compliance, REDCap is the more appropriate choice.
