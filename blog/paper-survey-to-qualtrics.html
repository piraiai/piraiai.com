How to Convert a Paper Survey to Qualtrics (2026 Guide)

Arun Raj•July 18, 2026•3 min read•Glossary
Definition

Converting a paper survey to Qualtrics involves three steps: scanning the paper document to PDF, processing the PDF to extract the text (via OCR if scanned), and converting the extracted content to a Qualtrics QSF file for import. Pirai AI handles all three input types and converts directly to Qualtrics QSF.

Why Teams Digitise Paper Surveys

Paper surveys are still common in clinical research, community fieldwork, legacy programme evaluation, and any research context where respondents lack reliable internet access. When a programme moves to digital data collection or when historical paper instruments need to be re-fielded online, converting the paper survey to Qualtrics avoids rebuilding the instrument from scratch and preserves the exact question wording required for longitudinal comparability.

Common scenarios include: clinical researchers digitising case report forms for electronic data capture in Qualtrics or REDCap; educational researchers converting validated paper instruments to Qualtrics for online administration; organisations digitising legacy satisfaction surveys that have been used in paper format for years and need to move online without changing the question structure.

Step 1: Scan to PDF

Scan the paper survey using any scanner, multifunction printer, or scanning app (Adobe Scan, Microsoft Lens, Apple Notes) at a minimum resolution of 300 DPI for standard printed text. Higher resolution (600 DPI) is better for small print or dense question layouts. Scan in greyscale or colour - black and white scans sometimes lose fine print. Export as PDF rather than individual JPEGs so the full document is one file.

For multi-page paper surveys, ensure all pages are included in the scan in the correct order. If using a phone camera, use a scanning app rather than a standard photo - scanning apps apply perspective correction and contrast enhancement that significantly improves text recognition in the next step.

Step 2: OCR and Prepare the PDF

OCR (optical character recognition) converts the scanned image to selectable, searchable text. There are two routes:

Option A: Upload the scanned PDF directly to Pirai AI. Pirai AI's Advanced PDF Processing applies OCR to extract text from scanned documents, then converts the extracted structure to Qualtrics QSF. This is the fastest route for most paper surveys - one upload handles scanning, OCR, and conversion in a single step.

Option B: OCR first, then upload. Use Adobe Acrobat, Google Drive (which applies OCR when you open a PDF in Docs), or a free OCR tool to convert the scanned PDF to a text-searchable PDF or Word document. Then upload the resulting file to Pirai AI. This two-step approach gives you a chance to review and correct the OCR output before conversion, which can improve accuracy for surveys with complex formatting or poor scan quality.

Step 3: Convert to Qualtrics

Upload the scanned PDF (or OCR-processed Word document) to platform.piraiai.com. Select Qualtrics as the target platform. Pirai AI reads the document structure, identifies questions, answer options, question types, and scale labels, and generates a QSF file for Qualtrics import. Review the extracted structure in the AI Survey Editor before downloading - correct any OCR errors in question text or scale labels directly in the editor.

To import: In Qualtrics, go to the Survey tab. Click Tools (top right) then Import Survey. Select your QSF file. The survey loads into the Qualtrics editor with all questions, answer options, and formatting from the paper survey. Add branching logic, embedded data, and randomisation in Qualtrics as needed after import.

Handling Handwritten Surveys

If the paper survey contains handwritten annotations, custom question additions, or handwritten answer options alongside printed text, OCR accuracy will be lower than for purely printed documents. For handwritten surveys, review the AI Survey Editor output carefully before downloading the QSF - look particularly for question text that may have been partially misread and for answer options that may have been merged or split incorrectly.

For surveys where the handwritten content is critical (e.g. handwritten in an additional language, or handwritten additions to a printed form), the two-step approach (OCR to Word first, review and correct, then convert) is more reliable than the direct PDF upload route.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Pirai AI convert scanned paper surveys to Qualtrics?+

Yes. Pirai AI's Advanced PDF Processing applies OCR to scanned PDFs to extract text, then converts the extracted survey structure to Qualtrics QSF format for direct import. What scan resolution is best for paper survey conversion?+

Scan at a minimum of 300 DPI for standard printed text. 600 DPI is better for small print, dense formatting, or surveys with footnotes and scale labels in smaller type. Use a scanning app on your phone (Adobe Scan, Microsoft Lens) rather than a standard camera photo for better perspective correction and contrast.

What happens if the OCR misreads my paper survey?+

Review the extracted survey structure in Pirai AI's AI Survey Editor before downloading the QSF file. Correct any OCR errors directly in the editor - fix question text, adjust scale labels, or remove duplicated text. For surveys with frequent OCR errors, run the PDF through Adobe Acrobat or Google Drive OCR first to produce a corrected Word document, then upload that to Pirai AI.

Can I convert a paper survey to REDCap instead of Qualtrics?+

Yes. Upload the scanned PDF to Pirai AI and select REDCap as the target platform instead of Qualtrics. The output is a REDCap data dictionary CSV for direct import into a new REDCap project. This is common for clinical researchers digitising paper case report forms for electronic data capture.
