Survey Platforms at US Universities

US R1 and R2 universities typically provide two or three survey platforms through institutional licences. Qualtrics is the dominant research platform - it was founded at Brigham Young University and institutional licences are now standard at most AAU universities (the 65 top US research institutions). REDCap is the standard for NIH-funded clinical research and is operated by more than 6,000 institutions worldwide through the Vanderbilt-managed REDCap Consortium. SurveyMonkey is widely used by market research teams and non-academic survey programmes.

IRB Approval and Your Survey Platform

All research involving human participants at US universities requires Institutional Review Board (IRB) approval under the Common Rule (45 CFR 46) before data collection begins. Your IRB protocol must specify the survey platform and data storage arrangements. Institutional Qualtrics (US cloud, covered by university data use agreements) and institutional REDCap (university servers) are well-understood by US IRBs and typically approved without additional justification. Key considerations for your IRB protocol:

  • Where is data stored? Qualtrics stores data on US-based cloud infrastructure. REDCap stores data on your institution's own servers. Google Forms (personal accounts) stores on Google's US infrastructure without institutional data use agreements.
  • Is the survey anonymous or identifiable? Anonymous online surveys typically qualify for expedited IRB review. Identifiable surveys require full board review with stronger data governance justification.
  • HIPAA compliance: For surveys collecting Protected Health Information (PHI), the platform must have a Business Associate Agreement (BAA). REDCap on institutional servers satisfies HIPAA requirements. Qualtrics has a HIPAA-compliant option on enterprise plans with BAA.

Frequently Asked Questions

What survey platforms do US universities provide for research?+

Most US R1 and AAU universities provide Qualtrics for general academic research surveys and REDCap for clinical and sensitive health research. SurveyMonkey is available for market research. Google Forms and Microsoft Forms are used for teaching and low-risk surveys. Platform access depends on your institution - contact your library, IT services, or research computing team.

Do US researchers need IRB approval for surveys?+

Yes. All research involving human participants at US universities requires Institutional Review Board (IRB) approval under the Common Rule (45 CFR 46) before data collection. Some minimal-risk surveys qualify for expedited review, but IRB approval is still required. Your IRB protocol must specify the survey platform and data handling arrangements.
