The Fastest Way to Create a Google Form From a Document

Arun Raj•August 20, 2026•5 min read•Guide
The Fastest Method

Manual question entry in Google Forms takes 45-90 minutes for a 30-question survey. Pirai AI takes 5-10 minutes for the same survey - upload, review, click Convert, authorise, done. Your form is in your Drive. This is the fastest way to turn a document into a Google Form.

What Makes It Fast

No reformatting. You do not need to restructure your Word document before uploading. Pirai AI reads the document as it is and extracts questions from numbered lists, bullet points, headings, and tables automatically.

No intermediate steps. You do not need to copy the document into Google Docs first. You do not need to install an add-on. You do not need to open a Script Editor or paste any code. Upload your document directly.

No manual question entry. Every question is extracted from the document automatically. You review - not rebuild. Editing in the workspace is faster than building from scratch because the question structure is already there.

Direct to Drive. One click and one Google authorisation creates the form directly in your Drive directly in your Google Drive. No downloading a file and importing it. No opening the form builder and starting blank.

The One-Click Process

1. Upload your Word, PDF, or Excel document at platform.piraiai.com (1-3 minutes).

2. Review extracted questions in the workspace. Fix any question types or wording if needed (2-5 minutes for most surveys).

3. Click Convert to Google Forms (under 1 minute).

4. Authorise with your Google account - sign in and click Allow (under 1 minute).

5. Open your new Google Form in Drive. All questions are in place. (Immediate)

Total: 5-10 minutes from document to live Google Form in your Drive.

The Only Things You Set Up Afterwards

The form structure and all questions are created automatically. The only things left to do in Google Forms: enable Quiz mode if this is an assessment (Settings > Quizzes), add section-based branching if the form routes respondents to different sections, and apply a theme. These take 2-5 minutes for most surveys.
