How to Test a Qualtrics Survey Link Before Launch

Arun Raj • October 5, 2026 • 9 min read

The survey has been written, reviewed and built. Every question is in Qualtrics, the logic is in place, and the link is ready to send. The only thing left is to check that it actually works.

That last check is where most survey problems slip through. A follow up question that never appears. A required question that lets people skip it. An Other box that accepts a blank. None of them show up until real responses arrive, and by then the data is already affected.

This guide covers what to check before a Qualtrics survey goes live, the three ways to test it, and how to prove every route works without clicking through the link by hand for an afternoon.

What to Check Before a Survey Goes Live

A survey link can look perfect in preview and still fail on the routes nobody tried. These are the areas that cause the most problems after launch.

The routes are the hard part

Checking one path through a survey is quick. Checking every path is not. A survey with three routing questions of three answers each has up to 27 combinations, and each one is a full pass through the survey. That is why routing defects are the ones that most often reach respondents.

Three Ways to Test a Qualtrics Survey

Each method answers a different question. Most teams need more than one.

1. Preview the survey yourself

Preview opens the survey as a respondent sees it, and your answers don't count towards your auditable responses. It is the best way to read the survey as a respondent would, but every route is a manual pass, and there is no record of which answers you tried.

2. Generate test responses

Qualtrics can generate random dummy data in bulk from the Tools menu, including random text for text entry questions. It is ideal for checking your dataset, exports and reports before launch, and these responses don't count towards your license limits. Because the answers are random, it isn't designed to follow a particular route, and Qualtrics notes that custom validation isn't applied to test responses.

3. Test the live link automatically

How to Prove Every Route With a Test Plan

A test plan lists the questions that drive your routing and the answers to try for each. Pirai QA Runner turns it into scenarios and runs each one in a fresh session.

  • 1
    Load every question Open the survey in the Qualtrics survey builder in another tab and click Load from survey builder, or upload a QSF. Every question appears in the panel, grouped by block, with required questions marked.
  • 2
    Pick the questions that drive routing Click a question and choose Vary across scenarios. Tick the answers to try, for example Very satisfied, Neutral and Very dissatisfied. Questions that should stay the same in every run can be kept fixed instead.
  • 3
    Preview the scenarios Choose Each value at least once for the fewest runs, or All combinations to test how two drivers interact. Untick any scenario you don't need.
  • 4
    Run the plan on the preview link Each scenario starts in a fresh session. Pinned questions get exactly the answers you chose; everything else is answered randomly from a seed.
  • 5
    Read the result for each scenario A pinned question is reported as applied, not shown, or not applied with a reason. A question that never appears in a route where it should is your routing defect.

How many scenarios do you need?

Each value at least once gives the fewest runs: the number of scenarios equals the largest number of answers on any one routing question. Three questions with three answers each need only three scenarios this way, instead of 27 for every combination.

How to Check Required Questions

Qualtrics has two kinds of required question. Force response blocks the respondent until they answer. Request response shows a prompt and lets them continue. Either can be set on a question whether or not the required asterisk is shown, because hiding the asterisk is a display setting.

The reliable check is behaviour, not appearance: click Next before answering and see what stops you. With required checks turned on, Pirai QA Runner does this on every page with required questions and reports one of three results:

  • Pass: marked required, and Next is blocked until it is answered.
  • Fail: marked required, but Next goes through on an empty page. Force response is probably off.
  • Flag: not marked required, but Next is blocked. Usually custom validation, or a setting nobody expected.

It also checks Other, please specify boxes: it picks the choice with the box, leaves the box empty and clicks Next. If Next is blocked, the box is required, and the runner fills it in and carries on.

Test on the preview link

Answers submitted through a live distribution link are recorded as real responses and count towards quotas. Use the preview link for testing wherever you can, and remove any test responses before analysis.

Keep Evidence of What Was Tested

When a client or stakeholder asks what was checked before launch, "we clicked through it" is not much of an answer. After a run or a plan, Pirai QA Runner lets you download:

  • Results: one row per question per page for a run, or one row per pinned answer per scenario for a plan.
  • Answers given: every answer in readable text, marked as pinned or random.
  • Required checks: one row per check, with pass, fail, flag, required or optional.

Every file opens in Excel. Answers come from a seed, so the same seed reproduces any run exactly after a fix, and you can show that the problem is gone.

A Checklist Before You Launch

  • Every routing question has been tested with each answer that changes the route.
  • Every question that should be required blocks Next when it is left empty.
  • Every Other, please specify box that should need text rejects a blank.
  • Validation messages appear for invalid values and not for valid ones.
  • Every route reaches the end of survey message.
  • The dataset and exports look right, using generated test responses.
  • Test responses have been removed or filtered out before analysis.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I test a Qualtrics survey before sending it?

Preview the survey yourself, generate test responses to check the dataset and reports, and test the live link for each route through the survey. Check routing, required questions, Other boxes, validation and the end of survey page before launch.

What is the difference between preview and generating test responses in Qualtrics?

Preview lets you answer the survey yourself without the response counting towards your auditable responses. Generate test responses creates random dummy data in bulk, which is useful for checking your dataset and reports, but the answers are random rather than chosen to follow a particular route.

How do I test skip logic and display logic in Qualtrics?

List the questions that drive your routing, decide which answers lead to each route, and run the survey once per route with those answers. Check that the questions you expect appear, and that the ones you don't expect stay hidden. A test plan in Pirai QA Runner does this automatically, one scenario per route.

How can I check that required questions are really required?

Click Next on a page without answering and see which questions block you. A question that is meant to be required but lets you through has Force response turned off. Pirai QA Runner does this check on every page and reports questions that are required but not enforced, or enforced but not marked.

Can I test a Qualtrics survey when the required asterisk is hidden?

Yes. Hiding the asterisk is a display setting; the question can still be required. Pirai QA Runner reads the required setting from the page even when the asterisk is hidden, and confirms it by clicking Next before answering.

Do test runs on a live link count as responses?

Yes. Answers submitted through a live distribution link are recorded as real responses and count towards quotas. Test on the preview link where you can, and remove any test responses before analysis.

What is Pirai QA Runner?

Pirai QA Runner is a free Chrome extension that tests Qualtrics survey links. It answers every question on each page, clicks Next until the end, follows test plans that pin the answers driving your routing, checks required questions, and lets you download the results as CSV files.

Does Pirai QA Runner use AI?

No. Answers come from a seed, so the same seed on the same survey always gives the same answers. Questions you pin in a test plan always get the answers you chose, so every run can be repeated exactly.

Is my survey data sent anywhere?

No. Pirai QA Runner runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent to Pirai AI or any other server, and files are saved to your computer only when you download them.
