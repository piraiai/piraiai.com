Why You Should Not Use a Google Workspace Add-On

Tools like Automagical Forms require you to install a Google Workspace Marketplace add-on, grant permissions to your Google Drive, and run inside Google Drive. If your institution restricts Workspace Marketplace installs, or you prefer not to give third-party add-ons access to your Drive, this is a problem.

Pirai AI is web-based with no Marketplace add-on required for either method. The Apps Script method requires zero Drive permissions to Pirai AI - the script runs inside your own Google account. The one-click OAuth export uses scoped drive.file access (creates new forms only - no access to existing files, folders, or documents). Neither method requires a Workspace Marketplace installation.
