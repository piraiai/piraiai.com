Best Survey Tools for Canadian Researchers in 2026

Arun Raj•August 05, 2026•5 min read•Guide
The Canadian Research Context

Canadian researchers work within the TCPS 2 (Tri-Council Policy Statement: Ethical Conduct for Research Involving Humans) ethics framework, with REB (Research Ethics Board) approval required for all human research. The three main funding bodies - NSERC, SSHRC, and CIHR - together form the Tri-Council. Survey platform choice is shaped by REB requirements, PIPEDA (federal privacy law), provincial privacy laws, and institutional licence availability. Pirai AI converts research instruments across all major Canadian university survey platforms.

Qualtrics - The Standard Research Platform

Qualtrics is the most widely used research survey platform across the U15 (Canada's 15 leading research universities, including University of Toronto, UBC, McGill, Alberta, and McMaster). Institutional licences provide SSO access and are typically restricted to academic research use. Most Canadian universities store Qualtrics data in US-based infrastructure - relevant under PIPEDA and provincial privacy laws for research involving personal information.

Best for: SSHRC-funded social science and humanities research. NSERC participant studies. Population health surveys without sensitive clinical data. Survey-heavy dissertation research at U15 institutions.

REDCap - Clinical and CIHR Research

REDCap is the standard for CIHR-funded clinical research and studies involving sensitive health information in Canada. Canadian hospital research institutes and academic health science centres operate REDCap instances - including the University Health Network (Toronto), McGill University Health Centre, BC Children's Hospital Research Institute, and many others. TCPS 2 Chapter 5 (Privacy and Confidentiality) requirements for health data are best satisfied by REDCap on institutional Canadian servers.

Best for: CIHR-funded clinical trials. Research involving health data under PHIPA (Ontario) or provincial equivalents. Studies where the REB specifies institutional data management. Canadian clinical trials under Health Canada oversight.

Microsoft Forms and Google Forms

Both are widely used in Canadian universities for teaching and low-risk surveys. PIPEDA requires that researchers disclose where personal information is stored - both platforms use US-based infrastructure by default. Under PIPEDA, US-hosted survey data may be accessible to US authorities under the USA PATRIOT Act, which must be disclosed to participants when collecting identifiable information. Some provinces (British Columbia, Quebec) have stricter provincial privacy laws that may additionally restrict US-hosted storage of personal information collected from provincial residents.

Indigenous Data Sovereignty - OCAP Principles

Research involving First Nations, Inuit, and Metis communities in Canada must consider Indigenous data sovereignty under the OCAP principles (Ownership, Control, Access, Possession), developed by the First Nations Information Governance Centre. Survey platforms used for Indigenous research should ideally support community-controlled data management - self-hosted options like REDCap or LimeSurvey on infrastructure governed by the community or partnering institution are more consistent with OCAP than US cloud platforms.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which survey platform do Canadian universities use for research?+

Most Canadian U15 universities provide Qualtrics for general academic research and REDCap for clinical or sensitive health data. Microsoft Forms and Google Forms are used for teaching and low-risk surveys. Platform choice is often determined by your REB protocol or funding body - CIHR-funded clinical research frequently uses REDCap.

Do Canadian researchers need REB approval for surveys?+

Yes. All research involving human participants at Canadian universities requires Research Ethics Board (REB) approval under TCPS 2 (Tri-Council Policy Statement) before data collection begins. Some minimal-risk research may qualify for delegated or expedited review, but REB approval is still required.

How does PIPEDA affect survey platform choice in Canada?+

PIPEDA (Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act) requires that participants be informed about how their personal information is collected, used, and disclosed - including cross-border transfers. Research using US-hosted platforms (Qualtrics, Google Forms) should disclose in the participant information sheet that data is stored in the US and may be accessible to US authorities. Quebec's Law 25 and BC's PIPA have additional requirements.
