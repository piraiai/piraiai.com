Pirai AI for TAFE NSW: Convert VET Assessments and Admin Forms in Minutes

Arun Raj•August 26, 2026•5 min read•Guide
For TAFE NSW Teachers and Assessors

TAFE NSW is Australia's largest provider of vocational education and training, serving more than 430,000 students annually across campuses in Sydney, Greater Western Sydney, Newcastle, Wollongong, and regional NSW. TAFE NSW teachers and assessors manage complex competency-based assessment documentation - knowledge questions, practical observation checklists, written response tasks, and student feedback surveys - most of which starts in Word or PDF and needs to be delivered digitally through Moodle, Google Forms, or Microsoft Forms. Pirai AI converts any VET assessment document to any of these platforms in minutes.

Moodle at TAFE NSW

Moodle is TAFE NSW's primary Learning Management System. Assessment activities, knowledge quizzes, reading and comprehension tasks, and student feedback surveys are delivered through Moodle course shells. Converting a knowledge assessment from a Word or PDF unit competency document to a Moodle quiz involves creating an AIKEN-format text file for import - a format most TAFE teachers are not familiar with and find time-consuming to produce manually.

Pirai AI generates valid AIKEN-format text files from any Word or PDF assessment document. Upload the competency unit knowledge questions from Word, select Moodle, download the AIKEN file, and import into your TAFE Moodle course via Quiz settings > Import. Questions appear as a Moodle quiz activity - marked automatically for knowledge questions with defined correct answers.

TAFE NSW Operations and Administration

Beyond assessment, TAFE NSW campuses generate significant administrative document volume:

  • Student enrolment and pre-enrolment forms: Course intake questionnaires, language literacy and numeracy (LLN) pre-assessment forms, and special needs support request forms converted from Word to Google Forms for online completion
  • Workplace learning placement forms: Student placement agreements, employer contact records, and workplace health and safety induction questionnaires converted to digital forms
  • Industry consultation surveys: Advisory committee surveys, industry feedback instruments for training package review, and employer satisfaction surveys converted from Word to Qualtrics or Google Forms
  • Staff professional development: Pre-PD needs analysis and post-PD evaluation surveys converted from Word to Google Forms or Microsoft Forms for TAFE NSW HR systems

TAFE NSW Sydney Campuses

TAFE NSW operates major Sydney campuses at Ultimo (adjacent to UTS), Meadowbank, Granville, Liverpool, Wetherill Park, Gymea, St George, and Northern Sydney Institute campuses at Chatswood, Hornsby, and Ryde. Each campus runs Moodle for course delivery and uses Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 for staff and student communication. Pirai AI works across all campus environments - converting the same assessment document to Moodle for LMS delivery and Google Forms or Microsoft Forms for supplementary digital collection.

Currency and Competency Document Conversion

TAFE NSW assessors maintain currency in their industry area - and VET training packages update regularly. When units of competency are superseded and new units are implemented, assessment tools need to be updated and rebuilt. Pirai AI converts revised competency unit assessment documents from Word or PDF to Moodle and Google Forms in minutes - significantly reducing the administrative burden of training package transitions on teaching faculty.
