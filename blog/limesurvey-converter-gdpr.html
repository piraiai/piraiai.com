Why European Researchers Choose LimeSurvey for GDPR

LimeSurvey GmbH is headquartered in Hamburg, Germany. Self-hosted on EU institutional servers, LimeSurvey satisfies GDPR compliance through:

  • No international data transfer: Participant data stays on EU servers - no cross-border transfer requiring Standard Contractual Clauses
  • Full institutional control: The institution is controller and processor - no Article 28 DPA with a third-party cloud vendor required
  • Right to erasure (Article 17): Built-in participant response deletion by token
  • Data portability (Article 20): CSV and SPSS exports for data portability
  • Accountability (Article 5(2)): Admin audit logs for administrator action tracking

German universities are the largest LimeSurvey adopters in Europe partly because the BDSG (Bundesdatenschutzgesetz) supplements GDPR with stricter requirements for certain data categories - self-hosted LimeSurvey satisfies both GDPR and BDSG without the complexity of international transfer mechanisms.

Pirai AI's GDPR-Compatible Conversion Process

Pirai AI converts your Word, PDF, or Excel document to LimeSurvey LSS format with the following data handling characteristics relevant to GDPR:

  • Client-side processing: Documents are processed in memory without persistent server storage
  • No document retention: Instrument documents are not stored after the LSS file is generated
  • No AI training: OpenAI API is configured with data sharing disabled
  • Data minimisation: Only the document content required for conversion is processed

For researchers concerned about GDPR Article 5(1)(c) data minimisation when processing draft or approved research instruments, this architecture ensures no third-party retention of the document.

GDPR-Compliant Survey Deployment Workflow

Step 1: Write your survey instrument in Word for ethics committee review and approval.

Step 2: Upload the approved Word document to Pirai AI. Select LimeSurvey and your DB version. Download the LSS file. Your document is not retained after this step.

Step 3: Import the LSS file into your institution's self-hosted LimeSurvey instance via Admin > + > Import. The survey is now on EU institutional infrastructure with full GDPR compliance.

Step 4: Configure participant management in LimeSurvey - anonymous surveys (no tokens) or pseudonymised (token-based). Configure cookie consent banner if required. Activate the survey.

Step 5: All participant data is collected on your EU LimeSurvey instance. No data leaves EU infrastructure.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Pirai AI GDPR compliant for European researchers converting documents to LimeSurvey?+

Pirai AI processes documents client-side without persistent server storage. Instrument documents are not retained after conversion. The OpenAI API is configured with data sharing disabled. This client-side, no-retention architecture aligns with GDPR data minimisation principles (Article 5(1)(c)).

Why do German universities prefer LimeSurvey for GDPR compliance?+

German universities operate under both GDPR and the BDSG (Bundesdatenschutzgesetz), which supplements GDPR with stricter provisions for certain sensitive data categories. LimeSurvey self-hosted on German institutional servers involves no international data transfer and provides full institutional control - satisfying both GDPR and BDSG without the complexity of Standard Contractual Clauses required for US cloud platforms.

How does converting a document to LimeSurvey support GDPR compliance?+

The conversion itself (Pirai AI generating an LSS file) does not involve processing participant data. It converts your survey instrument document to LimeSurvey format. Once imported into your self-hosted EU LimeSurvey, participant data collected stays on EU servers with no international transfer - the strongest GDPR compliance position for survey data.

What LimeSurvey DB version should European researchers use?+

Most current LimeSurvey installations in European universities run version 4.x or 5.x - use DB version 6 in Pirai AI. If your institution runs LimeSurvey 3.x (older installations), use DB version 5. Check Help > About LimeSurvey in your admin panel to confirm.
