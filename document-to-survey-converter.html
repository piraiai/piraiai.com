Security: How Your Documents Are Protected

Documents uploaded to Pirai AI are processed entirely in-memory and destroyed after conversion. No file is written to a server or retained. OpenAI API training controls are explicitly disabled - your survey content is never used for model training. For Google Forms output, Pirai AI generates a Google Apps Script that runs inside your own Google account with no Drive access granted to any third party. Read the full security guide.
