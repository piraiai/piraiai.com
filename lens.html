The Problem

Digitizing paper responses shouldn't mean weeks of manual data entry.

Typing up hundreds of scanned forms by hand is slow and error-prone. Generic OCR tools don't know your survey's questions or options, so they guess instead of matching. Every new batch means starting over.

WITHOUT LENS

Staff manually retype every checkbox and handwritten answer from scanned forms. Mistakes creep in with every hour of data entry. Every question type, matrix grids, rank orders, open text, needs its own workaround. There's no way to reuse the work across batches of the same survey.

WITH LENS

Lens reads each scanned or marked response against your survey's own schema. Once a survey's layout is mapped, every batch of that survey reuses it. Low-confidence answers are flagged automatically instead of silently guessed. Fix one question or one response without redoing the rest.

How It Works

From scanned PDF to structured data in four steps.

The layout only needs mapping once per survey. Every batch after that goes straight to extraction.

1

Bring your survey

Upload your Pirai AI base-format schema, or a raw questionnaire document and Lens derives one. This becomes the answer key every response is checked against.

2

Map the layout, once

Upload one blank or sample copy of the survey. Lens reads every page and proposes which questions live where. Review and confirm before processing real responses.

3

Scan and upload responses

Drop in scanned or marked PDFs in bulk. Lens reads checkboxes, ratings, and handwriting, verified against the schema, not guessed.

4

Review, reprocess, export

Low-confidence or blank answers are flagged for review. Edit any answer directly, reprocess a single question, or reprocess a whole response, then export to CSV or Excel.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is Pirai AI Lens?

Pirai AI Lens is Pirai AI's paper survey digitization tool. It reads scanned or marked paper survey response PDFs and extracts every answer, checkboxes, ratings, matrix grids, rank orders, and handwritten text, matched against your survey's own schema.

How does Lens read handwriting and marked checkboxes?

Lens uses AI vision models to look at each scanned page and report what's visible, checked against your survey's own known options and items rather than guessed freely. Answers Lens isn't confident about are flagged for human review instead of being silently recorded.

Do I need to rebuild my survey in a new platform?

No. Lens works from the same schema Pirai AI already derives for 19+ survey platforms, so you bring the survey you already built or already ran, and Lens digitizes the paper responses against it.

What happens to answers Lens isn't confident about?

Low-confidence and blank answers are flagged automatically in the review screen, so you can check exactly the answers that need a second look instead of reviewing every response from scratch.

Can I fix just one question instead of reprocessing a whole response?

Yes. Lens can reprocess a single flagged question in isolation, or reprocess a whole response, without disturbing anything else already extracted correctly.

Can I edit an extracted answer myself instead of reprocessing it?

Yes. Every extracted answer is directly editable in the review screen. You don't have to rely on AI reprocessing, a human always has the final say before anything is exported.

Is my scanned response data used to train AI models?

No. Response images are processed only to extract your survey data. Nothing you upload is used to train AI models. Learn more →

What question types does Lens support?

Single answer, multiple answer, dropdown, matrix, open text, form fields, rank order, and constant sum, eight question types in total.

What file formats does Lens accept?

Lens accepts scanned or marked response PDFs, and exports results as CSV or Excel, formatted with values or labels and headers matched to your survey platform's convention.

What languages can Lens read?

Lens reads handwriting and marks in the language your respondents actually used, it isn't limited to English-language surveys.

How much does Pirai AI Lens cost?

Lens is priced at $9.99 per 1,000 pages processed. Email us to get started →
