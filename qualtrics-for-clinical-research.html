Qualtrics in Healthcare

Qualtrics Healthcare Edition is widely used for patient satisfaction programmes (HCAHPS, CG-CAHPS), staff wellbeing surveys, and patient-reported outcome programmes that sit outside regulated clinical trial infrastructure. For regulated clinical trials requiring IRB audit trails and 21 CFR Part 11 compliance, REDCap is more appropriate. Pirai AI converts clinical survey documents to either platform.

Qualtrics HIPAA Configuration

Qualtrics Healthcare Edition operates on HIPAA-compliant cloud infrastructure with BAA (Business Associate Agreement) support. For healthcare organisations collecting PHI through Qualtrics surveys, the following configuration is required: verify your Qualtrics account is on the HIPAA-compliant environment (not the standard shared cloud), ensure a BAA is signed with Qualtrics before collecting PHI, enable response anonymisation for surveys where PHI linkage is not required, and restrict dashboard access to authorised personnel only via role-based permissions.

Patient Satisfaction Surveys in Qualtrics

Qualtrics is widely used for patient satisfaction programmes including HCAHPS (Hospital Consumer Assessment of Healthcare Providers and Systems), CG-CAHPS (Clinician and Group Consumer Assessment), ED-CAHPS (Emergency Department), and HHCAHPS (Home Health). The HCAHPS frequency scale (Never / Sometimes / Usually / Always) is configured as a Matrix/Rating Scale with custom scale labels. The HCAHPS 0-10 overall rating question uses a Linear Scale question type.

Pirai AI converts HCAHPS and other patient satisfaction instrument documents to Qualtrics QSF for direct import - preserving the exact scale labels required for CMS benchmark reporting. The patient satisfaction survey questions guide covers HCAHPS-specific design requirements.

Patient-Reported Outcomes in Qualtrics

For patient-reported outcome programmes that sit outside regulated clinical trial infrastructure - real-world evidence studies, patient registry surveys, post-market surveillance, and patient advocacy surveys - Qualtrics is appropriate. Configure composite score calculation using Qualtrics' Scoring feature to calculate PHQ-9, GAD-7, or custom PRO total scores automatically. Use Qualtrics Workflows to trigger alerts when threshold scores are exceeded and to route responses to care coordination teams.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Qualtrics HIPAA compliant?+

Qualtrics Healthcare Edition is HIPAA compliant and supports Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) for US healthcare organisations. Verify your account is on the HIPAA-compliant environment and that a BAA is in place before collecting Protected Health Information through Qualtrics surveys.

Should I use Qualtrics or REDCap for clinical research?+

Use Qualtrics for patient satisfaction programmes (HCAHPS), staff surveys, and care experience measurement where real-time dashboards and EHR-triggered distribution are priorities. Use REDCap for regulated clinical trials requiring ICH GCP audit trails, 21 CFR Part 11 compliance, and institutional data sovereignty. Many academic medical centres use both.
