LimeSurvey for Research

LimeSurvey is a leading open-source survey platform used by hundreds of universities, government research bodies, and NGOs globally. Its self-hosted architecture keeps all data on institution-controlled servers, its branching logic rivals commercial platforms, and its multilingual support covers international research programmes. Pirai AI converts research instrument documents to LimeSurvey LSS format in minutes.

Why Researchers Choose LimeSurvey

Complete data sovereignty. Self-hosted LimeSurvey keeps all participant data on your institution's own infrastructure. No research data passes through commercial cloud providers. For studies involving sensitive populations, health data, or participants in jurisdictions with strict data protection laws (GDPR in Europe, PDPA in Australia, POPIA in South Africa), institutional server hosting is often an IRB requirement. LimeSurvey's self-hosted Community Edition fulfils this without subscription cost.

Free for self-hosting institutions. LimeSurvey Community Edition is free and open source under the GPL licence. Universities, government bodies, and NGOs already running their own web servers can deploy LimeSurvey at zero software cost. The only costs are hosting and IT support - typically already covered by existing infrastructure.

Research-grade branching and conditions. LimeSurvey's conditions system and expression manager support complex skip patterns: show a question based on a prior answer, hide a block based on a respondent attribute, calculate a score mid-survey and branch based on the result. The expression manager syntax is more powerful than Google Forms' section-based routing and comparable to mid-tier commercial platforms.

Multilingual survey support. LimeSurvey supports surveys in multiple languages from a single survey structure, with right-to-left text rendering for Arabic and Hebrew. For international research programmes requiring the same instrument in English, French, German, and Arabic, LimeSurvey's built-in translation workflow is significantly more efficient than maintaining separate surveys per language.

Converting Research Instruments to LimeSurvey

Research instruments published as Word documents or PDFs must be manually recreated in LimeSurvey's question editor or imported via LSS file. Building the LSS manually requires familiarity with LimeSurvey's XML structure - not practical for most researchers without technical support. Pirai AI generates the LSS file automatically from the instrument document.

Upload your validated instrument PDF or Word document to platform.piraiai.com. Select LimeSurvey as the target. Choose the correct DB version for your LimeSurvey installation. Download the .lss file. Import via Admin > Survey List > Import Survey. All question groups, questions, and answer options from the instrument are created in LimeSurvey instantly with exact item wording preserved.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is LimeSurvey free for universities?+

Yes. LimeSurvey Community Edition is free and open source. Universities running their own web server infrastructure can deploy LimeSurvey at no software cost. Most European universities, many Australian universities, and a significant number of US institutions use self-hosted LimeSurvey for research data collection.

Is LimeSurvey suitable for IRB-approved research?+

LimeSurvey on institution-controlled servers is appropriate for many IRB-approved studies because it keeps participant data within institutional infrastructure. Whether it satisfies specific IRB requirements depends on your protocol - particularly around audit trails, consent procedures, and data encryption at rest. Consult your IRB application and institutional review board for your specific study.

How do I convert a validated survey instrument to LimeSurvey?+

Upload the instrument document (Word or PDF) to Pirai AI, select LimeSurvey, choose the correct DB version for your installation, and download the .lss file. Import it into LimeSurvey via Admin > Survey List > Import Survey. The exact item wording and scale labels from the validated instrument are preserved in the imported question groups and questions.

Can I convert a LimeSurvey survey to Qualtrics with Pirai AI?+

Yes. Export your LimeSurvey survey as an LSS file. Upload it to Pirai AI and select Qualtrics as the target. The LSS structure is read and converted to a Qualtrics QSF file for import. This is useful when a study moves from a self-hosted LimeSurvey environment to an institutional Qualtrics licence.
