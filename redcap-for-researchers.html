REDCap for Research

REDCap is used by over 6,500 institutions globally and is the standard electronic data capture platform for academic research involving sensitive data, longitudinal measurement, or regulated clinical protocols. Most universities provide free access through their institutional REDCap instance. Pirai AI converts validated instrument documents to REDCap data dictionary format in minutes, preserving exact item wording.

Why Researchers Choose REDCap

Institution-controlled data sovereignty. REDCap runs on your institution's own servers (or cloud infrastructure under your institution's control). Research data never leaves the institutional environment - a critical requirement for studies involving health information, personally identifiable data, or populations protected under IRB protocols.

Full audit trail. Every data entry, modification, and access event in REDCap is logged with timestamp and user ID. For studies requiring ICH GCP compliance or that may face regulatory scrutiny, this audit trail satisfies data integrity requirements that Google Forms and Qualtrics standard editions do not provide.

Validated instrument support. REDCap's data dictionary format can encode the complete structure of validated instruments - exact item wording, scale labels, branching logic conditions, and calculated composite scores. Once built, the instrument can be exported and shared with other REDCap institutions maintaining identical fidelity.

Longitudinal data structures. REDCap's Events and Instruments architecture supports multi-timepoint data collection under a single participant record. Baseline, 3-month, 6-month, and 12-month assessments all link to the same participant, enabling longitudinal analysis without data merging in external tools.

Free institutional access. REDCap is free for consortium member institutions. If your university, hospital, or research centre has a REDCap licence - and most do - you have access at no cost. Contact your research informatics or IT office to access your institution's instance.

Converting Validated Instruments to REDCap

The most time-consuming part of deploying a validated research instrument in REDCap is building the data dictionary manually. A 40-item PHQ-SADS, SF-36, PROMIS measure, or custom battery requires defining every field in REDCap's Online Designer - selecting the field type, typing the exact item wording, entering each answer choice in pipe-delimited format, setting branching logic conditions. One transcription error changes item wording and invalidates the instrument for publication.

Pirai AI converts the instrument document to a REDCap data dictionary CSV in minutes. Upload the PDF or Word version of the instrument, select REDCap, and download the data dictionary. Import it into your REDCap project via Project Setup > Data Dictionary > Upload File. The exact item wording from the source document is preserved verbatim in every field label.

IRB and Ethics Considerations

REDCap's audit trail, field-level access controls, and institution-controlled infrastructure make it a strong choice for IRB-approved studies requiring data integrity assurance. For studies involving HIPAA-protected health information, your institution's REDCap instance typically operates under the BAA established with your IT department. For multi-site studies, REDCap's user permission system allows site-level data segregation while maintaining a single project structure.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is REDCap free for academic researchers?+

REDCap is free for institutions that are members of the REDCap Consortium - which includes most universities and academic medical centres globally. Researchers access REDCap through their institution's instance at no personal cost. Contact your research informatics office, library, or IT department to find your institution's REDCap instance and request access.

Can I use REDCap for research with human subjects?+

Yes. REDCap is specifically designed for research involving human subjects. Its institution-controlled data storage, field-level audit trail, user access controls, and validated instrument support are well-suited to IRB-regulated research. The appropriateness for your specific study depends on your IRB protocol and your institution's data governance policies.

How do I get a validated research instrument into REDCap quickly?+

Upload the instrument document (Word or PDF) to Pirai AI at platform.piraiai.com, select REDCap as the target, and download the data dictionary CSV. Import it into your REDCap project via Project Setup > Data Dictionary > Upload File > Commit Changes. A 40-item validated scale goes from document to REDCap in under 15 minutes with exact item wording preserved.

Can REDCap handle longitudinal research designs?+

Yes. REDCap's Events and Instruments architecture supports multi-timepoint data collection under a single participant record. Define events (Baseline, Month 3, Month 6) and assign instruments to each event. All timepoints link to the same participant record for longitudinal analysis without external data merging.
